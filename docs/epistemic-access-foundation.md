# Epistemic Access / Sealed Accessible Content Packet Backbone Foundation

Tenth bounded slice, built on the Campaign Continuity Foundation (`docs/campaign-package.md`), the Canonical World Identity & Owner Foundation (`docs/canonical-world-foundation.md`), the Turnless Temporal & Resolution Transaction Foundation (`docs/temporal-foundation.md`, including its durable pre-stage transaction authority) and the Named-Actor Character Capability & Capability-Close Backbone Foundation (`docs/actor-capability-foundation.md`). One **committed, owner-provided source/carrier context** can now supply **one causally real payload to one explicit, bounded, named non-PC recipient** through the **lawful Epistemic Access owner (`epistemic_access` / `epistemic_access`)**: the owner establishes **actual receipt** as one immutable **BOUNDED_SHARED_ACCESS** event; where the payload's representational form depends on a Named-Actor capability it reads **only the already-committed capability at its declared capability close**; and it **seals engine-only provenance separately from the actor-safe Accessible Content Packet**, which is then available as a lawful downstream prerequisite. It is a foundation for later Actor/KTI consequences; it is not their implementation. The packet **stops before** recognition, comprehension, inference, credibility, belief, stance, decision or behaviour.

Governing lock: `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` (F-027 v1.0). Source: F-014D Epistemic Access — Working Operational Draft v2 (Sovereign-ratified; the W1-PS2 Sealed-Engine-Provenance / Accessible-Content-Packet split ratified 2026-09-25; lock pin `2026-09-24T16:25:17.059Z`) §§2, 2.1, 4.1, 4.2, 5 steps 2–4 and 6, 9, 11, 14, 18, 21, 24; RC-11 (SealedContext / AccessEvent) and RC-14 (Projection guard); WS-016 §27.1 and F-017 v0.3 §3 (CLOSE) for the capability read, as already implemented by IM-02 and the ninth slice. **CAL-01 (APPROXIMATE-PARTITION) remains UNCALIBRATED and is not used: every proof is exact. CAL-03 stays unadopted: the participant set here is _exactly one_ recipient by construction, which is a scope restriction and not a participant bound. No score, number, threshold, grade conversion, roll, prior, seed or default exists anywhere.** Every other F-027 open gate is unchanged.

This is a **foundation proof**, not an information system. All fixture data is synthetic (`packages/testkit/src/epistemic-fixtures.ts`): a test **access source** commits the carrier/context facts an event may pin (a stand-in for the foreign owners — a speaker, a document, a scene — that are not implemented), and a test **packet consumer** is the dependent consequence that materially requires a recipient's packet and records exactly the packet it read. Both are registered through the existing extension seam (which cannot write a canonical, kernel, Population, Production, Economy, World-State, Logistics, Named-Actor or Epistemic key space); neither owns anything Epistemic. The Epistemic Access owner is real and lives in `@immortalis/engine`.

## One canon: how it reuses IM-02 / IM-03 / IM-11 / IM-12 / IM-17

Nothing new is persisted outside the existing campaign package. All three Epistemic kinds are ordinary `OwnerFact`s written through `store.commit`, so the existing validation, expected-version checks, RC-16 receipts, idempotent replay, unknown-outcome reconciliation, hash-chained journal and package/load integrity are the ones in force. **The package format is unchanged (still format version 1); there is no second journal, scheduler, roster, sidecar save path, polling loop or per-actor/per-event refresh.** The temporal coordinator and the Named-Actor modules were not changed (conformance tests assert neither names the Epistemic owner). The only edits to predecessor engine code are additive wiring: the registry gains the three kinds, `identityMismatch` handles them, `canonicalAdmission` dispatches the Epistemic owner, object registration also asks the Epistemic rule, and `CanonicalCampaign.open`/`verify` also run the Epistemic verifier.

## The lifecycle: event → formation → consumption

1. **Event (authored).** `establishBoundedSharedAccess` publishes the sealed event. Admission requires the source to be a committed foreign fact, owned by the owner it names, **pinned at its current version** (read and depended on, never copied), not the Epistemic owner's own state, kernel state or a Named-Actor capability; the recipient to be a lawful Named-Actor subject (below); and the access time to be at or before the canonical basis (no future access). The event is the Epistemic owner's actual-receipt determination at its basis: it is engine-side, stamped `epistemic.engine-sealed`, and is **not** the packet and not an actor-side read.
2. **Material trigger = the consequence's object.** A dependent consequence registers its temporal object with the formation stage declared ahead of its own stage (and, where the payload's form depends on a capability, the Named-Actor owner's close stage ahead of that). Registration is refused unless the event is **already committed** — a consequence cannot create the access it needs. There is no standalone engagement, tick or sweep: with no consequence engaged, no packet is ever formed, however many events exist.
3. **Formation (IM-02 stage).** The coordinator resolves the declared stage after the capability close: it reads exactly the event (NOW) and, if required, the capability **AS-OF-DECLARED-CLOSE** (so it observes the committed close itself, not whatever came after it) and writes the packet (`epistemic.actor-accessible`) and the Sealed Engine Provenance (`epistemic.engine-sealed`) **together, once**, under its StageKey-derived group/intent identity and the transaction's durable authority. The StageKey is `(epistemic-access/<event>/<recipient>, epistemic_access / epistemic-form-accessible-packet, <packet key>)`: no horizon, source or capability version, retry, consumer or slice is an identity dimension.
4. **Consumption.** The consequence's stage reads the packet **AS-OF-NOW after formation** (F-014D §2.1) and cannot resolve before it. A later consequence of the same event/recipient declares the same stage in its own object and **adopts** the committed formation by identity — never duplicated, recomputed or improved. Two consequences due at the same instant share one formation: one declares the stage, the other reads the packet.

## Receipt, intelligibility and comprehension are separate

- **Receipt** is the event: a route (committed carrier, direct channel, recipient) established at a basis time. It is not intelligibility and not comprehension.
- **Representational intelligibility** is a separate determination in the packet: either the payload's form depends on no Named-Actor capability, or the recipient's own **already-committed** capability for the required faculty exists at the Named-Actor owner's declared close, and the packet **references** it. It is "available in a form the recipient can access" — nothing more.
- **Comprehension** does not exist here. The packet has no field for it, and no module names it.

## Capability: read at its close, never written, never improved

The Epistemic owner reads a Named-Actor capability only through the existing `namedActorCapabilityCloseRead` (AS-OF-DECLARED-CLOSE, Close Basis = the actor-profile capability close). It writes no capability and declares no capability stage of its own. A capability is sealed (the ninth slice), so a same-resolution or later capability change cannot reach an already-sealed packet: nothing can change a committed capability, and a sealed packet cannot be re-formed. A **later** lawful packet — another event — consumes its **own** later close and references it; the earlier packet is byte-identical (tested).

## PC authorship and aggregate identity

By identity, with no scan (the Named-Actor `actorBoundaryViolation`, reused), at the event and again at the formation (so an entity that comes under player control between the two gets no packet and its formation fails closed, while independent valid work in the same instant still resolves), and re-checked on load by receipt order: an identity with an I-002 Existence Contract, the entity the Role Registry binding names as the active PC, and a Population ledger identity are never **Named-Actor** recipients (the thirty-fourth slice adds the one explicit active-PC recipient path below; this boundary is unchanged for every event that does not assert it). The Epistemic owner provides no PC cognition, no PC knowledge adjudication and no Player Referential Authority.

## Active-PC recipient and the PC Capability Close (thirty-fourth bounded slice)

The one recipient of a BOUNDED_SHARED_ACCESS event may also be the **canonically bound active Player Character**. Nothing else about the path changes: one event, one sealed packet and one sealed provenance, one IM-02 formation stage, the same StageKey, sealing and replay behaviour. Supporting seam authority (not a new lock pin): I-002 (`2026-09-25T00:15:54.536Z`), WS-016 (`2026-09-24T17:38:59.210Z`), F-016 Phase 2B PCC-01–03 (`2026-09-21T23:18:36.650Z`) and the Canonical Role Registry v1 (`2026-09-25T08:21:22.816Z`); the governing rule citation of the Epistemic kinds is deliberately unchanged, so the unchanged Named-Actor path and every existing save keep their committed provenance byte-for-byte.

- **Classification is the Role Registry binding, by identity.** The event carries one optional literal, `recipient_role: "role.player_character"` (the Role Registry slot name). It is an _assertion_, never state: admission, registration, formation and load verification (by receipt order) each check it against the committed `role-registry/binding/role.player_character` fact (`recipientBoundaryViolation`). An I-002 Existence Contract alone, an unbound or other identity, an absent binding, a binding not owned by the Role Registry and a Population ledger identity are refused. Without the assertion the existing Named-Actor boundary (`actorBoundaryViolation`) applies **unchanged and unrelaxed** — the bound PC is still refused — so an event established for a Named-Actor identity is never reinterpreted if that identity is bound later (its formation fails closed; no cross-handover reconstruction).
- **The capability is the PC owner's, read at the PC Capability Close.** `requiredCapability` is the one statement of the read: a PC event requires `pc/<pc>/capability/<capability>` (owner `pc_existence`), declared through the existing `pcCapabilityCloseRead` builder (AS-OF-DECLARED-CLOSE, Close Basis `PC Capability Close`, F-016 PCC-03); a Named-Actor event requires the Named-Actor owner's capability exactly as before. There is **no fallback in either direction**. The consequence's object declares the PC owner's close stage ahead of the formation (the existing `pcCapabilityDeclaration`, only for a committed basis), as the Named-Actor path does; a committed close is adopted by identity.
- **The packet references; it never interprets.** For a PC the packet's `intelligibility` is `ESTABLISHED_CAPABILITY` with the exact committed fact key, version, and close `{ name: "PC Capability Close", group_id }`; the sealed provenance records the same fact, version and committing group. The marker is never read, compared, thresholded or converted; F-014D writes no capability and invokes no Causal Draw. The close name is the only widening of the packet contract (two literals) and is re-derived, never chosen by the packet.
- **Fail closed, writes nothing.** A PC capability not committed at its close leaves the formation unresolved (`PREDECESSOR_ABSENT`, as for a Named-Actor capability: absence is no negative fact). A wrong owner, key, holder, capability, close name, close stage or close group (or a Named-Actor fact in place of the PC's) is refused by the single derivation, at the owner operation, at IM-11 admission (including a raw `store.commit`) and on load. A PC event may not rest on the PC owner's capability key spaces as its information source (a PC capability is visible cross-owner only at its close).

Not implemented (fail closed / unrepresentable): PC cognition, comprehension, belief or presentation of the packet; broadcast/cohort access; presence, range and latency; retrieval/memory and Past-Basis reconstruction; KTI for a PC recipient (the KTI boundary is unchanged and still refuses it); I-001/Narrative; PRA; capability development or loss; cross-handover reconstruction; a PC with several recipients. Evidence: `tests/contract/epistemic-access-schemas.test.ts`, `tests/unit/epistemic-pc-recipient.test.ts`, `tests/integration/epistemic-pc-recipient.test.ts`, `tests/conformance/epistemic-access-conformance.test.ts`; the predecessor PC-capability conformance guard that forbade any Epistemic wiring now exempts exactly this bounded read. Register rows `IA-11`, `IA-14` and `IA-16` gained scope text and tests as partial evidence only; no status or gate changed.

## Judgment calls and open residuals (none resolves an open gate)

- **Intelligibility is the committed capability's existence at its close; the marker is not consulted.** No source in the lock defines how a qualitative grade (C/B/A/S, EX, ungraded) bears on whether a payload is _accessible_, and the engine must not invent a threshold. The packet therefore references the capability and states only that the representation is accessible. A recipient whose required capability is **not committed at the declared close** cannot be answered "not intelligible" either (the capability record is persisted only when consequential, so absence is not a negative fact): the formation stays **unresolved** (`PREDECESSOR_ABSENT`), publishes nothing and invents nothing, and its dependents wait. A negative, marker-sensitive or partial-intelligibility result needs a source rule and is **not implemented** (a candidate Implementation Escalation for the next Epistemic slice, not needed by this one).
- **Receipt is an Epistemic-owned authored determination, verified structurally.** The engine enforces that the route rests on a committed, pinned, foreign, earlier source/carrier, that the recipient is a lawful non-PC subject, that the access time is not in the future, and that nothing else (objective truth, host/GM/tool visibility, authority, group membership, an uncommitted source, the recipient's own capability) can stand in for it. It does **not** verify co-presence or sensory range of a named actor with the carrier: no source in the lock supplies a presence/perception owner for a Named Actor, and none was invented. The authored payload and presented attribution are likewise not checked against the carrier's own value (the carrier's value schema belongs to its owner); only a payload that carries the engine reference (key) of its true source is refused.
- **Only the direct channels `PERCEPTION` and `SPEECH` are representable.** Message, record, memory, monitoring and broadcast channels need travel/latency, locatability, reconstruction or audience semantics that are out of scope; they are unrepresentable, not approximated.
- **Exactly one recipient.** A bounded multi-recipient set is not modelled; because the participant list is a one-element array by construction, no explicit-participant bound (CAL-03) is adopted or implied.
- **Sealed Engine Provenance has no declared reader yet.** Audit, Temporal (Knowledge Time), KTI and GM/world resolution are not implemented; the provenance is available only through the owner's typed engine-side read (`engineSealedProvenance`), and the registration rule refuses every declared read of it, so it can never become an actor-side input by declaration. The same rule refuses a consequence's read of the engine-side event: an actor-side consequence reads the formed packet only.
- **No Named-Actor existence record yet** (as in the ninth slice): a recipient is an opaque identity that is not a PC, not under player control and not a Population ledger.
- **An object may register before its capability close is declared** (parity with the ninth slice): it is then unresolved at its boundary, fail closed, rather than refused at registration.
- **Contradictions / conflicting content (F-014D §11)** are not modelled: the packet has no contradiction field. Presented Source Attribution may differ from the truth and stays presented-only.
- **Implementation Escalation — GitHub Issue #11 stays open and unimplemented.** Genuinely uncertain Named-Actor capability establishment needs a lawful uncertainty mechanism this engine does not have. Nothing here uses, needs or approximates one: the proof uses only capability established through the supported causally determined branch, and no RNG, seed, roll table, probability, default grade, chooser or randomization owner exists in this slice (a conformance test asserts it).
- **Cost.** The advance path reads the engaged event, its object, the capability close and the consumer by identity and writes only that event/recipient's keys. `verifyEpistemicIntegrity` (like the other verifiers) lists all facts once per open — a diagnostic path (CAL-06), not the advance path.

`BROADCAST_EXPOSURE` (fails closed; never approximated by recipient copying), cohort/event-basis membership, message travel and latency, courier/logistics carriage for information, durable-record search and locatability, memory reconstruction, monitoring and live feeds, telephone-game relay, deception generation, Past-Basis late materialization, contradictions, aggregate/cultural exposure, KTI learning, transfer packages, competence and diffusion, Player Referential Authority, recognition / comprehension / inference / misreading, credibility weighting, belief or stance, goals or motives, relationships, mandates and offices beyond existing prerequisites, autonomous decisions or behaviour, PC cognition (the active-PC recipient and its PC Capability Close read are the thirty-fourth slice, below), Temporal Knowledge Time / Actionable Knowledge Time, Narrative/GM presentation, projection-before-NPK, host/UI/protocol exposure, provider adoption, deployment, managed-database recovery, migration, PF-6 / F-028.
