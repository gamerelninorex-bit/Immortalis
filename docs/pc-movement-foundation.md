# Active-PC Voluntary Movement Commitment → Single-Leg Physical Relocation Foundation

Fortieth bounded slice, built on the Canonical World Identity & Owner Foundation (`docs/canonical-world-foundation.md`), the Single-Leg Logistics Transfer & Destination Handoff Backbone (`docs/logistics-foundation.md`), the World-State Entity Continuity / Transition Close foundation (`docs/entity-continuity-foundation.md`), the Existing-Actor → PC handover (`docs/existing-actor-handover-foundation.md`) and the Active-PC Post-Handover Physical Locus Custody (`docs/pc-locus-custody-foundation.md`, the thirty-ninth slice). One real active Player Character can now **lawfully commit one voluntary relocation, become lawfully in transfer under Logistics, and reach one World-State destination truth**, without any owner inventing willingness, route, duration or success, and without a rival current-locus authority at any durable cut:

player commitment → PC-owned Movement Service Request → World-State stationary custody handoff → Logistics single-leg transfer → explicit lawful delivery boundary → World-State destination commit → Logistics retirement.

This is **one journey for one PC**, not a movement system. Governing lock: `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` (F-027 v1.0). No source pin, Runtime Contract snapshot, open gate or acceptance status changed. PF-6/F-028 remains BLOCKED / NOT ACTIVATED.

## Authority

That contract is **not a spec-lock pin**: it is cited in the governing rule's lineage, never added to the lock. The clauses this slice rests on:

- **§4** voluntary movement intent originates only with `role.player` + `permission.player_choice.commit`; the Role Registry binding is control, not a physical-position writer; Logistics is the sole current locus from dispatch until the physical owner accepts; the destination commit is the same physical custodian's ECT-01-compatible commit.
- **§5 / §6** the binding's `coupling.world` / `coupling.locus` is a binding-time referent and the consumed `world_actor_coupling` is immutable provenance: neither is ever the PC's current locus. Ordinary travel invokes no Role Registry change (**§12**).
- **§8** the exact transition: precondition → dispatch (the custodian yields, retaining no competing stationary value) → transfer (Logistics alone) → delivery cause → destination commit by the same custodian → retirement only after the destination commit.
- **§9** exactly one authority answers "where is it now?".
- **§10 / §11** request ownership and physical-subject ownership stay separate: the request is PC/player-side, the moved subject is admitted from the lawful physical custodian through a narrow owner-specific adapter, and Logistics never manufactures a fallback PC entity. ECT-01 stays the destination principle and never becomes a PC identity owner; its one-transition limitation is kept (no second journey).
- **§14** fail-closed conditions.

## One authority at every durable cut

The sealed PR #41 custody record is immutable **predecessor provenance**: the origin of the movement, never rewritten. The destination is World-State's own ECT-01 transition for the same continuing PC reference (`world/<w>/entity/<pc>/transition`, the existing `world_entity_transition` kind). Where is the PC? One rule, three reads by identity and no scan (`resolveEntityLocus`, now over the physical subject):

| Durable state                                                          | Phase                                      | Authority                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| custody only; commitment, request or Logistics commitment, no dispatch | `STATIONARY_AT_ORIGIN`                     | World-State's stationary custody record                                                                   |
| transfer `IN_TRANSFER`, with or without Logistics' delivery result     | `IN_TRANSFER`                              | **Logistics' movement locus**; the PC is nowhere stationary. Logistics' arrival is not destination truth. |
| World-State's transition committed, transfer not yet retired           | `DESTINATION_COMMITTED_RETIREMENT_PENDING` | World-State's destination; Logistics' movement locus has lapsed with the destination commit (no rival)    |
| transition committed, transfer `RETIRED`                               | `DESTINATION_COMMITTED`                    | World-State's destination                                                                                 |

The yield at dispatch is atomic with the dispatch commit, so there is never an interval with two authorities, and the destination commit is a dependent of Logistics' delivery while the retirement is a dependent of the destination commit. The Role Registry binding and the consumed actor coupling are read by nothing as a current locus: a handed-over PC with no custody record has **no** current-locus answer, and after the journey the binding still says "origin" while the physical owner says "destination", harmlessly.

## Reuse of the Logistics and ECT-01 seams: narrow, fail-closed additions

1. **The moved subject** (Logistics `deriveCommitment`, admission, adapter, integrity; ECT-01 `deriveEntityTransition`, its admission, provider and verifier): the subject may be a fallback entity (unchanged) or the PC's custody record. For the PC, the **only** admissible request is its own PC-owned request at its own slot, and a PC-owned request moves only its own PC (request ownership and subject ownership stay separate). A Logistics commitment for the PC additionally re-judges the whole PC-side chain (the commitment-of-record and the request, with every pin still current) and refuses if a fallback entity exists under the PC's identity.
2. **The PC changes locus only through its own Logistics delivery-complete result.** `deriveEntityTransition` refuses any other causing result for a PC subject, however well formed, so there is no generic PC relocation path.
3. **No rival subject.** A conserved reference that is an active PC's identity is not accepted as a Production fallback entity (the reverse of PR #41's guard), so the PC never has two physical-subject records.

## Behavior, end to end

1. The existing machinery has completed the handover and PR #41's `establishPcPhysicalLocusCustody` has committed the custody record. Unchanged.
2. `commitPcVoluntaryMovement` commits the **commitment-of-record** (willingness only). Nothing moves; the PC is still stationary at the origin.
3. `establishPcMovementRequest` commits the **PC-owned request** derived from it. Still nothing moves.
4. `LogisticsAdapter.commitMovement` (the explicit, externally declared `delivery_at`) commits the single-leg Logistics commitment pinning the custody record as the moved subject. `dispatch` commits the transfer: Logistics is the sole current locus.
5. The transfer's one temporal object is registered; at the declared boundary the coordinator resolves delivery → World-State's destination commit → Logistics' retirement, in that order, in one transaction.
6. Everything survives save → close → load with convergence on the uninterrupted run from every durable cut (including crashes between the delivery result, the destination commit and the retirement); a lost reply converges by identity (RC-16).

## Construction decisions that are technical encodings, not new semantics

- **Facet name.** `pc_voluntary_commitment` (the sources prescribe no serialization vocabulary); it is a third facet of the one existing PC owner, never a second PC owner and never a "movement" owner.
- **One slot per PC, sealed.** The commitment and the request are create-only at `pc/<pc>/movement/…`; with Logistics' and ECT-01's per-entity slots this is the one-journey constraint. Request and commitment identities are derived from the PC identity (`pc-move-commitment:<pc>`, `pc-move:<pc>`), so the existing Logistics identities derive from it without a clock, counter or random value.
- **Yield by resolver.** Dispatch yields the stationary authority exactly as it does for a fallback entity: by the one resolver, atomically with the transfer commit. No relinquishment fact, successor record or location ledger is invented; the sealed origin record is the transition's `from`.
- **`SAME_ENTITY_CONTINUES` / `QUANTITY_AND_CONDITION_UNCHANGED`.** The existing ECT-01 literals are used unchanged for the PC (nothing about the PC changes but its locus); the transition cites the custody record as the continuing reference and owns no PC identity.

## Residuals

- Exactly one journey per PC under the current one-transition / one-movement slots. A second commitment, request, Logistics commitment or transition is a version conflict at every layer.
- The delivery boundary is declared by the caller of `LogisticsAdapter.commitMovement`; nothing here derives, defaults or validates it against distance (no source supplies one). The player authors no timing.
- No automatic action-result presentation: the existing Event-Signal output path (PR #36–#40) can present lawful results separately.
- A binding that is no longer the PC's, or a deactivated PC, cannot occur under today's sealed binding and unimplemented deactivation; the chain checks that would refuse it are defence in depth and are exercised only where state can be forged (raw commits, saved-state edits).
- Local success is not provider or recovery acceptance (IA-07 BLOCKED).
