# GIP Office / Authority Locus Backbone Foundation

Thirteenth bounded slice (the `holder_ref` branch; the alternate `decision_body_ref` branch was added by the fourteenth slice, see [the section below](#decision-body-office-reference-branch-fourteenth-slice)), built on the GIP Stable Institution Identity Backbone Foundation (`docs/gip-institution-identity-foundation.md`), the Campaign Continuity Foundation (`docs/campaign-package.md`) and the Canonical World Identity & Owner Foundation (`docs/canonical-world-foundation.md`). **One explicitly authored office input beneath one already-committed Institution Identity** makes the lawful GIP owner (`gip` / `gip_institution_identity`, IM-06) commit **one canonical Office / Authority Locus**; save → close → load → continue preserves the same office, its authority fields, its prerequisite institution reference and its provenance; and later lawful subsystems may **read it by exact key without gaining GIP write authority**. In ordinary language: the engine can now remember that **institution X has office Y, whose authority comes from source Z, covers this competence, carries these powers, duties and rules, and currently points to this holder**. **Authority belongs to the office/order, not personally to the holder.** It must not — and does not — let anyone infer that the holder owns the authority, chose anything, that a law was enacted, that anyone knows of the office, or that the authority has been exercised.

Governing source (exact pins live in `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` and the owner registry): **WS-007** — the Sovereign-ratified GIP Working Operational Draft v2.1 (frozen F-027 pin `2026-09-24T01:42:24.952Z`) §2.1, §2.2, §3.2 and §21.1/§21.2, with the GIP Scaling Profile v1.1 §3 and §5 (Red-confirmed at pin `2026-09-25T00:59:55.526Z`), the OB-046 closed record and F-017 v0.3 in the lineage. Hard-preflight selection and scope: the thirteenth-slice implementation brief (Notion `3ec20af9-01e0-8131-9de2-f77b21505ee7`, pin `2026-10-01T15:18:37.180Z`).

**Not implemented (out of scope, unchanged):** delegations, concurrence requirements, review/veto/override and conditional authority; appointment, removal, succession, delegation, authority amendment, holder replacement or any other office transition; Governance Instruments, collective decision procedures and votes; authority exercise or implementation reach; institution rename/split/merge/abolition; faction / Autonomous Collective promotion; Named-Actor decision-making; KTI institution-wide development or organizational-scope learning (the implemented KTI transfer evidence remains named-recipient scoped; no institution-wide evidence bridge is invented); Diplomacy; Economy consequences; Epistemic dissemination of office knowledge; NPK/UI/host exposure; any RNG, seed, roll, prior or chooser (GitHub Issue #11 is **still open and untouched**); provider adoption, deployment, migration activation, calibration closure and PF-6/F-028 (**unchanged and blocked**). The acceptance register gains only truthful **partial** evidence (IA-01, IA-02, IA-03, IA-06, IA-22); no row is promoted and no open gate changes.

## Identity, keys, lifecycle and idempotence

- **Exact join.** The office's `institution_id` is the whole join to the Institution Identity: the admission requires the identity at `gip/institution/<institution_id>/identity`, pinned in `reads` and `depends_on`. An institution label never joins (a label that happens to be a valid identifier names no identity and is refused as a missing prerequisite), a foreign fact never stands in, and an office cannot precede its institution.
- **One slot per office of an institution.** The key is `institution_id` × `office_id` (Scaling Profile §3 cardinality: Office/Institution × authority relation). A retry, a duplicate construction or a raw-store write reaches the same occupied slot, so no sibling authority exists. `office_id` is addressed beneath its institution; this foundation does not add a cross-institution office-id uniqueness index (that would need a scan or a second ledger).
- **ADVANCING, initial establishment only.** An office can later lawfully change holder, powers, delegation or appointment/removal state, so the kind is `ADVANCING`: the owner's own expected-version chain, not a permanent seal. This slice implements the first creation only; GIP admission refuses every other change (`AMENDMENT_NOT_SUPPORTED`), and a second creation of an occupied key is refused by the store (`VERSION_CONFLICT`). Later GIP work adds owner-defined transitions against the same key — no second ledger or sibling record is needed.
- **Retry converges, conflict fails closed.** Re-presenting the same intent (a lost reply, a restart, a crash at any durable step) is reconciled by identity (RC-16) to the original receipt. A different intent for an established office — equal or different content — and the same intent with changed content are refused; the committed office is never rewritten.
- **Authority is at the office.** `holder_ref` is a bare reference. It is not resolved against any actor, and no authority, personality, capability, knowledge, loyalty, competence or permission is copied into any Named-Actor or other owner's state; establishment writes exactly one key. The Institution Identity stays byte-for-byte unchanged.
- **No scan, clock or RNG.** Establishing or reading one office touches only its institution's identity key and its own key; only the diagnostic load-time verifier lists facts, once per open. There is no timer, tick, polling or randomness.

## Interpretations recorded for review

1. **Authored references and prose are carried, not interpreted.** `authority_source_ref` is an opaque canonical reference (not resolved: no authority-source ontology, Governance Instrument or constitutive act is implemented), and `holder_ref` is an opaque canonical reference (not resolved: no actor-existence rule is ratified for it). `competence_scope`, each power and duty, and the three rules are authored text exactly as stated — no power taxonomy, scope grammar or rule language is invented.
2. **Unknown/unresolved rules are carried as authored prose.** WS-007 §3.2 says a selection, succession or removal mechanism that does not exist, is unknown or is unresolved is recorded explicitly rather than invented. Each rule stays required authored text; if the authoritative authored value itself states that the mechanism is absent, unknown or unresolved, that prose is carried verbatim and the engine does not interpret it. No status enum, special literal, rule language or interpreter exists, and a missing, null or non-text (structured) rule fails closed.
3. **`powers[]` and `duties[]` are required arrays of authored text, carried verbatim.** WS-007 §3.2 fixes no minimum count and no uniqueness, so none is imposed: an explicitly authored empty array or a repeated entry is stored exactly as authored (no normalisation or deduplication).
4. **Single facet.** The office reuses the owner's one registered facet (`gip_institution_identity`, a technical encoding); the kinds are told apart by their exclusive key spaces, as KTI and the other multi-kind owners do. The existing identity facet and its fixtures are therefore unchanged.

These are narrowings, not new rules; each is a candidate for the later GIP work it belongs to.

## Decision-Body Office Reference Branch (fourteenth slice)

WS-007 §3.2 defines the office reference as **`holder_ref OR decision_body_ref`**. The thirteenth slice implemented only `holder_ref`; the fourteenth slice completes the alternate **initial-establishment** branch, so a new office may now point to a decision body rather than one individual. In ordinary language the engine can now remember that **institution X has office Y; office Y owns this authored authority, and its present institutional reference is decision body B rather than one named holder.** That does **not** mean decision body B was modeled as an autonomous collective, that its members exist, that it voted, that it knows anything, or that the authority was exercised. Authority is persisted at the office/order, never at the body or its members.

Hard-preflight selection and scope: the fourteenth-slice implementation brief (Notion `3ec20af9-01e0-8122-b2ee-f43008318191`, pin `2026-10-01T16:33:14.593Z`).

**What changed.** (1) The executable office contract (`contracts/src/gip.ts`) implements the source's `holder_ref OR decision_body_ref`: the office value is now exactly one of two strict branches sharing every common authored field — `OfficeAuthorityLocusHolderSchema` (… + `holder_ref`, exactly the thirteenth-slice value) and `OfficeAuthorityLocusDecisionBodySchema` (… + `decision_body_ref`) — joined as `OfficeAuthorityLocusSchema`. There is no discriminator field (the source defines none): the branch **is** the one reference field present. An office carrying both references, neither, an empty/null/structured reference or any unmodelled field matches no branch and fails closed, in the adapter and for a raw store commit. (2) The office kind's governing-rule citation now truthfully reflects the implemented surface (see interpretation 5): `GIP_OFFICE_RULE` is the current citation, the thirteenth-slice text is retained as `GIP_OFFICE_RULE_THIRTEENTH_SLICE`, new offices must carry the current one (admission), and load accepts the historical one only for a genuine legacy holder office. (3) Test fixtures gain `decisionBodyOfficeAuthorityLocus` (and `DECISION_BODY_REF_*`). The engine's key, admission checks, adapter methods and load-time verifier are otherwise unchanged, because none of them ever read the reference.

**What did not change** (the same, for both branches): the GIP owner and its one facet; the one office fact kind (`gip_office_authority_locus`) and its `ADVANCING` lifecycle classification; the canonical key `gip/institution/<institution_id>/office/<office_id>` (the reference branch is **not** an identity dimension, so a decision-body office never mints a sibling authority and a retry/replay converges on the same office); the one prerequisite (the exact committed Institution Identity, read by key at its current version and depended on); the create-only establishment path and `AMENDMENT_NOT_SUPPORTED` for every later write; the persistence/recovery path and load-time integrity; the provenance and frozen-source lineage (the same WS-007 artifact, exact native pin and authority lineage; only the clause text of the office citation moves, see interpretation 5). Existing holder-reference saves load without migration, rewriting or reinterpretation.

**Boundary.** `decision_body_ref` is an opaque canonical reference only. It is never resolved or dereferenced against any Named Actor, Autonomous Collective State, Population, Institution Identity, membership or other owner; no decision-body object, membership ledger, council roster, faction, aggregate stance, actor state or collective personality is created; and nothing about competence, permission, consent, acceptance, knowledge, deliberation, choice, vote, closure, procedure or exercise is inferred from it. Establishment writes exactly the one office key and reads exactly the one Institution Identity key, whatever the reference names (spy-proven in tests). It adds no GIP stage, close, tick, scheduler or periodic work, requests no actor/ACS/Faction decision, creates no F-014D packet and touches no KTI, Population, Culture, Economy, Production, Logistics, Military, Health, Diplomacy or World state.

**Still unsupported, and failing closed.** An established holder office cannot become a decision-body office; an established decision-body office cannot change body, switch to a holder, be appointed/removed/succeeded, delegate or have its authority amended. Any such write is refused by GIP admission as `AMENDMENT_NOT_SUPPORTED` (a second creation of the occupied key is a `VERSION_CONFLICT`); the adapter exposes no method for any of them. Not implemented either: a GIP decision procedure, vote or closure rule; Governance Instruments, Scope Bindings and Administrative Reach; institution-wide KTI evidence; Autonomous Collective State, Faction State and Named-Actor decision-making; and **any** RNG, seed, roll, prior, chooser or uncertainty mechanism — GitHub Issue #11 is **still open and untouched**. PF-6/F-028 stays **BLOCKED / NOT ACTIVATED** and no other F-027 gate changed. The acceptance register gains only truthful **partial** evidence on the same five rows (IA-01, IA-02, IA-03, IA-06, IA-22); no row is promoted.

### Interpretations recorded for review (fourteenth slice)

5. **Governing-rule provenance: a truthful current citation and a narrowly bounded historical one.** Canonical integrity compares an office fact's recorded governing rule to the registry citation _whole_ on every load. The thirteenth-slice citation says `decision_body_ref` is "not implemented": lawful provenance for the holder offices committed under it (which must load unchanged, with no migration or rewriting of those facts), but untrue for anything committed once the branch is implemented. So: (a) `GIP_OFFICE_RULE` is now the **current** citation — the same WS-007 artifact, exact native pin (`2026-09-24T01:42:24.952Z`), authority lineage, effective basis and `supersedes`, with the clause reading `holder_ref OR decision_body_ref` and no longer calling the branch unimplemented; (b) the thirteenth-slice citation is retained byte-for-byte as `GIP_OFFICE_RULE_THIRTEENTH_SLICE` and registered on the office kind as its only `legacyRule`; (c) GIP admission requires the **current** citation on every new office of either branch, so the historical one is never stamped on a write; (d) on load a persisted office may carry the current citation, or **exactly the whole historical citation and only if its value is a holder-branch office** (the one class it lawfully governed). A decision-body office carrying the historical citation, any altered or partial historical citation, the historical citation on another kind, or any other rule is `RULE_DRIFT`; the compatibility is not an "accept any old rule" path. Both citations' hashes are pinned by conformance tests. A campaign may therefore hold legacy-citation holder offices beside current-citation offices of either branch.
6. **`decision_body_ref` has the same canonical-reference form as `holder_ref`.** The source fixes no format for either; both are the engine's opaque canonical identifier type and nothing else, with no ontology, existence rule or resolution. If a lawful decision-body referent ontology or owner is ever ratified, resolution belongs to that later work.
7. **Two strict branches, no discriminator.** Frozen WS-007 §3.2 is the authority for `holder_ref OR decision_body_ref`; the executable contract only implements it. The XOR is carried by the shape (a strict object per branch), not by a source-invented `kind`/`branch` field or a refinement; the `OfficeAuthorityLocus` type is the corresponding union.
