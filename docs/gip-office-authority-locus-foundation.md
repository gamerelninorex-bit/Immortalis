# GIP Office / Authority Locus Backbone Foundation

Thirteenth bounded slice, built on the GIP Stable Institution Identity Backbone Foundation (`docs/gip-institution-identity-foundation.md`), the Campaign Continuity Foundation (`docs/campaign-package.md`) and the Canonical World Identity & Owner Foundation (`docs/canonical-world-foundation.md`). **One explicitly authored office input beneath one already-committed Institution Identity** makes the lawful GIP owner (`gip` / `gip_institution_identity`, IM-06) commit **one canonical Office / Authority Locus**; save → close → load → continue preserves the same office, its authority fields, its prerequisite institution reference and its provenance; and later lawful subsystems may **read it by exact key without gaining GIP write authority**. In ordinary language: the engine can now remember that **institution X has office Y, whose authority comes from source Z, covers this competence, carries these powers, duties and rules, and currently points to this holder**. **Authority belongs to the office/order, not personally to the holder.** It must not — and does not — let anyone infer that the holder owns the authority, chose anything, that a law was enacted, that anyone knows of the office, or that the authority has been exercised.

Governing source (exact pins live in `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` and the owner registry): **WS-007** — the Sovereign-ratified GIP Working Operational Draft v2.1 (frozen F-027 pin `2026-09-24T01:42:24.952Z`) §2.1, §2.2, §3.2 and §21.1/§21.2, with the GIP Scaling Profile v1.1 §3 and §5 (Red-confirmed at pin `2026-09-25T00:59:55.526Z`), the OB-046 closed record and F-017 v0.3 in the lineage. Hard-preflight selection and scope: the thirteenth-slice implementation brief (Notion `3ec20af9-01e0-8131-9de2-f77b21505ee7`, pin `2026-10-01T15:18:37.180Z`).

**Not implemented (out of scope, unchanged):** `decision_body_ref`; delegations, concurrence requirements, review/veto/override and conditional authority; appointment, removal, succession, delegation, authority amendment, holder replacement or any other office transition; Governance Instruments, collective decision procedures and votes; authority exercise or implementation reach; institution rename/split/merge/abolition; faction / Autonomous Collective promotion; Named-Actor decision-making; KTI institution-wide development or organizational-scope learning (the implemented KTI transfer evidence remains named-recipient scoped; no institution-wide evidence bridge is invented); Diplomacy; Economy consequences; Epistemic dissemination of office knowledge; NPK/UI/host exposure; any RNG, seed, roll, prior or chooser (GitHub Issue #11 is **still open and untouched**); provider adoption, deployment, migration activation, calibration closure and PF-6/F-028 (**unchanged and blocked**). The acceptance register gains only truthful **partial** evidence (IA-01, IA-02, IA-03, IA-06, IA-22); no row is promoted and no open gate changes.

## Identity, keys, lifecycle and idempotence

- **Exact join.** The office's `institution_id` is the whole join to the Institution Identity: the admission requires the identity at `gip/institution/<institution_id>/identity`, pinned in `reads` and `depends_on`. An institution label never joins (a label that happens to be a valid identifier names no identity and is refused as a missing prerequisite), a foreign fact never stands in, and an office cannot precede its institution.
- **One slot per office of an institution.** The key is `institution_id` × `office_id` (Scaling Profile §3 cardinality: Office/Institution × authority relation). A retry, a duplicate construction or a raw-store write reaches the same occupied slot, so no sibling authority exists. `office_id` is addressed beneath its institution; this foundation does not add a cross-institution office-id uniqueness index (that would need a scan or a second ledger).
- **ADVANCING, initial establishment only.** An office can later lawfully change holder, powers, delegation or appointment/removal state, so the kind is `ADVANCING`: the owner's own expected-version chain, not a permanent seal. This slice implements the first creation only; GIP admission refuses every other change (`AMENDMENT_NOT_SUPPORTED`), and a second creation of an occupied key is refused by the store (`VERSION_CONFLICT`). Later GIP work adds owner-defined transitions against the same key — no second ledger or sibling record is needed.
- **Retry converges, conflict fails closed.** Re-presenting the same intent (a lost reply, a restart, a crash at any durable step) is reconciled by identity (RC-16) to the original receipt. A different intent for an established office — equal or different content — and the same intent with changed content are refused; the committed office is never rewritten.
- **Authority is at the office.** `holder_ref` is a bare reference. It is not resolved against any actor, and no authority, personality, capability, knowledge, loyalty, competence or permission is copied into any Named-Actor or other owner's state; establishment writes exactly one key. The Institution Identity stays byte-for-byte unchanged.
- **No scan, clock or RNG.** Establishing or reading one office touches only its institution's identity key and its own key; only the diagnostic load-time verifier lists facts, once per open. There is no timer, tick, polling or randomness.

## Interpretations recorded for review

1. **Authored references and prose are carried, not interpreted.** `authority_source_ref` is an opaque canonical reference (not resolved: no authority-source ontology, Governance Instrument or constitutive act is implemented), and `holder_ref` is an opaque canonical reference (not resolved: no actor-existence rule is ratified for it). `competence_scope`, each power and duty, and the three rules are authored text exactly as stated — no power taxonomy, scope grammar or rule language is invented.
2. **Unknown/unresolved rules are carried as authored prose.** WS-007 §3.2 says a selection, succession or removal mechanism that does not exist, is unknown or is unresolved is recorded explicitly rather than invented. Each rule stays required authored text; if the authoritative authored value itself states that the mechanism is absent, unknown or unresolved, that prose is carried verbatim and the engine does not interpret it. No status enum, special literal, rule language or interpreter exists, and a missing, null or non-text (structured) rule fails closed.
3. **`powers[]` and `duties[]` are required arrays of authored text, carried verbatim.** WS-007 §3.2 fixes no minimum count and no uniqueness, so none is imposed: an explicitly authored empty array or a repeated entry is stored exactly as authored (no normalisation or deduplication).
4. **Single facet.** The office reuses the owner's one registered facet (`gip_institution_identity`, a technical encoding); the kinds are told apart by their exclusive key spaces, as KTI and the other multi-kind owners do. The existing identity facet and its fixtures are therefore unchanged.

These are narrowings, not new rules; each is a candidate for the later GIP work it belongs to.
