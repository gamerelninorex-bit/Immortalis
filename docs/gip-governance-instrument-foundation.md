# GIP Governance Instrument Core Backbone Foundation

Sixteenth bounded slice, built on the GIP Authority Scope Binding Backbone Foundation (`docs/gip-authority-scope-binding-foundation.md`, fifteenth slice), the GIP Office / Authority Locus Backbone Foundation (`docs/gip-office-authority-locus-foundation.md`, thirteenth and fourteenth slices), the GIP Stable Institution Identity Backbone Foundation (`docs/gip-institution-identity-foundation.md`), the Campaign Continuity Foundation (`docs/campaign-package.md`) and the Canonical World Identity & Owner Foundation (`docs/canonical-world-foundation.md`). **One explicitly authored Governance Instrument core record beneath one already-committed Office / Authority Locus and one already-committed Scope Binding of that same office** makes the lawful GIP owner (`gip` / `gip_institution_identity`, IM-06) commit **one canonical Instrument**; save → close → load → continue preserves the same Instrument, its two references, its authored fields and its provenance; and later lawful subsystems may **read it by exact key without gaining GIP write authority**. In ordinary language: the engine can now remember that **this durable governance rule or act exists; this exact authority is its source; this is its authority basis; this is its one core operative-effect class; this existing Scope Binding says where it is aimed; these are its authored validity entries under the relevant governing orders; and this is its authored lifecycle state.** It must not — and does not — let anyone infer that a proposal was lawfully enacted, that a decision procedure ran, that the Instrument is valid, in force or applicable to a live person, place or transaction, that it was promulgated, disseminated, implemented or enforced, or that anyone knows, accepts, obeys or resists it.

Governing source (exact pins live in `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` and the owner registry): **WS-007** — the Sovereign-ratified GIP Working Operational Draft v2.1 (frozen F-027 pin `2026-09-24T01:42:24.952Z`) §2.1, §2.2, §4.1, §4.2 and §21.1/§21.2, with Concept Amendment v2.1 A7 (validity-by-order and lifecycle/operative status are separate dimensions) and the GIP Scaling Profile v1.1 §3 (Instrument/Clause cardinality; this slice implements the Instrument unit only), §4 and §5 (Red-confirmed at pin `2026-09-25T00:59:55.526Z`), the OB-046 closed record and F-017 v0.3 in the lineage. Hard-preflight selection and scope: the sixteenth-slice implementation brief (Notion `3ec20af9-01e0-81af-9e91-ed4d4816474e`, pin `2026-10-01T22:06:35.989Z`).

**Not implemented (out of scope, unchanged):** institutional decision procedure, proposal passage or enactment, Actor/Faction/Autonomous Collective choice, voting or assent; runtime validity calculation and runtime lifecycle advancement; addressable clauses (WS-007 §4.4) and any multi-effect array; every WS-007 §4.3 conditional field except the one optional `predecessor_instrument_ref` (twenty-first slice, below); applicability resolution; promulgation or publication; dissemination or awareness; Administrative Reach, implementation, enforcement, compliance and resistance; office appointment, removal and succession; Political Relation; any governing-order, constitutional-law or authority-basis object; foreign-owner state; any new GIP temporal stage, Close Basis, tick, scheduler, polling loop or lifecycle timer; NPK/UI/host exposure; any RNG, seed, roll, prior, chooser or uncertainty mechanism (GitHub Issue #11 is **still open and untouched**); provider adoption, deployment, migration activation, calibration closure and PF-6/F-028 (**unchanged and blocked**). The acceptance register gains only truthful **partial** evidence (IA-01, IA-02, IA-03, IA-06, IA-22); no row is promoted, IA-04 is not claimed (no temporal stage or close is added) and no open gate changes.

## Identity, keys, lifecycle and idempotence

- **Identity is `instrument_id` alone.** The key is `gip/instrument/<instrument_id>`. The source office, the Scope Binding, a governing order, a recipient, a subject, a place and the lifecycle status are **not** identity dimensions; reusing an `instrument_id` beneath another office or binding reaches the same occupied slot and fails.
- **Two exact prerequisites, one authority.** `source_authority_ref` is the exact address of one committed Office / Authority Locus (the office's own key); `scope_binding_ref` is the exact address of one committed Scope Binding (the binding's own key). The contract requires the binding's office address to equal the source authority, and admission and load verification re-check it against the committed facts, so a Scope Binding of a different authority fails closed. The office may carry a `holder_ref` or a `decision_body_ref`; the branch changes nothing about the Instrument, its key, its prerequisites or its write, and neither the holder nor the body is ever read.
- **One slot per Instrument.** A retry, a duplicate construction or a raw-store write reaches the same occupied slot, so no sibling Instrument exists. Distinct Instruments are distinct **only** by their canonical authored `instrument_id`: two Instruments with identical content under different ids are both kept (nothing is merged or deduplicated). `validity_by_order[]` stays inside the one Instrument; no Instrument is ever copied per governing order, recipient, resident, place, Population member, actor or transaction.
- **ADVANCING, initial establishment only.** An Instrument can later lawfully change validity or lifecycle (WS-007 §4.1), so the kind is `ADVANCING`: the owner's own expected-version chain, not a permanent seal. This slice implements the first creation only; GIP admission refuses every other change (`AMENDMENT_NOT_SUPPORTED`), and a second creation of an occupied key is refused by the store (`VERSION_CONFLICT`). Later GIP work adds owner-defined transitions against the same key — no second ledger or sibling record is needed.
- **Retry converges, conflict fails closed.** Re-presenting the same intent (a lost reply, a restart, a crash at any durable step) is reconciled by identity (RC-16) to the original receipt. A different intent for an established Instrument — equal or different content — and the same intent with changed content are refused; the committed Instrument is never rewritten.
- **Authority, binding and identity stay untouched.** The Instrument writes exactly one key. The office, the Scope Binding and the Institution Identity stay byte-for-byte unchanged.
- **No scan, clock or RNG.** Establishing an Instrument touches only its office key, its binding key and its own key; reading one touches only its own key; only the diagnostic load-time verifier lists facts, once per open. There is no timer, tick, polling or randomness.

## Interpretations recorded for review

1. **References are exact canonical addresses, not new fields.** The brief fixes the Instrument to the seven source identifiers and forbids `institution_id`, `office_id` and `scope_binding_id` convenience fields, yet `source_authority_ref` must reference an exact committed office and `scope_binding_ref` an exact committed Scope Binding. The office is addressed in the repository by its own key (`gip/institution/<institution_id>/office/<office_id>`) and a binding by its own key (`…/scope-binding/<scope_binding_id>`), so each reference carries exactly that address — a single scalar string — and the same-authority rule is a structural comparison of the two addresses. No resolution mechanism, label match or foreign identity is introduced; the address grammar is the existing key encoding (a technical encoding, as the brief permits).
2. **`validity_by_order[]` is a mandatory authored array with no source-undefined restriction.** WS-007 §4.1 defines the field, the minimal entry shape (`governing_order_ref`, `validity_status`) and the core validity literals, but no minimum cardinality, no uniqueness of `governing_order_ref` and no deduplication, merging or conflict-resolution rule. This slice therefore keeps the field required, keeps each entry strict, and introduces **no** minimum cardinality, uniqueness, deduplication, merging or conflict-resolution rule: an empty array and repeated `governing_order_ref` entries (with the same or different `validity_status`) are carried exactly as authored — never reordered, merged, deduplicated, reconciled or interpreted. This does not declare repeated entries legally meaningful or desirable; it preserves source silence, and any future restriction requires separately pinned authority.
3. **Opaque references.** `authority_basis_ref` and each `governing_order_ref` are the engine's opaque canonical identifier type and nothing else: no existence rule, ontology or resolution; no foreign-owner state is created from them (tests name a real locus, world, actor, body, institution and the office itself as referents and show the reads and writes do not change).
4. **Authored statuses are not reconciled.** Validity and lifecycle are independent authored values: an `active` lifecycle under a `void` order and a `repealed` lifecycle under a `valid` order are both stored exactly as authored, because the engine calculates neither and WS-007 §4.1 states that lifecycle is not validity.
5. **Conditional fields and clauses fail closed.** The strict contract rejects every §4.3 field name other than the twenty-first slice's `predecessor_instrument_ref` (and a clause or multi-effect surrogate) rather than ignoring or carrying it; an Instrument that needs one is unsupported by this build.
6. **A new governing-rule citation; the others are untouched.** The sixteenth-slice Instrument kind recorded the citation now preserved as `GIP_INSTRUMENT_RULE_SIXTEENTH_SLICE`; the current successor `GIP_INSTRUMENT_RULE` (twenty-first slice, below) replaces it for new writes. At the sixteenth slice the Instrument citation had the same WS-007 artifact, exact native pin and authority lineage as the other GIP kinds; clauses §2.1/§2.2, §4.1, §4.2, §21.1/§21.2, Amendment A7 and the Scaling Profile §3/§4/§5. `GIP_RULE`, `GIP_OFFICE_RULE`, its historical thirteenth-slice companion and `GIP_SCOPE_BINDING_RULE` are byte-for-byte unchanged (hashes pinned by tests), so nothing is rewritten.
7. **Single facet.** The Instrument reuses the owner's one registered facet (`gip_institution_identity`, a technical encoding); the kinds are told apart by their exclusive key spaces, as before. No existing GIP kind, fixture or save changes.

These are recorded interpretations of the brief, not new rules; none adds a source-undefined executable restriction, and each is a candidate for the later GIP work it belongs to.

## Twenty-first slice: the optional `predecessor_instrument_ref`

The twenty-first bounded slice (implementation brief: Notion `3ed20af9-01e0-8168-8fb8-e2171c79ba49`, pin `2026-10-02T07:17:00.367Z`) extends the strict Instrument with the exact WS-007 §4.3 field `predecessor_instrument_ref` and nothing else from §4.3. Initial establishment only: no lifecycle transition, dereference, dependency, supersession behavior or foreign-owner mutation was added.

- **One optional, singular, opaque reference.** `predecessor_instrument_ref` is one authored reference from the current Instrument toward a predecessor Instrument identity/source, in the repository's existing canonical opaque-reference representation (`EntityId`, as the other GIP reference fields). It is carried exactly as authored: omission means absence (no default is synthesized); `null`, an explicit `undefined`, an array, a map or object surrogate, an empty or malformed value and every renamed or plural alias fail closed. No normalization, expansion, aliasing or derived surrogate exists.
- **A lineage pointer, nothing more.** It is never dereferenced on establishment, read, recovery or integrity verification; no referent need exist; no referent lifecycle, validity, owner lookup, key join or dependency is required or registered; the referenced predecessor is never read, written, back-linked or rewritten; no successor pointer, supersession act, lifecycle advancement, validity change or reciprocal edge is created; and no legal continuity, authority, recognition, applicability, implementation, enforcement or knowledge is inferred from it. Self-reference, shared references and reference cycles are not checked (the source defines no such rule): they are authored text.
- **Not an identity dimension.** The key is still `gip/instrument/<instrument_id>`; two establishments with the same `instrument_id` address the same occupied slot whatever the optional reference, so a same-intent retry with a changed, added or removed reference is a changed-intent conflict and a different establishment at an occupied slot fails closed with no sibling Instrument.
- **Admission is unchanged.** One Instrument write at expected version zero, exactly the two existing prerequisites (the source Office and its Scope Binding), no extra read, no `depends_on` entry for the reference, no second commit, no Temporal object. Owner, facet, fact kind, key, ADVANCING classification and Instrument/Clause unit are unchanged.
- **Governing-rule lineage.** The sixteenth-slice citation (which says §4.3 is not implemented) is preserved byte-for-byte as `GIP_INSTRUMENT_RULE_SIXTEENTH_SLICE` (hash pinned) for genuine historical core-only Instruments. The current `GIP_INSTRUMENT_RULE` is a truthful successor over the same WS-007 source, pin and lineage covering the core plus only the optional `predecessor_instrument_ref`, with every other §4.3 field and every §4.4 clause stated as not implemented. New writes (with or without the reference) use only the current citation; admission refuses the historical one.
- **Historical compatibility.** The Instrument kind uses the existing fact-kind `legacyRule` mechanism (no new generalized framework): a persisted fact may carry the historical citation only as the whole identical citation on a value that conforms to the old core-only shape (`GovernanceInstrumentCoreSchema`). A historical-citation Instrument carrying `predecessor_instrument_ref` is therefore rule drift on load, since the old rule never authorized the field. Nothing is migrated, rewritten or re-cited: a loaded historical Instrument stays byte-identical, and the campaign continues beside it. The Office kind's unrelated holder-branch compatibility is untouched.

Evidence: `tests/contract/gip-instrument-predecessor-schemas.test.ts`, `tests/integration/gip-instrument-predecessor.test.ts`, `tests/conformance/gip-instrument-predecessor-conformance.test.ts`; the predecessor tests were narrowed only where the field, the successor citation or the Instrument kind's new historical compatibility entry made a pinned statement stale. Out of scope and unchanged: `supersession_ref` and every other §4.3 field, addressable clauses, any validity or lifecycle transition, predecessor dereference or validation, Office / Scope Binding change, foreign-owner state, GitHub Issue #11 (still open and untouched) and every provider, deployment, migration, calibration and PF-6/F-028 state. The Runtime Contract and `open-gates.yaml` are byte-for-byte unchanged.
