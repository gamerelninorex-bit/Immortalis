# Exact Finite Raw-Source Drawdown & Conserved Production Reserve Inflow Foundation

Bounded slice on the Production & Resource Conservation Backbone (`docs/production-foundation.md`), the Canonical World Identity & Owner Foundation (`docs/canonical-world-foundation.md`) and the Turnless Temporal & Resolution Transaction Foundation (`docs/temporal-foundation.md`). The canonical campaign can now hold **one exact, finite World-State Source Condition, established before any request**, and **Production's own, distinct Capability / Access basis for it**; start **one identity-stable, scoped request for an exact amount of the same untransformed raw material**; and, at that extraction's one explicitly declared boundary, have the existing IM-02 kernel resolve — in dependency order — **Production's Production-caused Source Delta, World-State's net Source Condition (delivery, with any PHYSICAL exhaustion) and, only after that, Production's conserved Reserve inflow**. The source remainder and the Reserve increase are **derived by the owners**, never supplied by the request or the fixture; a later independent request cannot draw what is spent; and after an interruption between the owners a later lawful recovery finishes or truthfully holds the same identities.

## The chain, in dependency order

One extraction is ONE temporal object (`obj-extraction-{id}`) with ONE earned boundary (the explicitly declared extraction boundary, fixture evidence and never a Production default) and three declared stages with disjoint produced keys, each reading only the output of the stage before it — a chain, never an instantaneous NOW cycle:

1. **Production emits the Source Delta** (`production-emit-source-delta`; reads the sealed extraction). It is the key's Pending Drawdown.
2. **World-State delivers it** (`world-state-deliver-source-drawdown`; reads the delta). It commits the net Source Condition, the delivery record and any PHYSICAL exhaustion.
3. **Production credits the Reserve inflow** (`production-credit-reserve-inflow`; reads the delivery, the delta and the extraction). It credits exactly the delivered quantity, once, closes the commitment and advances the key's watermark.

At every committed cut each unit is in exactly one place: still source-owned (committed, nothing yet moved), an undelivered Source Delta, delivered and awaiting its credit (the source is already net; the Reserve not yet credited), or Reserve-held. There is no spendable output before the delivery. A delivery cannot decrement twice (it is recorded once, by identity). All three stages are derived by pure functions (`source-drawdown/derive.ts`) that the owner operation and IM-11 admission both call.

## Start, commitment and concurrency

An extraction **start** is one group (the sealed extraction and the key's account holding the commitment) on pins of the source-owned request (a foreign fact, referenced and never copied), the basis, the Source Condition, the Reserve and the canonical temporal basis. The quantity is the pinned request's. Admission re-derives it and refuses, recording nothing, unless: the request is its own source's fact (an unregistered extension owner's; never a canonical, kernel, Production or World-State fact), scoped to the Source Condition's own locality and asking for exactly the same material and unit; the source is exact, a committed World-State Source Condition at its current version and not exhausted; the basis covers this exact key, material, unit and locality and its named physical access fact is one the source states; the Reserve holds the same material in the same unit at the same locality; and the extraction starts at canonical time with a later boundary.

Scarcity follows F-017 v0.3 §4 "Same-time contention": for a conserved quantity with no owner allocation, priority or neutral rule, claim arrival or processing order decides nothing. A claim made at a **strictly earlier canonical instant** is genuine causal precedence — it protects its material, and a later-instant claim the remaining material (`recoverable − commitments of strictly earlier instants`) cannot support receives no credit and no partial fill (`CLAIM_INCOMPATIBLE`, `INSUFFICIENT_RECOVERABLE`, nothing recorded). Claims of the **same canonical instant** have no precedence over one another, so none is refused for arriving second or credited for arriving first: each is recorded, and its standing is a function of the whole set (`claimStanding`). If the same-instant claims together fit what earlier instants left, all are credited; if not, **every one is `CONTESTED`** — none emits a Source Delta, none is delivered or credited (Production's admission refuses the emit stage, `CONTESTED_SAME_INSTANT_CLAIM`), and the conflict stays unresolved, preserved for the lawful owner/chooser. No priority, allocator, chooser or time rule was added. A second protection, because delivery precedes any read of a key's Source Condition (WS-003 §4B(4)): a key with **undelivered drawdown** holds the read (`CLAIM_INCOMPATIBLE`, `SOURCE_DELIVERY_PENDING`) and the emit stage of a second drawdown is likewise not admitted until the first is delivered, so each key has at most one pending representation and the delivery of "all pending" is the delivery of that one. That hold only orders claims that are both supported; it never changes which are.

## Judgment calls and open residuals (none resolves an open gate)

- **Capability / Access basis is an authored Production premise.** No Population, KTI, Logistics, legal or Economy producer exists for it, so the narrow path is stated by literals (embedded NON-material Tier A labor, direct raw physical collection, no encumbrance); a labor-calibrated, specialist, encumbered or transformative path is unrepresentable. It carries no `can_extract` / `succeeded` assertion, and the source's existence is never taken as capability or permission (§4). Amendment or withdrawal of a basis is not implemented (create-only); a premise is guarded only by the pinned versions.
- **Request authorization** is the existing carrier discipline: the request is the fact of its own source owner, pinned, exact, with no priority/authority reference representable. No participant-authorization model beyond that was invented. Economy-sourced requests are not accepted by this path (no Economy realization stage is declared for an extraction).
- **The extraction boundary is a declared value**, as the Production operation's completion is: no duration, rate or schedule default exists, and a stage resolves only at its object's declared boundary — never at a requested horizon.
- **Pending Drawdown representation.** The one pending representation is the sealed Source Delta; a key's account lists its open commitments by identity, and the extraction's phase (committed, pending, delivered, credited) is derived from the facts that follow it, never stored twice. Because owners commit in separate groups, the instant between World-State's delivery and Production's account close is truthfully "delivered, awaiting credit"; the unit is counted on the source side only.
- **No carried flow.** No rate, profile, renewal, regeneration, Next Boundary Basis, sustainability claim, unknown/latent quantity, source split / merge / re-key or ACCESS exhaustion exists; their absence is structural (strict schemas). A delta for any other resolution key fails closed. If a live source flow were needed, §4B's Next Boundary Basis would apply and this slice would stop rather than enlarge.
- **Registration window.** As for a Production operation, the extraction is started and its temporal object registered as two publications; if a process is interrupted between them, the extraction simply stays committed and unengaged (its quantity truthfully held, the source and Reserve unchanged) and re-presenting the registration completes it. No enrollment guard was added to the kernel (a pre-existing residual of the Production slice, unchanged).
- **Same-instant contention** between incompatible claims on the scarce source is resolved by no order at all (above); the owner allocation, priority or neutral rule that would resolve a contested set does not exist and is the precise dependency for ever crediting one (IA-05 stays NOT_IMPLEMENTED). Contested claims keep protecting their material against later instants (fail-safe, nothing is created) and stay held. Commitments jointly supported by the source complete in either order to the same final state (tested). Stage processing among supported stages uses the existing stable bookkeeping order, which never orders comparable stages. The predecessor Production operation's own start-time claim check (reserve claims) is unchanged and out of this slice.
- **Basis/secrecy** of Production- and World-State-written drawdown facts are inherited from the authored facts they advance, never defaulted.
- **Cost.** The advance path reads the engaged extraction's facts, its source, account and Reserve by identity. `verifySourceDrawdownIntegrity` (like the other verifiers) lists all facts once per open — a diagnostic path (CAL-06), not the advance path.
