# Production & Resource Conservation Backbone Foundation

Fifth bounded slice, built on the Campaign Continuity Foundation (`docs/campaign-package.md`), the Canonical World Identity & Owner Foundation (`docs/canonical-world-foundation.md`), the Turnless Temporal & Resolution Transaction Foundation (`docs/temporal-foundation.md`) and the Population & Cohort Reconciliation Backbone Foundation (`docs/population-foundation.md`). The canonical campaign can now **establish one exact local Production Reserve, start one bounded Production operation that claims exactly the material it needs, reach that operation's explicitly declared completion boundary through the existing IM-02 kernel, consume the claimed input exactly once, complete one durable on one conserved output reference, have the World-State fallback physical-entity custodian accept that same reference exactly once (F-017 CG-2) — and, after save, close and load, continue across the unresolved operation without duplicate consumption, output, completion or acceptance.**

Governing lock: `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` (F-027 v1.0). Sources: WS-003 Production & Resources (Working Operational Draft v1; lock pin `2026-09-25T13:44:44.982Z`) §§2, 3, 4, 8, 9, 13A, 14–16, 19; WG World Generation & World-State Kernel (lock pin `2026-09-25T13:44:22.668Z`), "Ownership Guards — F-016 Ratified Fallback Objective Physical-Entity Custody"; F-016 Integrated Review Candidate v0.4 manifest (pin `2026-09-25T13:48:12.575Z`); F-017 v0.3 §5.7 and CG-2 (conformance pin `2026-09-25T23:42:27.802Z`). **CAL-01 (APPROXIMATE-PARTITION) remains UNCALIBRATED and is not used: every proof is exact. CAL-02 / L-50 remains OPEN: labor and specialist competence are explicitly non-material (Tier A embedded) and no labor-calibrated path exists. CAL-03 adopts no participant number.** Every other F-027 open gate is unchanged.

This is a **foundation proof**, not a production simulator and not a Logistics slice. All fixture data is synthetic (`packages/testkit/src/production-fixtures.ts`); materials, classes, quantities, units and the T0/TC coordinates are test evidence, not IMMORTALIS canon, gameplay defaults or calibration.

## One canon: how it reuses IM-02 / IM-03 / IM-11 / IM-12 / IM-17

Nothing new is persisted outside the existing campaign package. Production and custodian facts are ordinary `OwnerFact`s written through `store.commit`, so the existing validation, expected-version checks, RC-16 receipts, idempotent replay, unknown-outcome reconciliation, hash-chained journal and package/load integrity are the ones in force. **The package format is unchanged (still format version 1); there is no second journal, save, sidecar, inventory or recovery identity.** The temporal coordinator was not changed: Production's objects register through the one `registerObject` path and resolve through the one `advance` (a conformance test asserts the coordinator never names Production). The one additive commit-core change is the error code `CLAIM_INCOMPATIBLE`.

## Start, claim and the exactly-once conservation

An operation **start** is one group (the operation, its RC-06 claim and the Reserve holding that claim open) built on three pins: the source-owned request (a foreign fact in its source's own key space, referenced and never copied), the canonical temporal basis it starts at, and the Reserve. Admission re-derives it:

- the operation, claim, output reference, temporal object and boundary identities are derived from the operation identity alone (the operation identity _is_ the request identity), so a request fixes at most one operation and a retry reaches the same identities;
- the claim is for exactly the declared input quantity of this Reserve's material, and is **supported**: the quantity already held open by the Reserve's own open claims (read by identity) plus this claim must not exceed what the Reserve holds. Otherwise the whole group is refused (`CLAIM_INCOMPATIBLE`) and **nothing is recorded** — no operation, no claim, no object, no change to the Reserve (RC-10: no immortal pending object for a failed claim);
- the operation produces exactly what the request asked for; the request's scope is the Reserve's own locality.

A **completion** is re-derived from the committed operation, claim and Reserve (`deriveCompletion`, the single statement of the arithmetic, used by both the owner operation and admission): the claimed input leaves the Reserve once (recorded once per operation in `consumed`), the claim becomes `CONSUMED`, the operation `COMPLETED`, and exactly the declared output is sealed in the envelope. Nothing is created ex nihilo: the output never enters a Reserve, and Production holds no current quantity of it.

## CG-2: completion is not availability

Production's completion stage and the World-State custodian's acceptance stage are the two declared stages of one operation's object. Availability is **derived from acceptance** (`ProductionAdapter.availability`): `NOT_COMPLETED` → `COMPLETED_PENDING_ACCEPTANCE` → `ACCEPTED_AVAILABLE`, and the last state exists only once the custodian's fact exists for the same conserved reference at the pinned completion version. The custodian's admission re-derives the entity from the pinned envelope (same reference, class, quantity, condition, locality) and the fact is create-only, keyed by the reference, so a second acceptance is a version conflict. After acceptance Production cannot write the entity (its key space is World-State's), and not even World-State can amend it (no amendment mechanism exists). If the acceptance cannot commit (the custodian operation is absent or its group is refused), the stop is `DEPENDENCY_UNRESOLVED` with the boundary still active; the completion stays completed-but-unavailable across save, close and load, and the continuation **adopts** the committed Production stage (never recomputes it) and accepts once.

## Turnless Production completion

One operation is one temporal object with one earned boundary — the explicitly declared completion boundary, fixture evidence and never a Production default — so only the causally engaged operation becomes active. An advance short of the boundary engages and changes nothing; an advance past it resolves the boundary at **its own instant** (the envelope's `completed_at` is the boundary, never a requested horizon); stage identity (`(process, owner operation, result)`) excludes horizon, retry, source-version churn and slicing. A completion group commits only for an engaged, unretired boundary of exactly that operation, and only as its IM-02 stage (see the interrupted-window note below). There is no Production tick, shift loop, annual pass or polling, and unrelated dormant operations/Reserves/objects are never read, staged or written (spy-store measured).

## Judgment calls and open residuals (none resolves an open gate)

- **Claim ownership.** WS-003 §9/§19 assign "open claim references keyed by live commitment × consumed shared constraint" to Production, so the claim is a Production fact and IM-11 admission performs the conflict check, rather than a separate commit-service-owned fact. Commit order confers no priority: there is no preemption, ranking or displacement, a later claim against already-committed material simply receives no credit, and F-017's rule that arrival order decides nothing for a conserved quantity lacking an owner allocation rule is respected by never choosing between claims — same-instant contention between simultaneously staged stages, owner-supplied allocation rules and choosers are **not implemented** (IA-05 stays NOT_IMPLEMENTED).
- **Acceptance read mode.** F-017 names CG-2 a GROUP on the same conserved reference without a read mode. The custodian reads the sealed envelope AS-OF-NOW after the completion commit (the envelope is create-only, so NOW and the completion are the same value); no new Close Basis vocabulary was invented. The two stages form a linear dependency, not a cycle, so no GROUP authorization is used.
- **Request carrier.** The sources fix no carrier vocabulary for a Production Request. The requester holds it in its own key space (as Population's causal owners hold envelope registers); Production pins it and consumes only a well-formed one with no priority/authority reference.
- **Quantities are exact non-negative integers** in a caller-named unit (a technical encoding of "exact"); no fractional, ranged or approximate quantity is representable, and the transformation's input and output quantities are the operation's own declared values.
- **Interrupted completion window (repaired after Blue review of `1db5aa1`).** A stage group is credited only by its transaction, and owner stages commit before the kernel group advances canonical time, so a Production completion (and the custodian's acceptance) can lawfully sit committed, uncited, while canonical time is still before the declared boundary — exactly when a process was interrupted between a stage and the kernel group. Canonical time therefore cannot tell that stage from an arbitrary raw publication of the identical group, and admission originally accepted both. It now distinguishes them by the existing RC-16 / RC-04 identity: a completion or acceptance is lawful **only as its IM-02 stage** — published under the group and intent identity derived from its StageKey, caused by a transaction (`stageIdentityViolation`, the one rule that raw admission and load-time verification both apply). A raw `store.commit` that supplies the exact derived operation/Reserve/claim/envelope under any other identity is refused (`REFERENCE_INVALID`), so it cannot consume the Reserve, seal a future completion or let a raw acceptance expose the durable; load fails closed on an otherwise identical completion or acceptance that lacks the stage identity. A stage that genuinely committed during an interrupted transaction carries that identity, loads, and is **adopted** by re-presenting the same transaction (crash after the completion stage, and after the acceptance stage, are both tested), with no second consumption, output or acceptance. Residual, stated plainly: the identity is derived from public inputs, so a caller that deliberately reproduces the coordinator's stage identity and a transaction cause is indistinguishable from the coordinator committing that stage — the same trusted archivist authority that can write kernel groups raw. That is the existing trust model (Charter principle 14 applies at the host boundary, not here); the repair closes the arbitrary raw publication, not a deliberate impersonation of IM-02, and no new durable record (second scheduler, sidecar or recovery identity) was introduced to do more. Since the PR #8 repair such a stage also depends on the durable authority of its transaction, opened by the kernel before it (`docs/temporal-foundation.md`), which is what keeps this window recoverable without letting a copied identity pass.
- **Same-instant completions on one Reserve.** Two operations whose claims the Reserve jointly supports and whose boundaries coincide both complete in one transaction, each consuming its own claim once; the final Reserve and the consumption record do not depend on stage order. Each envelope's retained `delta` (before/after) is the sequential before-basis of its own group and so follows the coordinator's stable bookkeeping order — recorded evidence, not a substantive result.
- **Basis/secrecy.** Inherited from the authored facts a stage advances (the operation's labels for its group; the envelope's for the accepted entity), never defaulted.
- **Cost.** The advance path reads the engaged operation, its Reserve, claim, the world/locus and the one envelope by identity. `verifyProductionIntegrity` (like the other verifiers) lists all facts once per open — a diagnostic path (CAL-06), not the advance path.
