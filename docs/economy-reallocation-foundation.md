# Conserved OOC Economic Reallocation Between Two Holding Positions

Bounded slice on the Economic Holding / Account position (`docs/economy-holding-foundation.md`) and the canonical store, owner registry and admission machinery it reuses. The canonical campaign can now **move one exact available quantity between two independently committed, distinct Economy-owned Economic Holding positions of the same economic object and unit, on a separately identified OOC scenario reallocation instruction — Economy, not the caller, re-deriving eligibility and both resulting positions atomically — answer a later availability read or hold differently on both positions, and recover the same single movement exactly across save, close and load and across an interruption at every durable step.**

Governing lock: `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` (F-027 v1.0, unchanged; no lock, register, gate or Runtime Contract snapshot was touched). Source: WS-004 Economy / Exchange / Demand (Working Operational Draft v1.1; lock pin `2026-09-24T11:45:02.825Z`) §2.1, §2.2, §3 (holdings are conserved across transfers and recompression), §4 (No-Double-Spend), §20 (Commitment and Conservation), §27.14, §28, §30. The implementation brief is the BLUE v4 bounded implementation brief of 2026-10-10 (native pin `2026-10-10T01:22:57.565Z`).

This is a **conserved book-position movement test for an OOC scenario**, not trade. The updated balances are **not** property title, spend, payment, settlement, exchange performance, delivery, item movement, currency issuance, entitlement creation, fiscal power, player purchasing or autonomous commerce. The instruction is scripted, test-authorized mechanism input — **not** a player's or NPC's in-world choice. A real transfer needs a separately established competent in-world decision or standing allocation mechanism, any materially applicable authority / entitlement, and any downstream result owner; those remain **EXTERNAL PREREQUISITES MISSING FOR LIVE COMMERCE**. Everything in `packages/testkit/src/economy-holding-fixtures.ts` is synthetic (the holders, the object, the unit and the amounts 7, 2, 3, 4, 5, 9).

## The witness

Two openings, committed independently (separate premises, separate publications): position A = holder H, 7 of object O; position B = another holder, 2 of the same O and unit. A later OOC instruction names both positions at the versions it saw, an amount of 3, and its own reallocation / instruction identities. Economy re-reads both positions AS-OF-NOW and commits **A 4 available, B 5 available, combined 9**, in one group. A later availability read shows both changed answers; a new hold is judged on them (B can now be held for 5, A for at most 4). Neither opening premise is rewritten.

## Behaviour

- **Instruction** (`ReallocationInstructionInputSchema`, strict): `campaign_id`, `reallocation_id`, `instruction_id`, `from` / `to` (`holding_id`, `holding_version`) and `quantity`. There is no field for a resulting position, a verdict, an approval or winner, an owner result, a restriction / authority reference, a legal priority, a title, a price, a settlement date or consent.
- **Economy refuses, changing nothing**: a wrong or missing campaign (`INPUT_INVALID`); a missing / unknown position (`PREREQUISITE_MISSING`); the same position on both sides (`INPUT_INVALID`); a stale version on either side or a reused reallocation identity (`VERSION_CONFLICT`); positions of different visibility (`INPUT_INVALID`); an amount that is not **wholly available** on the source, a different object or unit (`INSUFFICIENT_AVAILABLE`). An active encumbrance reduces what the source can send; it neither evaporates nor transfers, and a later cancellation returns its recorded amount to the same source.
- **Atomic**: both positions and the record are one group under expected-version protection on both positions, so two reallocations (or a hold and a reallocation) pinned at the same version cannot both succeed; the loser re-reads and is judged on the real remaining amount. No arrival-order allocation rule was added.
- **Exactly once**: re-presenting the intent replays the original receipt (RC-16); a changed amount, version, position, instruction or reallocation identity under the same intent is `INTENT_CONFLICT`; the same content under a new intent is a duplicate and is refused.
- **Verification**: each position's counters must equal the sum of the reallocations that name it; each reallocation must name two committed positions and their holders as committed, of one object and unit, at versions that exist. Value moved without a recorded cause, or recorded without moving, is detected on open.

## Judgment calls and open residuals (none resolves an open gate)

- **Technical representation.** The sources fix the owner (Economy), the relation fields and the conservation rule (§3) but not an encoding of a movement. `reallocated_in` / `reallocated_out` and the `economy/reallocation` record are technical encodings chosen so the old `established` opening premise is neither reinterpreted nor rewritten and the merged integrity / admission checks are not weakened. The word "transfer" is deliberately not used in the contract vocabulary (the merged scope test forbids it) and the record's `nature` literal names what it is not.
- **Same-holder positions.** The brief requires two distinct positions; it does not forbid one holder holding both. No rule was invented either way: distinctness is by position identity only.
- **Campaign check.** The instruction names its campaign and Economy compares it with the canonical campaign it is applied to; a position of another campaign is not readable from this store in any case.
- **One visibility.** Both positions must carry identical `basis` / `secrecy` labels, since one record cannot truthfully carry two visibilities; otherwise the instruction is refused. This is a conservative technical refusal, not a new substantive rule.
- **Restricted / authority-conditioned positions** are not representable (`restricted` is `0`-only, `restriction_or_authority_ref` is `null`-only); the instruction schema has no restriction field, so such an instruction is refused as invalid input.
- **No Flow / Delta (§21) and no Claim / Obligation (§11).** Neither is mandatory for this nonlegal movement and neither was created.
