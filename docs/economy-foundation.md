# Economy Demand → Production Request → Realized Access Backbone Foundation

Sixth bounded slice, built on the Campaign Continuity Foundation (`docs/campaign-package.md`), the Canonical World Identity & Owner Foundation (`docs/canonical-world-foundation.md`), the Turnless Temporal & Resolution Transaction Foundation (`docs/temporal-foundation.md`), the Population & Cohort Reconciliation Backbone Foundation (`docs/population-foundation.md`) and the Production & Resource Conservation Backbone Foundation (`docs/production-foundation.md`). The canonical campaign can now **carry one material demand on an already-committed Population carrier, have Economy fix that demand and issue exactly one Production Request, let the existing Production machinery lawfully make the output, let the existing World-State custodian accept the physical result, and only then let Economy record realized access — and, after save, close and load, continue across the unresolved request without duplicating the demand, the request, the Production operation, the material consumption, the output, the custodian's acceptance or Economy's result.**

Governing lock: `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` (F-027 v1.0). Sources: WS-004 Economy / Exchange / Demand (Working Operational Draft v1.1; lock pin `2026-09-24T11:45:02.825Z`) §§2.1, 2.2, 3, 5.1–5.3, 8, 11, 20, 21, 27.1, 27.2, 27.13, 27.14, 33; WS-003 §13A Production Request (the carrier Production already consumes); F-017 v0.3 (conformance pin `2026-09-25T23:42:27.802Z`), the implicated Economy↔Production interface only. **CAL-01 (APPROXIMATE-PARTITION) remains UNCALIBRATED and is not used: every proof is exact. CAL-02 / L-50 remains OPEN: no labor or competence path exists. CAL-03 adopts no number.** Every other F-027 open gate is unchanged.

This is a **foundation proof**, not an economy simulator and not a Logistics slice. All fixture data is synthetic (`packages/testkit/src/economy-fixtures.ts`); goods, classes, quantities, units, carriers and the T0/TC coordinates are test evidence, not IMMORTALIS canon, gameplay defaults or calibration. Unlike the predecessor slices there is **no synthetic requester**: the request's source is Economy itself, and Economy, Production, Population and the World-State custodian are all real owners.

## One canon: how it reuses IM-02 / IM-03 / IM-11 / IM-12 / IM-17

Nothing new is persisted outside the existing campaign package. Economy facts are ordinary `OwnerFact`s written through `store.commit`, so the existing validation, expected-version checks, RC-16 receipts, idempotent replay, unknown-outcome reconciliation, hash-chained journal and package/load integrity are the ones in force. **The package format is unchanged (still format version 1); there is no second journal, save, sidecar, ledger, inventory or recovery identity, and no Economy tick, price refresh, market pass or global scan.** The temporal coordinator was not changed (a conformance test asserts it never names Economy or Production); Economy's stage resolves through the one `advance`. The only edits to predecessor engine code are additive: Production's declaration adds the Economy stage for an Economy-sourced operation, and Production's start admission accepts the Economy-registered request kind (and no other registered kind) as a request source.

## Demand → Request: demand cannot create supply

A demand is issued in **one group** that writes the Demand Envelope and its one Production Request, create-only. Admission re-derives it:

- the demand, request, keys and identities are those derived from the demand identity alone (the Production operation identity _is_ the request identity), so a demand fixes at most one request and a retry reaches the same identities;
- the request is **exactly** what the demand allocates (the demanded good and quantity at the demand's locality, sourced by Economy, no priority);
- the carrier is an **already-committed Population carrier**: a Unit of the pinned ledger, or a Cohort that Population has composed with exact, non-empty membership; the ledger (and Cohort) is read at its current version, depended upon and pinned. Economy records only the identity and the version; the demand carries no count, no membership and no Population schema, and Population's facts are byte-identical afterwards;
- the demand is carried at its carrier's own locality;
- **pre-commit availability** (WS-004 §27.2) is read AS-OF-NOW at the canonical temporal basis and sealed with the demand: no supply existed for this request when it was committed. Realized output can never feed it back: the demand is sealed, and load verification rejects a physical result that was not committed strictly after the demand it answers.

Issuing a demand creates no Production state, no entity, no claim, no temporal object and no boundary. Purchasing capability, preference and entitlement have no field to live in. Starting the Production operation on the request is Production's own publication, through its existing start path: Production pins the Economy request (a foreign fact, referenced and never copied) and applies its own RC-06 claim. A request Production cannot support with unclaimed Reserve quantity receives no credit and no operation, and Economy records no result for it.

## Completion is not satisfaction: the third stage

One Economy-sourced operation is still **one temporal object with one earned boundary** (its explicitly declared completion boundary), now with **three** declared stages in the order WS-004 §27.14 names: Production's completion, the World-State custodian's acceptance of the same conserved reference (CG-2), then **Economy's realized-access record**. The third stage reads — AS-OF-NOW, as a dependent of the acceptance — the demand, the request, the operation, the completion and the custodian's accepted entity. Reading the entity is what makes acceptance a precondition: if the custodian is unavailable the stage is `PREDECESSOR_BLOCKED`, the boundary stays active, the completion stays completed-but-unavailable and the demand stays open, across save, close and load; the continuation **adopts** the committed stages and the realization is recorded once.

Realized access is re-derived (`deriveRealization`, the single statement of the rule, used by the owner operation, admission and load verification) and is refused unless: the request is the one the demand allocated; the operation is the one that request fixed and is completed; the entity is exactly what the custodian's acceptance derives from that completion (the same conserved reference, accepted once); and the entity is exactly the demanded good and quantity at the demanded locality. It is recorded at the operation's own boundary instant, never a requested horizon. Like Production's completion and the custodian's acceptance, it is lawful **only as its IM-02 stage** (the group and intent identity derived from its StageKey, caused by its transaction; `stageIdentityViolation`, the one existing rule): a raw publication of the identical group under any other identity is refused. Economy cannot rewrite the demand, the request, the pre-commit availability or the Production choice: they are sealed, and the realization only _pins_ them.

## Judgment calls and open residuals (none resolves an open gate)

- **Third stage, not a second object.** The sources fix the staged order (§27.14: realized result → Economy access update) but no object vocabulary. Making Economy's record one more declared stage of the operation's existing object keeps one boundary, one causal process and one transaction; a separate Economy object would have needed its own boundary, which the sources do not give it. The cost is that Production's declaration now includes Economy's stage for an Economy-sourced operation (a pure function of the operation's request owner).
- **Request carrier.** WS-003 §13A has the request's source own it. Economy holds it in its own key space as a registered kind; Production's start admission accepts that one registered kind in addition to an unregistered source's own fact.
- **Pre-commit availability encoding.** WS-004 §27.2 names the read but no vocabulary. It is encoded as the AS-OF-NOW read at the pinned canonical temporal state with the sealed statement that no supply existed for this request; the check is by identity (the one conserved output reference of the request), never a scan of supply.
- **Exact satisfaction only.** A demand is satisfied by the one accepted result of its one request, exactly. Partial satisfaction, substitution, surplus, shortage conclusions, unmet-demand records and adequacy conclusions (WS-004 §§7, 14) are **not implemented**: an unmet demand is simply an open demand with no realization.
- **Carrier.** A Cohort carries a demand only when Population has composed it with exact, non-empty membership; an uncomposed or unquantified Cohort fails closed. No additive Cohort arithmetic is performed, so the overlap/partition rules (§5.3) are not exercised. The sources do not freeze whether one carrier may carry several demands for the same good (§5.4: "duplicate representations of the same requirement do not accumulate"); **no uniqueness-per-carrier is invented** (it would need a scan or an unfrozen identity rule), each demand has its own request and its own conserved output, and the proof's fixtures never rely on it either way.
- **Pins are historical.** A demand pins the carrier fact version it resolved against; Population keeps advancing, and load verification accepts a pin at or before the current version. A Unit that later exits is a historical reference, not a violation.
- **Quantities are exact non-negative integers** in a caller-named unit (Production's technical encoding of "exact"); the demanded quantity is the request's and the operation's own declared output.
- **Stage-identity residual** (inherited, stated plainly): the identity is derived from public inputs, so a caller that deliberately reproduces the coordinator's stage identity and a transaction cause is indistinguishable from the coordinator committing that stage — the same trusted archivist authority that can write kernel groups raw. The repair closes arbitrary raw publication, not deliberate impersonation of IM-02; no new durable record was introduced to do more.
- **Basis/secrecy.** Economy-written facts inherit the authored demand's labels, never defaulted.
- **Cost.** The advance path reads the engaged chain by identity: the demand, request, operation, completion, accepted entity and the world/locus. `verifyEconomyIntegrity` (like the other verifiers) lists all facts once per open — a diagnostic path (CAL-06), not the advance path.
