## Why it exists

The earlier observation slice (`docs/active-pc-direct-observation-foundation.md`) lets the original PC look at the _Locus description_: prose a person authored into the World Profile and Locus. The First Playable gap this closes is the next one: a **genuinely world-owned, local physical cause** (a Source Condition committed by World-State before any character existed) that reaches the PC through a properly qualified F-014D perception route. It is a necessary predecessor for informed PC choices and later independent NPC reactions; it is not those things.

## The path

1. **Source establishment (upstream, unchanged).** `establish-locality` has World-State commit the world, the locus and the Source Condition; `author-character` and `establish-character-locus` bind the character and its ORIGINAL custody at that locus; Temporal establishes canonical time. The tests read the committed fact back (owner, facet, version 1, committing group) before any look.
2. **Selection and confirmation.** The operator names the candidate. The choice shown for typed confirmation names the candidate and says it is not what the character sees. Nothing is asked of the host if the source is refused.
3. **The scoped question** (nothing written): built only from committed facts at the current canonical basis; adds the access facts of the one source.
4. **The reading.** The engine validates the answer against the delivered words and the withheld set and **derives** the verdict. A reading is retained create-only as sealed evidence whatever it derives.
5. **The receipt** (only `OBSERVATION_ESTABLISHED`): the Epistemic owner's event, pinning the Source Condition; then durable transaction authority, the packet and sealed provenance, and the sealed transaction — the unchanged event-triggered formation at the unchanged basis.
6. **The read.** The existing guarded Glance returns the existing presentation, or the one opaque refusal.

## What the host is given, and what it never is

The host receives, of the source, **only its owner-authored access facts**, as premises like any other fact. It is never given the quantity, the recoverable amount, the material class, the dimension, the key, the deliveries, the exhaustion state, or the **establishment statement**.

The establishment statement is withheld on purpose: it is the owner's quantity assertion (the shipped example is "The first pan holds exactly 120 sacks of raw salt."), not a descriptive premise, and the brief's "proper minimum committed descriptive/access source premises" is the access facts. A source whose access facts are empty has nothing descriptive to be seen by and is refused rather than delivered a premise that does not exist.

The source's engine reference is redacted from the delivered document, so the host cannot echo it; the engine nevertheless refuses it, and the other withheld values, if it is produced by any other means.

## Validation (the unchanged rule plus two)

Everything `deriveObservation` checked is unchanged: every premise exists in the delivered context and is quoted verbatim; premise kinds are fixed (faculty only on the character's own statements; position on the confirmed placement statement and world facts; path and appearance on world facts — a source access fact is a world fact); every delivered fact, the source's included, is assessed exactly once; an appearance exists only when faculty, position and path are all affirmatively established; the verdict is derived, never read. A source access fact can never establish the character's faculty or position.

Added: (1) **scope** — in a scoped question the appearance is of that source (`source.` fact), never of a neighbouring fact; (2) **withheld** — the appearance carries no engine reference and none of the withheld values: the source key and material class as a whole word or phrase however its separators and case are spelled (`raw-salt` ⇒ `Raw salt`), and each exact quantity as a whole number (`120` but not `1200`).

Both are enforced at the host-facing pre-check (scope only), at the adapter, at IM-11 admission of the retained reading and at load verification, from the same function. A reading whose pinned source has since been advanced by its owner is verified as history: its scope is still checked from the record alone, and its content is never re-derived from later canon.

## One observation per character per canonical instant

The observation identity is `(character, canonical instant)` and takes no source. A scoped and an ordinary reading of the same instant therefore collide, exactly as two readings always did: the first earned event holds the instant; a different reading is refused by the Epistemic owner (`formObservedPacket`) and never replaces or re-earns it. The operator command replays only **its own scope**: an observation of another scope (a different source, or none) holds the basis, is reported as unavailable with nothing changed, and is never shown as the requested look.

## Fixed-shape read

The Glance gateway reads six premise slots for **every** observation. The sixth is the Source Condition when the retained decision cites one and the sentinel `-` (which can name no committed fact) when it cites five. Both forms, and every concealed state of either, read the same ten identities in the same order, then the unchanged projection's own eight. The instrumented tests compare the ordered traces across none, obstructed, committed-but-unsealed, current, superseded, historical, tampered (basis, retargeted source, moved version), a hidden source, a wrong client, hidden decision/custody/event/packet/state and malformed or over-claiming requests; and rebuild a gateway that reads the sixth premise only when cited to show the instrument fails it. This is a deterministic bounded shape, **not** a wall-clock constant-time claim.

## Fail closed

An absent, malformed (not an `EntityId`), foreign-owned, mislocated (another locus of the same world), exhausted or undescribed source is refused with the single message "no source is available to this observation", before the host is asked, writing nothing. No source is discovered, enumerated or searched: one identity is derived and read. An over-claiming host request (a source, a key, an event, a basis, a recipient) is the existing strict refusal.

An **exhausted** source is refused. The sources do not say whether a depleted source is still seen, and the exhausted Source Condition's access facts are stale descriptions of what was; refusing is the conservative reading and is flagged below as a judgment call, not a rule.

## Recovery

Tests lose the reply of, and kill the process around, the retained reading, the event, the transaction authority, the packet and the sealed transaction, for a scoped observation; the same command resumes without re-asking, re-forming or re-confirming what it holds and lands on exactly the campaign an uninterrupted run produces. A **real** later change of the source — a Production request, basis, extraction and the owners' own Source Delta, World-State delivery and Reserve inflow, never a hand edit — advances the Source Condition to a later version: the earlier event (which pins version 1), its packet and its provenance are byte-identical and verify clean, the Glance is the one refusal once the basis has moved, a new look at the later basis is its own observation pinning the new version (and withholds the new net amount too), and a source the delivery exhausted is refused with the message of an absent one.

## Judgment calls and residuals (none resolves an open gate)

- **Establishment statement not delivered.** See above; a different reading of "minimum" (delivering it) would put an exact quantity in the host's document in the shipped example. BLUE may rule otherwise.
- **Exhausted source refused**, and a source with no access facts refused. Both are fail-closed, not guessed; neither is a new game rule.
- **Mechanical, not semantic, withholding.** The engine cannot see that a text _means_ the hidden thing. That an appearance is raw (no identification, ownership, tonnage) is instructed and is the host's; only identifiers and exact quantities are refused mechanically. An access fact that itself names the material or states the quantity makes those words un-sayable in the appearance (a whole-word match is refused): author access facts as _appearance_.
- **Operator-side only.** The host-request route (`immortalis-observe-request-answer`) and the MCP surfaces are unchanged and unscoped; no chat, phone or host-callable route can name a source.
- **No shipped requester.** Production's requester is an unregistered extension owner, so a campaign that has drawn from its source can be opened only with that extension; the drawdown tests therefore act engine-direct after the draw.
- **One locus, one source per setup** remains the scope of `establish-locality`; further sources in tests are committed through World-State's own operation.
- **One scripted scene** (an alcove, a bench, a pale crust). No optical algorithm, perception radius, light model or identification rule exists or was added.
