## 5. Findings (all non-gating; all precision or disclosure)

### RF-16 — S4.03 H02, A0 cell: "G no license to invent PC commitments"

- **Issue.** S4.03 defines G as "a specified missing producer/contract". The cell can be read two ways and is wrong on either. As a protective limit (the GM must not invent PC commitments), it is not a missing producer; the same limit is written as a plain property elsewhere (A: "no guide may fabricate consent"; MX: "wrong grade refuses"; D in S4.04: "guard refusal on invented consent") and carries no G. As a gap (A0 has no mechanism licensing the GM to supply routine premises), it duplicates the sparse-source-readiness G already in H01 and is not what "invent PC commitments" says.
- **Effect.** It adds an apparent A0-only gap in the one row about PC agency, where the packet's agency boundary (S4.03 "Agency and authority interpretation") is meant to apply identically to all six columns.
- **Requested delta.** Re-express as a retained limit with its tier (for example "R: no mechanism by which the GM manufactures PC commitments; U original wording at host"), and state any routine-premise producer gap once, under H01.

### RF-17 — S4.03 H01, B cell: K-4 treated unlike the other five columns

- **Issue.** Five columns carry a U for actual GM behaviour (A0 "U genuine-host GM play"; A "U genuine-host K-4"; C, D and MX "U actual GM behavior"). B's cell does not, and it alone states a failure condition: "a reactive form relay would fail K-4".
- **Effect.** A shared host- and behaviour-dependent U becomes a one-sided conditional failure for B, and B's K-4 status reads as resolved apart from that. A guidance-and-retrieval-only arm (A) is at least as exposed to a reactive-relay outcome.
- **Requested delta.** Add "U actual GM behavior" to B. State the reactive-relay failure condition once, in the eligibility disposition, as applying to every arm, or give each arm its own K-4 falsifier.

### RF-18 — Grant and continuation regime is specified for B and MX only (S4.03 H05; S4.04 S06)

- **Source basis.** A0's resume predicate compares amount, until and dispatch reference and does not consult client or grant (`apps/phone/src/goal.ts` 283–291). - **Issue.** S4.04 S06 names the A0 regime and states retention for B and MX, but the C and D cells are silent, and the U lists in H05 for C ("interruption/reopen across barrier and owner stages") and D ("runtime/fsync/fault/reopen qualification") omit it. C's reuse list (S3.03 C item 7) names core, persistence, OAuth and session validators but not the goal coordinator, so C's retention of A0's regime is implied, not stated.
- **Effect.** The matrix can read as parity between a specified B and unspecified C and D on idempotent retry, one of the two A0 qualifications the study has carried since the first handoff (ordinary gather adoption).
- **Requested delta.** Add to the C and D cells of S06, and to the H05 U lists, that operation-specific grant and continuation authority is DESIGNED and unreviewed (S3.05 R04; S3.10), with D's replacement and falsifier named or kept as a named U.
