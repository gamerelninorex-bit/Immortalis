## What the measurements say

- **Bootstrap-bearing cold and warm open, equal journal length:** the bootstrap records cost a small fixed amount that vanishes into the journal's own replay cost as history grows. At 11 entries the cold open is 157 ms against 92 ms (about 1.7×; the 8 records are a large share of a tiny journal; 6.7 kB more is read), at 111 entries 847 ms against 734 ms (+15%), and at 511 and 2,011 entries the two are within run-to-run noise (6.16 s against 6.06 s; 18.7 s against 20.4 s). Groups historically verified are identical in every cell; warm reopen, the ordinary commit and peak RSS are within noise at every size. The extra bytes read are 6.7 kB at 11 entries and about 33 kB at 511 and 2,011 entries.
- **Open after a torn journal tail (a crash mid-append):** the tail (43 bytes) is reported as residue and never adopted, for both campaigns; the open cost equals the ordinary cold open of the same journal (2,011 entries: 19.5 s bootstrap-bearing against 19.2 s), so recovery of a torn tail adds nothing measurable and bootstrap records do not change it.
- **Bootstrap crash recovery:** restarting the service over a SCOPE send window left open by a crash and resuming it takes about 31 ms (27–36 ms, about 80 ms CPU, 403 bytes written, peak RSS about 158 MB at a 5-entry journal) and calls the driver **zero** times in all five samples: the reserved invocation is recovered or parked, never regenerated. This is the decision path only (outbox and canonical scan); the end-to-end resume to a committed act is exercised functionally by the witness (stage D, interruption) rather than timed here.
- **Not measured, stated plainly:** recovery at a long history (the reserved-window fixture is a 5-entry journal because the bootstrap needs a fresh locality; the torn-tail row covers long-history open recovery); crash recovery inside a conserved collection (functional witness only); genuine model, host or client latency (T2/T3, UNRUN); retained live storage; H-07 (skipped in CI).
- **OB-030 stays a separate pre-live-open debt.** The first forced replay of an OLD journal (about 631 s and about 859 MB at 2,003 entries on this machine) is unchanged by this measurement and remains the separately reviewed persistence mitigation required before any live campaign is opened; no persistence redesign is added here.
