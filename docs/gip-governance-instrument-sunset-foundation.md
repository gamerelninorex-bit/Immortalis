# GIP Governance Instrument Sunset Foundation

GIP sunset bounded slice, built on the GIP Governance Instrument Core Backbone Foundation (`docs/gip-governance-instrument-foundation.md`), the Turnless Temporal & Resolution Transaction Foundation (`docs/temporal-foundation.md`) and the NULL-PC OOC advance gateway (`docs/protocol-null-pc-ooc-advance-foundation.md`). **A GIP-authored, already committed, `active` Governance Instrument that carries an exact `sunset_condition` becomes `lifecycle_status = expired` only after the Temporal kernel's earned later boundary is reached and GIP's own authorized lifecycle stage commits.** Its exact `instrument_id`, source office, Scope Binding, original `operative_effect`, `validity_by_order[]` and provenance are preserved; a later exact-key read reports `expired`; and the outcome survives save/close/load, replay and an interruption at every durable step. It does **not** prove that a proposal was lawfully enacted, adjudicate disputed legal validity, or infer notice, applicability, enforcement or knowledge.

Governing source (exact pins live in `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` and the owner registry): **WS-007** — the Sovereign-ratified GIP Working Operational Draft v2.1 (frozen F-027 pin `2026-09-24T01:42:24.952Z`) §2.1, §2.2, §4.1, §4.2, §4.3 (`sunset_condition`), §21.1/§21.2 with Concept Amendment v2.1 A7 and the GIP Scaling Profile v1.1; **F-014B** Turnless Temporal Resolution Kernel (lock pin `2026-09-25T23:42:29.547Z`) and **F-017** v0.3 stage identity. Hard-preflight selection and scope: the sunset-slice implementation brief (Notion `3f420af9-01e0-819b-8b7b-dc6c0b9bb2a`... pin `2026-10-09T09:13:08.950Z`). This is one bounded construction, **not** a design amendment, a general institutional decision/enactment engine, a release or a PF-6/F-028 activation.

**Not implemented (out of scope, unchanged):** institutional decision procedure, proposal passage or enactment; discretionary legal choice and contested governing-order adjudication; renewal, repeal, supersession and every other lifecycle transition; every WS-007 §4.3 conditional field except `predecessor_instrument_ref` and `sunset_condition`; any contingent, subjective, externally evidenced, disputed or uncomputable sunset condition; calendars, durations and natural-language dates; applicability resolution, promulgation, dissemination, awareness, Administrative Reach, implementation, enforcement and compliance; actor cognition; any scheduler, tick, phase, polling loop, population scan or universal Instrument scan; any RNG (GitHub Issue #11 is **still open and untouched**); provider adoption, deployment, migration, calibration and PF-6/F-028 (**unchanged and blocked**). No IA/V row is promoted, no register row gains full evidence and the frozen F-027 artifacts (spec lock, Runtime Contract, acceptance register, open gates) are byte-for-byte unchanged.

## The enrollment and recovery protocol (cross-owner, crash-safe)

GIP's publication and the Temporal kernel's boundary registration are **distinct owners' commits**. The protocol keeps a future material sunset from being lost, and from being crossed, at every interruption point:

1. **GIP publishes** (one create-only group): the Instrument (`active`, exact `sunset_condition`) and GIP's enrollment receipt. From this commit the sunset is a **durable, known-unregistered material term**.
2. **The kernel registers** the one object `gipSunsetTemporalObject` derives from the committed Instrument and receipt (identities derived from the Instrument alone, RC-16). Registration executes nothing, advances no time and expires nothing; the object's resolved basis is the receipt's `basis_time`, so a registration-time read cannot expire the original creation stage.
3. **A lawfully player-authorized advance** crossing the boundary engages that exact indexed object (no scan). The kernel opens the transaction's durable authority, GIP's stage commits `expired` under it, then the kernel group records the transaction, retires the boundary and moves time.

## Source-fidelity register

- `sunset_condition` is the WS-007 §4.3 **conditional** field under its exact name. The source lists no wire shape for it; the encoding is the one exactly timed, unambiguous subset representable in the campaign's existing canonical time unit - a bare `CanonicalTime`. It is not `expires_at`, not a `renewal_rule`, not an `activation_condition`, and nothing is parsed, defaulted or derived from it.
- The §4.1 mandatory fields, the singular scalar `operative_effect` and `validity_by_order[]` (independently authored entries of `governing_order_ref` and `validity_status`) are unchanged, never overwritten or deduplicated by a sunset (Amendment A7: validity and lifecycle are separate dimensions). The status enumerations are the source's own; the proven branch is exactly `active → expired`.
- Same `instrument_id` = same `gip/instrument/<instrument_id>` slot across every stage, single owner/facet GIP, no per-recipient/order/scope copy. The Temporal fact, object and boundary index belong solely to the Temporal kernel; the stage identity is (causal process, owner operation, prospective result) with no horizon, source version or retry.
- Any order-relative current-force conclusion is limited to the authored `validity_by_order[]`; no universal validity, applicability, enforcement, notice or knowledge is inferred.

## Interpretations and engineering choices recorded for review

1. **The receipt and the pending guard are this slice's engineering method for the cross-owner crash boundary** (brief §5: "the builder may select the technical method; any new substantive ordering/authorization rule must be escalated"). The ordering is the brief's own (GIP publishes, then the kernel registers after verifying the committed record). The method adds no scheduler and no scan: a **single fixed-key** receipt, written atomically with the Instrument, records the only thing the kernel cannot otherwise know - that a committed sunset is not yet registered - and the kernel refuses to take canonical time to it until it is. Alternatives rejected: Temporal-first registration (contradicts the brief's source order and leaves a registered object with no Instrument, which can become an unresolvable held boundary); a universal Instrument scan or a guest-supplied `due` flag (both forbidden).
2. **At most one enrollment is in flight.** The receipt slot is created, or advanced from a receipt whose object is already registered; a second sunset-bearing Instrument waits until the first is registered (recovery is one call). This bounds the pending set to one without a list; earlier receipts stay in the journal.
3. **`FactKind.laterLegacyRules`** is one small registry field (one entry, the Instrument kind) because the Instrument kind now has two genuine superseded citations; `legacyRule` was a single slot. It is bounded exactly like `legacyRule` (whole citation, value class it governed) and is not a general framework.
4. **A stage may run for a transaction that opened at the sunset** (a held continuation of an unresolved boundary resumes at it); one that opened after it cannot exist, because canonical time never passes an active boundary.
5. **The gateway.** Only the NULL-PC OOC advance gateway registers `gipSunsetExpiryProvider`. The active-PC transfer gateways keep their pinned provider sets, so a sunset due inside one of those advances is held (`PROVIDER_ABSENT`) and completed by a provider-equipped continuation; it is never skipped.
6. **Residual, stated plainly.** Between a stage's commit and the kernel's close the Instrument reads `expired` while canonical time and the boundary have not yet moved - the existing IM-02 stage-before-kernel-commit window every owner has; the same transaction re-presented adopts the stage. A trusted archivist can still write a lawful raw transaction by hand.
