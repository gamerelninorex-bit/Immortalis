# GM-led natural first play

This page records what the 30 October 2026 bounded construction release adds to the supported chat loop, how to operate it, which parts are
evidenced only by mechanism tests, and what is **not** built. The authority is the BLUE brief _GM-led Natural First-Play Rebuild_ and the
Operating Charter's standing requirement _GM-led natural chat play_ (see `IMPLEMENTATION_CHARTER.md`). Nothing here is a full-game claim.

**31 October R-1 repair:** BLUE's same-PR direction [6538562410](https://github.com/gamerelninorex-bit/Immortalis/pull/94#issuecomment-6538562410)
governs this delta under the [ratified active-PC current-look section](https://app.notion.com/p/3f320af901e08166ad96ca0fc5e5069d), native
`2026-10-31T03:34:15.074Z`, clauses 4, 6 and 8. GM explanatory prose no longer establishes viewing position. A placement-only seed that states
no required viewing circumstances now stays **UNRESOLVED**, including after a supported ordinary-look feasibility ruling. The positive
mechanism fixtures explicitly supply independent lawful placement evidence. This is a repair of evidence handling, not natural-play acceptance.

## What a player can now do in one request

| Request                                           | Tool                                                   | What the engine does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "gather three"                                    | `gather` `{ amount, until: "start" \| "complete" }`    | A **trusted coordinator** carries the original request through the owners' existing chain (commit, reach reading, Production admission, completion). It is not a world owner. It retains its own record before each dispatch, adopts an open attempt of the same quantity instead of duplicating it, returns control at a material boundary, and resumes the **same** goal after an interruption. |
| "where are we with that?"                         | `play_status`                                          | Owner-backed account of the current goal and the character's gathering (`NO_ATTEMPT`, `COMMITTED`, `NOT_ADMITTED`, `IN_FLIGHT`, `COMPLETED`, `STOPPED`, `HELD`). Read from the owners, never from the goal record alone. Shows no source or Reserve quantity.                                                                                                                                     |
| "surprise me" (the one missing handling duration) | `delegate_handling_duration` `{ shortest?, longest? }` | The player (or a seed that leaves routine detail to the Game Master) hands the choice of that single duration over. **No number is required.** `role.gm` chooses once; the owner validates and seals it as `PLAYER-DELEGATED`. Numeric limits the player states are the only constraints an owner can verify.                                                                                     |
| "look at the logs"                                | `observe_surroundings` `{ attention? }`                | The player's own words (at most 240 characters) go to the Game Master as the request. No posture, direction, lighting or eye question is put to the player.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

`start` stops at Production admission and passes no time. `complete` carries time only to **that act's own** earned completion. A `gather`
for another quantity while a different attempt is open returns `ANOTHER_ATTEMPT_OPEN`; it never replaces, widens or finishes that attempt.
The older primitive tools (`collect`, `continue_collection`, `finish_collection`, …) keep their meaning; `continue_collection` also performs the
first reach reading and admission.

`route_status` / readiness payloads carry `report_scope: "SETUP_READINESS_ONLY"`. They report setup readiness, not whether play has begun
(they replace a constant `campaign_started: false` that could be misread as a runtime probe). A terminal `opening` request receipt is marked
`historical` and no longer overrides the next step.

## The scene step of a look

A look is one request. When the deployment has a host bound to the `GM_SCENE_RESOLUTION` operation the engine asks it, before the perception
question, for the **current viewing**, as two questions that are never confused:

- the player's **words**, if any, are _classified_ (fresh for any new words): an ordinary look (routine attention, turning and opening the eyes —
  I-001 §8), or a materially new commitment (climbing, walking, taking), which is returned to the player as `OBSERVATION_NEEDS_CHOICE` and
  never ruled on;
- the **physical circumstance** is the unchanged basis's one ruling, asked of the **basis alone** (no wording reaches it) and retained whatever its
  verdict. A supported ruling must quote at least one of the character's accepted statements (the I-002 baseline); the confirmed placement and world facts can complement or veto it but never replace it, and nothing rests on the request. A negative, conflict or
  unresolved result is sealed evidence only (no synthetic negative fact is published) and **cannot be reopened or promoted by rewording**: only a
  changed canonical basis, or a changed pinned input, is a new physical task.

The engine **derives** the verdict from the host's answer (verbatim anchoring to delivered text, the kind of premise each claim may rest on).
Only `VIEWING_SUPPORTED` is published, and only by the World-State physical custodian, from one retained ruling named by task identity
(`world_pc_current_viewing`; sparse, create-only, sealed). Every other ruling stays sealed referee evidence. The direct-perception question then
**consumes** the published circumstance, so a changed input is a new task: an earlier negative decision stays byte-identical, a retained ruling is
never asked again, and same-input retries cannot reroll. The circumstance is not an I-002 intrinsic branch, a Role Registry binding, a Production
result or a receipt. The player sees only what the earned Glance returns.

The published premise reaches direct perception as canonical JSON containing only the typed `SUPPORTED` state, canonical instant,
five pinned versions and ruling/custodian provenance. The stored `circumstance.statement` remains byte-for-byte explanatory journal text;
it is not delivered or accepted as sensory evidence. `CURRENT_VIEWING` remains a readable premise-kind identifier but cannot establish
faculty, position, path or payload. Position requires independently quoted affirmative placement or specific lawful physical-owner facts
establishing the actual viewing circumstances. Shared locus, intent, feasibility and intrinsic faculty do not establish facing, open eyes,
sightline or visibility. The host must answer `NOT_ESTABLISHED` when those circumstances lack support. The engine enforces quotation and
premise-kind separation; it does not mechanically understand whether a host's interpretation of lawful placement/world prose is correct.
Independent position evidence does not have to cite the feasibility premise. No facing owner, Health writer or broader GM permission is added.
The current context has no separately identified physical-owner record of this PC's sensory position. Its general world/locus/source facts
may complement the independently cited confirmed placement, but cannot substitute for that PC-specific evidence (clear air alone remains
rejected). A future standalone specific-physical-owner carrier would require separately scoped work; none is invented here.

R-1 mechanism regressions in `tests/integration/current-look-premise.test.ts` retain the exact phrase “facing the woodpile, eyes open” in the
GM explanation while a bare placement yields `position NOT_ESTABLISHED`, no receipt, and the same unresolved perception after paraphrases and
reopen. They reject both feasibility-as-position and relabelling that premise as a world fact. Blind and unresolved-sight PC baselines receive
no visual receipt even with a favourable scene script. The positive in `gm-scene`/`chat-scene` uses independent affirmative placement with
sensory/path prerequisites. Context construction makes the identical reads with 0 and 40 unrelated synthetic retained facts; the fixed Glance
reader and its read-count tests are unchanged. All are **MECHANISM ONLY**; genuine host/client evidence remains UNRUN.

**Health limitation (ratified clause 3):** the scene and direct-perception contexts deliver accepted PC statements and the existing physical
premises, not Health-owned impairment/process facts. PC-declared absent or unresolved sight is exercised fail closed by the scripted host.
This does not demonstrate handling of a later Health impairment or pending Health process, nor prevent a semantically dishonest host from
misreading a PC quotation. Clause 3 is **not fully enforced or accepted** by this repair. Adding a Health reader/writer or new owner design is
outside this scope and remains held for BLUE.

## A delegated handling cycle (I-002 §9; CPAA-C1 v0.3 F-2 "lawfully player-authored **or delegated**")

The R-5 route accepts the player's **own** words for the one missing handling duration. Where the player instead says "surprise me", the choice
is delegated, and the engine does what I-002 §9 requires rather than putting the Game Master's wording into the player's mouth.

1. **Scope.** The player delegates (their seed leaves routine detail to the Game Master, or they say "surprise me"). **No number is required**: with no limit
   the scope is the branch, the character's accepted baseline and a strictly positive whole number of the campaign's unit; the engine invents no rate and no
   ceiling. A numeric limit the player themselves states ("never more than five seconds") is the `longest` bound and a lower one the `shortest`; these are the
   **only** constraints a delegation carries, because they are the only ones an owner can verify against the sealed choice. The player's free words are
   **not** part of the scope: they would be certified as "within constraints" on the host's say-so (BLUE hold H1-C), so the tool refuses them, names
   `state_handling_duration` as the way to say them, and never drops them silently. A qualitative wish ("nothing frantic") is therefore not a constraint
   this record can validate (L1). The status offers the delegated path beside the player's own words and asks the player ONE choice question, not a rate form.
2. **Selection.** One exchange task (`PC_COLLECTION_DELEGATE`) is built from committed facts only: the scope, the character's accepted baseline, any
   earlier closure of the branch and the method. `role.gm` answers once: it may refuse (the duration is already stated, the branch is closed, or the
   request adds a capability) or choose a whole number inside the scope, write one statement that states that number and unit and no other number,
   and quote the baseline words that name the actuator it times. The engine validates all of it; nothing in the answer carries a role, a verdict or a time.
3. **Delegation Commit Record.** `pc_delegated_clarification` (a second kind of the same PC-owner facet; create-only; `SEALED`) carries every element §9
   names: scope/constraints (the numeric limits); candidate space and generation basis (the baseline and the task, by digest); method class `ROLE_GM_NON_RANDOM_SELECTION` and
   version `pc-cycle-gm-selection:v1`; executing role `role.gm`; `draw: null` (the method is not random); the selected content with its content **seal**;
   the validation result; the commit time (effective from the canonical time at acceptance, never earlier) and the governing package version
   (`f027` / `v1.0`, from the build's spec identity). The owner **re-derives** the validation, the task and the seal from the sealed content at admission and at
   every load; the record cannot claim it. Its claim provenance is the literal `PLAYER-DELEGATED`: there is no path that records the Game Master's words as
   `PLAYER-AUTHORED`.
4. **One slot, and never late.** The branch has one slot per character, whichever route filled it: after a delegation the player's own R-5 words are refused, and
   after R-5 a delegation is refused. A delegation is declined once **any** collection commitment of the character exists ("a delegation first offered after a
   consequence depends on it is declined and the branch stays unresolved", I-002 §9); load verification also refuses a record committed after a commitment.
5. **Character Mirror.** The command reports the scope, the method class and version, the executing role, the seal, the effective time and the governing
   package, and then the sealed statement. The readback shown with the request carries the scope and the method but **not** the chosen number, and the same question
   always finds the same retained selection, so a declined or repeated request cannot buy a second draw. This branch's content is not concealed after commit
   because the preparation must quote it verbatim; a concealed variant is not built (L1).
6. **Consumers.** The mechanism and the `role.gm` cycle attestation follow the record by exact version and key; the attestation task tells the host that the
   claim is delegated. The attestation, the exact numeral-and-unit match and the owners' timing are unchanged, and the collection is timed by exactly the sealed cycle.

Mechanism evidence only: the selection and the attestation in the tests are scripted plumbing. No real GM host has chosen or attested anything (GENUINE HOST is UNRUN).

## Operating it

- The scene host is a separate, least-privilege host. Add `gm-scene` to `IMMORTALIS_REFEREE_HOST_IDS` (its operation layout is
  `GM_SCENE_RESOLUTION` only) and bind it as for the other hosts (`docs/ai-referee-host-foundation.md`). A deployment already registered with
  `gm-setup,gm-collection` keeps loading unchanged.
- The delegated handling cycle uses its own least-privilege host, for the same reason. Add `gm-delegate` to `IMMORTALIS_REFEREE_HOST_IDS` (its operation layout
  is `PC_COLLECTION_DELEGATE` only; the operator CLI binds it with `referee-bind-delegation-host`). `gm-collection` keeps exactly its two operations, so a
  deployment already registered with `gm-setup,gm-collection` keeps starting unchanged (a test pins this). Without a host bound to the operation the status keeps
  offering only the player's own-words route and `delegate_handling_duration` is refused with a named reason, before anything is asked.
- The setting must **author** the physical statements a lawful look rests on (light, a clear path). The Game Master rules on what the world
  states; it may not invent a missing world fact. A sparse world with none yields an unresolved perception, not a fabricated one.
- Recovery: an interrupted look is read back from the owners. Decisions are found under the task identities captured before the command ran
  **and** the same attempts rebuilt now (a look that ran the scene step asked its perception question under an identity that includes the facts
  the scene step published). A pending operation blocks every other action, so the rebuilt identities are those of the same basis.

## Independent consequence (bounded)

The only independent consequence demonstrated is one an **already-lawful producer** already owns: a Governance Instrument with an exact sunset,
enrolled with the Temporal kernel, is expired by GIP's own stage when canonical time reaches the sunset. It is shown twice: at the active-PC
advance gateway (`tests/integration/collection-independent-consequence.test.ts`) and **with chat gatherings before and after it**
(`tests/integration/chat-independent-consequence.test.ts`, BLUE HOLD H3). **Scope of this evidence (MECHANISM ONLY):** the step that carries time across the
due boundary is a direct gateway call between two chat sessions; the chat tools themselves cannot cross it. An event that becomes due **inside** the ordinary
chat loop is not built and is an affected-scope STOP for BLUE and the Sovereign (L9), not a gap that was hidden.

The chat composition uses the lawful order. The operator, with the service stopped and each command under its own typed confirmation, states the
starting chronology (the campaign's **one** Temporal-owned origin) and then opens the Governance Instrument (that command requires the origin and
makes no other). Only then does the player's character exist, kept and placed through the chat tools. The chat adapter **consumes** the origin: the
starting moment is already DONE; an identical restatement replays ("already established; nobody was asked"); a different moment is refused
("established once and never reset or replaced; nothing was dispatched") and nothing changes. Then:

- One handful, ending before the sunset, is exact and leaves the Instrument untouched.
- A gathering whose interval **touches** a registered boundary — the closure rule is inclusive, so even one ending exactly at the sunset — is
  **withheld** by the Production owner ("another process falls due within the work's interval"). Nothing is debited, credited or passed; the
  player is told only that the attempt was not admitted. The independent event is neither skipped nor hidden.
- Canonical time then passes through the lawful active-PC advance (the one step the chat tools do not offer), and GIP's own stage expires the
  Instrument once, **at the sunset instant**, changing only its lifecycle status. A following chat gathering is exactly what the owners compute,
  and no player-facing surface mentions the event.
- No cause, no consequence: a sunset beyond the work's end leaves the Instrument active with its boundary intact; a hand-written expiry (with or
  without the producer's stage identity) is refused, on the chat-built campaign too.

This is **not** a generic event or NPC framework and not a non-PC actor's initiative.
