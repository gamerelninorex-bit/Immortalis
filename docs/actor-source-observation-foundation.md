# Named-Actor Source-Qualified Direct Perception Foundation

> **OOC DEVELOPMENT / SCENARIO INSTRUMENTATION.** One bounded **mechanism** that lets the trusted operator ask whether ONE already-established **non-PC Named Actor**, whom World-State holds coupled to ONE locus, directly perceived at most ONE co-located World-State **Source Condition** at the current canonical basis — and, only where a retained, engine-validated reading establishes it, has the Epistemic owner commit the existing `BOUNDED_SHARED_ACCESS` event, the actor's Accessible Content Packet and the separate Sealed Engine Provenance. It decides nothing the actor makes of it. It adds no perception rule, optics, range, NPC behaviour, goal, belief, stance, mandate, action, movement, source consumption or time.

## Why it exists

The actor recipient (`BOUNDED_SHARED_ACCESS`, `recipient_role` omitted) and the World-State coupling of an already-existing actor were both on `main` before the corridor slices. What was missing is the **producer**: nothing in canon could say that a non-PC actor ever _received_ a world-owned physical cause. Existing authored events are an upstream fixture that simply says "the actor saw X". This slice builds the smallest independently testable, source-defined, non-tautological NPC precursor on current main: the same F-014D §6 Direct Perception Adapter the Player Character already uses, for an actor, over the same owner-committed Source Condition.

## The path

1. **Upstream (unchanged).** World-State commits the world, the locus and the finite Source Condition; the Named-Actor owner commits the actor's capability basis and, when a dependent consequence needs it, the capability at its declared close; World-State commits the existing-actor coupling; Temporal establishes canonical time. The tests read each committed fact back before any look.
2. **The request and its confirmation.** The operator names actor, capability and source. The confirmation names all three and the exact versions cited, says the source is a candidate and not what the actor perceives, and says nothing is decided about what the actor makes of it. Nothing is asked of the host if the question cannot be built.
3. **The question** (nothing written): built only from the five committed facts below at the current basis.
4. **The reading.** The engine validates the answer against the delivered words and the withheld set and **derives** the verdict. A reading is retained create-only as sealed evidence whatever it derives.
5. **The receipt** (only `OBSERVATION_ESTABLISHED`): the Epistemic owner's event pinning the Source Condition; then durable transaction authority, the actor's packet and its sealed provenance, and the sealed transaction.
6. **What is shown.** The operator is shown identities (the event and packet keys), never the appearance: the packet is the actor's, and showing it would make out-of-character knowledge a receipt (F-014D §24).

## What the host is given, and what it never is

The host receives the actor's capability as one item, a fixed engine-worded coupling statement (_"The World-State owner records this actor as currently coupled to this locus of this world."_ — it states no facing, posture or open senses, because no owner has committed any for an actor), the world and locus facts, and **only the source's owner-authored access facts** as `source.access.N` premises. It never receives the source's quantity, recoverable amount, material class, dimension, key, deliveries, exhaustion state or establishment statement; the key is redacted from the delivered `refs`, and the engine nevertheless refuses it, and every other withheld value, if produced by any other means. The delivered document tells the host it decides nothing about what the actor makes of the appearance, wants, believes or does.

## Validation (the PC question's discipline with the actor's own premise kinds)

Every premise exists in the delivered context and is quoted verbatim; the **faculty** rests only on `ACTOR_CAPABILITY`; the **viewing circumstances** rest on the `COUPLING_STATEMENT` read with world facts (a shared locus alone does not establish them, and the coupling statement is required); the **path** and the **appearance** rest only on world facts; every delivered fact is assessed exactly once; an appearance exists only when faculty, position and path are all affirmatively established and is OF the one source (`source.` fact), anchored to a quotation of it; it carries no engine reference and none of the withheld values. A missing faculty, unestablished circumstances or an open fork is `OBSERVATION_UNRESOLVED`, never an obstruction; `OBSERVATION_OBSTRUCTED` needs an established blocked path from an established viewing position.

Enforced at the host-facing pre-check, the adapter, IM-11 admission of the retained reading and load verification, from the same functions. A reading whose pinned source or capability has since been advanced by its owner is verified as history, from the record alone; its content is never re-derived from later canon.

## One observation per actor per canonical instant

The event identity is `(actor, canonical instant)` in a namespace of its own (`actor-obs-…`), so it can never collide with a PC observation. A second reading of the same instant — another appearance, or another source — is retained as sealed evidence but never replaces or re-earns the observation: the Epistemic owner refuses its event and the first earned event, packet and provenance stay byte-identical. `observe-actor` replays only its own scope: an observation of another source or capability holds the basis, is reported as unavailable with nothing changed, and is never shown as the requested look.

## Fail closed

A question is refused before any host is asked, writing nothing, when: the identity is not a lawful non-PC Named Actor (a Player Character, an entity under player control, an aggregate); World-State holds no coupling of it; its capability is absent, not at its owner's declared close, another actor's or another owner's; the coupled world or locus is not committed; or the source is absent, malformed, foreign-owned, at another locus or world, exhausted or undescribed (all of those with the single message _"no source is available to this observation"_). No source is discovered, enumerated or searched: one identity is derived and read.

## Recovery

Tests lose the reply of, and kill the process around, the retained reading, the event and the packet-formation transaction; a reopened campaign finds the identical identities and payload, forms nothing twice, and lands on exactly the campaign an uninterrupted run produces. Saved packages whose reading, event, packet, provenance, source or coupling were edited do not verify.

## Composition choices BLUE should rule on (none resolves an open gate)

The brief says to STOP, not improvise, if the frozen sources cannot authorize the actor-specific referee binding without new semantics. The following are the places this slice composed existing authority rather than finding it ready-made. Each is fail-closed; none adds a game rule.

- **The actor's sense rests on its own Named-Actor capability.** The only committed actor-side state a non-PC Named Actor has today is capability basis / capability facts; there is no actor statement of a sensory faculty like a PC's I-002 statements. The faculty is therefore established only by the actor's own closed capability, **delivered as its authored identity** (e.g. `keen-sight`), and the _host_ judges, by quotation, whether that identity establishes the relevant sense. An actor with no such capability, or only an unrelated one, is `UNRESOLVED`. This is the brief's "any material actor capability at the correct declared close" read as a premise, not as a `representation` (the event's `representation` stays null: a raw appearance depends on no capability). If BLUE intends a different faculty source, this is the one place that changes.
- **The coupling statement is a fixed sentence.** World-State commits where the actor is, not how it faces or whether its senses are open. The position premise is therefore the coupling read with the world and source facts; the host may not assume a posture or direction that is not stated.
- **A basis is not a sense.** A capability whose close has not resolved is refused as a question, not treated as established.
- **No unscoped actor question.** Unlike the PC, an actor perceives a co-located owner-committed source or nothing; the actor's world and locus descriptions are not an observation target.
- **Transaction context.** The kernel's transaction context admits only the already-approved player-choice pair, so the event-triggered formation carries it as the PC's does; it confers nothing (the receipt was earned by the retained reading).
- **Exhausted and undescribed sources are refused** (as for the PC), not guessed.
- **Mechanical, not semantic, withholding.** The engine cannot see that a text _means_ the hidden thing; only identifiers and exact quantities are refused mechanically.
- **No shipped actor.** The shipped binary has no embedding's owner registrations, and a Named-Actor capability is committed only at the close a dependent consequence declares, so the command is exercised through an embedding's registrations (as the existing-actor tests are). The host-callable MCP fast-feedback for this operation checks the answer against the delivered words and names the task by its identity, because the delivered `refs` redact the source key and the task identity cannot be recomputed from them (the engine re-derives everything against the real context).

## BLUE HOLD on PR #84 (exact head `b405504`)

- **H2 (repaired).** An obstruction now needs the actor's sense ESTABLISHED as well as the position and a blocked path; a blocked path with no established sense is `OBSERVATION_UNRESOLVED` and earns no packet. Regressions: direct derivation, retained admission (a record that calls it an obstruction is refused), reopen verification.
- **H1 (NOT cured; stopped, not manufactured).** A shared locus is not an actor's viewing position, and the coupling statement is a locus-level sentence. The missing producer is an **owner-committed, actor-specific viewpoint within the locus** (where the actor is, which way it faces or its line of sight to the source) and a **sensory-faculty statement of the actor** beyond the opaque capability identity. No existing owner commits either: World-State's coupling names a locus only, `NamedActorCapabilitySchema` holds an identity and a close, and no locus sub-position, facing or line-of-sight field exists in any contract. Adding a generic world-fact quotation would not supply it, and inventing one is outside this repair. The positive path in this branch therefore proves the mechanism **only** over a scripted host that treats the coupling as establishing position, and it must not be read as a receipt a real actor could earn today. BLUE to name the authority or producer.
