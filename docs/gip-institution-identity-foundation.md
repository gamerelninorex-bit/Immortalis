# GIP Stable Institution Identity Backbone Foundation

Twelfth bounded slice, built on the Campaign Continuity Foundation (`docs/campaign-package.md`) and the Canonical World Identity & Owner Foundation (`docs/canonical-world-foundation.md`). **One explicitly established institution identity input** makes the lawful GIP owner (`gip` / `gip_institution_identity`, IM-06) commit **one stable canonical Institution Identity reference**; save → close → load → continue preserves the same identity, provenance and keying; and later lawful subsystems may **read it by exact identity without gaining GIP write authority**. In ordinary language: the engine can now remember that **an institution exists as the same institution**. It is the owner-correct root for later offices, procedures, Governance Instruments, KTI institutional development, collective interfaces and diplomacy; it is not their implementation. **It must not — and does not — make the institution a person, a faction, a decision-maker, a KTI learner, a population, an asset owner or a catch-all state bag.**

Governing source (exact pins live in `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` and the owner registry): **WS-007** — the Sovereign-ratified GIP Working Operational Draft v2.1 (frozen F-027 pin `2026-09-24T01:42:24.952Z`) §2.1, §2.2 and §3.1, with the GIP Scaling Profile v1.1 §3–§5 (Red-confirmed at pin `2026-09-25T00:59:55.526Z`), the OB-046 closed record and F-017 v0.3 in the lineage. Hard-preflight selection and scope: the twelfth-slice implementation brief (Notion `3ec20af9-01e0-81c4-92e7-e9754b66924e`, pin `2026-10-01T14:22:36.738Z`).

**Not implemented in this twelfth slice (out of scope then):** rename, split, merge, abolition, replacement, succession or any other identity lifecycle transition; Office / Authority Locus (out of scope for this slice and implemented separately by the thirteenth slice, see `docs/gip-office-authority-locus-foundation.md`; the Institution Identity contract itself is unchanged by it); Governance Instruments, scope bindings, political relations, pending decisions, administrative reach, stability; GIP procedures, votes, legitimacy, recognition; Autonomous Collective / Faction promotion; Diplomacy; membership/headcount or any universal organization registry; KTI Class-D; F-014A cognition; Health, Military, Culture, ASR, projection/NPK; any RNG, seed, roll or workaround for GitHub Issue #11 (**still open and untouched**); provider adoption, deployment, migration, calibration closure and PF-6/F-028 (**unchanged and blocked**). The acceptance register gains only truthful **partial** evidence (IA-01, IA-02, IA-03, IA-06, IA-22); no row is promoted and no open gate changes.

## Identity, keys and idempotence

- **Canonical identity.** `institution_id` is authored, explicit and causally addressed (the publication's `cause`); it is the whole identity dimension and the whole key. The label is not an identity dimension, so two institutions may share a label and a rename is not representable.
- **One slot per institution.** A retry, a duplicate construction, a raw-store write or another owner reaches the same occupied, sealed slot, so no rival writable identity can exist.
- **Retry converges, conflict fails closed.** Re-presenting the same intent (a lost reply, a restart, a crash at any durable step) is reconciled by identity (RC-16) to the original receipt. A different intent for an established institution — equal or different content — and the same intent with changed content are refused; the committed identity is never rewritten.
- **Nothing is auto-created.** There is no derivation path from another owner's fact: a Population ledger, an institutional KTI development object, a locus designation, a PC binding or any label creates no identity.
- **No scan.** Establishing or reading one identity reads and writes only its own key; only the diagnostic load-time verifier lists facts, once per open.

## Interpretation recorded for review

The frozen schema lists `predecessor_ids[]`, `successor_ids[]` ("only where material") and an optional `constitutive_source_ref`. The brief bounds establishment to a **continuing** institution and defers every lifecycle transition. `predecessor_ids[]` and `successor_ids[]` are material only to the later transitions, so they are **not fields of this record** (unrepresentable, fail closed) rather than free-form placeholders; that narrowing is unchanged, and adding them is later work with the transitions they belong to. The optional `constitutive_source_ref` was likewise not a field of the twelfth-slice record; the twentieth slice (below) adds it, and only it. The record's provenance is the committed fact's own group, cause and governing-rule citation.

## Twentieth slice: the optional `constitutive_source_ref`

Twentieth bounded slice (brief: Notion `3ed20af9-01e0-819a-bb70-c2160ab103e8`, pin `2026-10-02T05:18:30.842Z`; construction base `3b2ba3995a670f9db100f3c2db799864605ee080`). An authored initial Institution Identity may optionally carry the exact WS-007 §3.1 field **`constitutive_source_ref`** — optional, singular, opaque. Initial establishment only; the Institution Identity lifecycle, office authority, instruments, procedures and every foreign-owner state are untouched.

- **Representation.** One optional `EntityId`, the existing canonical opaque-reference representation of the GIP reference fields (`authority_source_ref`, `decision_body_ref`, `scope_ref`, …). It is carried exactly as authored: no alias, normalisation, expansion, pluralisation or derived surrogate. Omission means absence — no default is synthesized, and `null`, an explicit `undefined`, an array, an empty or malformed value and any renamed or plural alias (`constitutive_source_refs`, `constitutive_source_ids`, …) fail closed under the strict schema.
- **A reference only.** It is never dereferenced on establishment or read, need not name any existing source, has no referent-type restriction (a Governance Instrument is one lawful possible referent, not the only one, and nothing joins on it), creates no Governance Instrument, constitutive act, office, institution, authority or external fact, copies nothing from its referent, registers no dependency, and lets nothing (legality, validity, authority, continuity, succession, recognition, implementation, knowledge) be inferred from it.
- **Unchanged identity and admission.** Owner `gip`, facet and fact kind `gip_institution_identity`, key `gip/institution/<institution_id>/identity`, `SEALED`, create-only; `institution_id` is still the only identity dimension, so two establishments for one `institution_id` address the same slot whether the reference is absent, equal or different. Admission is unchanged: one write at expected version zero, no foreign read, no `depends_on`, no second commit, no Temporal object.
- **Rule and compatibility.** The current `GIP_RULE` already cites WS-007 §3.1 including the optional `constitutive_source_ref` and is byte-for-byte unchanged (its hash is pinned by a conformance test); new identities record it, and no historical governing-rule compatibility entry is added. Existing saves without the field load byte-for-byte without migration; identities carrying it round-trip exactly through save → close → load; same-intent identical retries converge (RC-16); a changed, added or removed reference on the same intent, or any different establishment at an occupied `institution_id`, fails closed.
- **Still not representable:** `predecessor_ids[]`, `successor_ids[]`, `continuity_status` other than `continuing` (renamed, split, merged, abolished, replaced), and every office, power, authority, membership, faction, instrument body, asset, resource, KTI, actor, political, military, economic, territory or property-bag field.

Evidence: `tests/contract/gip-constitutive-source-schemas.test.ts`, `tests/integration/gip-constitutive-source.test.ts`, `tests/conformance/gip-constitutive-source-conformance.test.ts` (plus the narrowed predecessor GIP guards). F-027 register: bounded partial evidence only under IA-01, IA-02, IA-03, IA-06 and IA-22; no row is promoted, IA-04 is not claimed, IA-07 stays BLOCKED, and the Runtime Contract and open-gates snapshots are unchanged.
