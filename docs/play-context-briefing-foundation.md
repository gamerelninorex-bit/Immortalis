# Player-Safe Supported-Play Briefing Foundation

One bounded, **read-only, mechanism-only** context-delivery capability for the existing ORIGINAL-world, active-PC supported-play route: a compact briefing built from disclosures that **already exist**, so that a Game Master that resumes play (or has just seen a material operation finish) does not have to read the setting, the character, the play state and the character's current awareness one after another.

**What this is not.** It is not a new owner, fact, cache, summary, registry, second save or omniscient GM view. ## The briefing (technical wire naming is free; the content is not)

```
briefing
  status                       QUALIFIED | NOT_QUALIFIED | BLOCKED | UNAVAILABLE
  authored_starting_context    authored information; NOT a current sensory, Health or rights clearance
    setting                    ESTABLISHED + the existing LocalityReadback, unchanged   (its `established` is the authored starting quantity, never the current remainder)
    character                  BOUND + the existing CharacterReadback, unchanged        (the complete accepted Mirror and the accepted presence statement)
  out_of_character             engine information for the GM; NOT automatic character knowledge
    play                       the existing PlayRead: phase, committed requested quantity, canonical time
    next                       the one applicable safe next step (as play_status reports it)
  character_awareness          what the character has actually perceived and that is still current
    current_observation        the unchanged Glance gateway Result: the EventSignalPresentation byte for byte, or its unchanged opaque refusal
  scope_notice                 fixed: limited to these disclosures; establishes no unrepresented constraint or permission; grants no action authority
```

`QUALIFIED` means only that the ORIGINAL setting and the active PC are established and verified, so this is a supported-play briefing; it says **nothing** about whether any action is prepared or permitted (setup readiness is `collection_setup_status`'s, and an owner decides every action). `NOT_QUALIFIED`: readable, not yet at that point. `BLOCKED`: an owner-verified part is present but cannot be reconciled (an operator must inspect it). `UNAVAILABLE`: it cannot be read now. The goal record (workflow plumbing) stays in `play_status`'s own fields and is not part of the briefing.

Not carried, by construction: raw world facts, remaining stock or Reserve totals, private referee tasks, hidden hazards, NPC cognition, source credentials, general journal or history, any inferred sensory verdict, any recalled or revived packet, any hidden-state reason. A component the owners cannot support is a closed state (`NOT_ESTABLISHED`, `NOT_BOUND`, `BLOCKED`, `UNAVAILABLE`) with **no** readback and no cause; its detail lives in the existing status tools.

## The path (the model supplies nothing)

1. The player-scope tool takes `{}`. Campaign, active PC, credentials, host, source pins and operation identities come from trusted configuration and canonical qualification.
2. ONE `openCharacterCampaign`: trusted operator and retained-state directory, retained setup identity, retained KEEP, and the package's own journal replay and canonical verification, under its single-writer lock. A package a command holds, an unverifiable package or an unreadable record is `UNAVAILABLE`/`BLOCKED`; nothing is repaired, adopted or written to make it read.
3. Campaign isolation: a package holding another campaign is `BLOCKED` with no part delivered.
4. The readers share one fact listing and the actual successful open verification. Disposable dependencies re-authenticate the complete basis at entry and after the last await; a changed basis invokes strict verification and the final guard withholds the result. The ordinary path has four direct full listings (shared reader, Epistemic proof, entry and final guards), 27 inside open/verification, and one complete owner-verification pass. Each reader judges exactly as it always did.
5. Only for a `QUALIFIED` campaign (setting `ESTABLISHED` and character `BOUND`) is the Glance read, by `readPlayerViewGlanceOnOpen`: operating-system owner, host secret, the one authorized view agreeing with the canonical active-PC binding (retained KEEP, release and binding proofs, residue, canonical and Epistemic integrity), then the **unchanged** gateway with its fixed read trace and its one strict request `{ operation: "read_current_observation_glance" }`. A host the operator never bound, a missing secret, a wrong secret, another character and every other failure are the gateway's one refusal. **This read binds and mints nothing** (the older `read_current_observation_glance` tool still binds a configured host lazily; the briefing does not).
6. The campaign is closed before the result returns. `play_status` takes its `play` from this same open, so its terminal response costs one open, not two.

The `readingSetting` protection the status reads have applies here too: a command that would open the same package while the briefing is being read is refused cleanly ("try again"; nothing dispatched). While a goal is being carried out the briefing is `UNAVAILABLE` without reading, exactly as `play_status` already reported its `play`. Concurrent briefing reads in one server are serialised (they would otherwise meet each other's writer lock).

## Ordinary play (what the GM does and does not do)

| Ordinary conversation                                   | LLM-facing calls                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Let's continue. What were we doing?"                   | `get_play_context({})` once on entry or resumption, instead of the four separate reads. The GM separates OOC status from what the character has perceived.                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| "I glance back at that pile." (same view already shown) | **0** additional calls to infer ordinary attention and turning and to render the same lawful content. No new fact, knowledge, elapsed time or physical success is established. A known blindness, closed eyes or obstruction still prevents a new sight claim; an unavailable or superseded packet is not revived. First or new perception stays outside this case. |
| "Gather one log into that local store."                 | `gather` then, if a reach task is raised, the existing `referee_list_tasks` → `referee_get_task` → `referee_submit_decision` → `play_status` exchange: **at least 5 calls**, plus any polling or reconciliation actually required. The terminal `play_status` already carries the briefing: no further context read.                                                |

For a lost reply, count the actual existing reconciliation and status calls; never call it failure, duplicate the action or promise a fixed total. The context read cannot release a pending-operation barrier (pinned in `chat-adapter.test.ts`).

## Engineering Acceptance

- **Player result and burden.** Play resumes through one briefing and continues in ordinary conversation without filing micro-details; the GM keeps interpretation and narration. Independent NPC and world initiative is **OUT OF SCOPE** (no proactive-GM claim is made, so no initiative event is built).
- **Reused path and complexity.** The setting, character, play and Glance readers are reused; the Glance gateway, its fixed trace and its refusal are untouched. Added machinery: one composer module, an optional shared-read parameter on three readers, one trusted on-open entry that shares the existing single gateway call site, one tool, and a serialisation of concurrent briefing reads.
- **Whole-operation cost.** See the evidence document and its dated PR101 superseding note. Retrieval still grows with the package's total facts through the common verified open and enumerations. The briefing uses one open; PR101 reduces duplicate complete owner passes from two to one and total full listings from 54 to 31, including both fresh input guards and the Epistemic proof. Wall-time improvement remains host-dependent, with no general latency claim.
- **Provisional limits.** The briefing still enumerates the package (the existing play reader's commitment search and the epistemic proof have no keyed alternative today); a keyed per-PC commitment pointer, or a list parameter on `verifyEpistemicIntegrity`, would remove it but needs an owner or engine change outside this brief. Reconsider when a measured resume latency on a retained deployment (OB-030) or a campaign history that makes the shared open dominate play justifies it. The briefing deliberately carries no fees, carried inventory, general narrative retention or private GM context (indispensable source seams remain DESIGN-UNRESOLVED); revisit through I-001/OB-030 when the briefing materially needs undisclosed GM context, unsupported durable consequences, new perception semantics or the retained-host milestone.
- **Existing conflict preserved, not solved.** `chat-tools.ts` served perception guidance and `referee/derive.ts` position-premise guards still require the qualified observation path, while current-look lowering v0.8 §3B says ordinary implied means classify a request but do not establish bodily action; A7 asks for a broader routine-inference target. This slice neither invokes that pipeline nor weakens it, encodes no eye/facing record, and does not claim it solved. A substantive repair requires its own bounded BLUE–RED–Sovereign path.
