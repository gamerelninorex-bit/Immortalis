# Economic Holding / Account Position & Reversible Economic Encumbrance Foundation

Bounded slice on the Economy Demand → Production Request → Realized Access Backbone (`docs/economy-foundation.md`) and the canonical store, owner registry and admission machinery it reuses (`docs/campaign-package.md`, `docs/canonical-world-foundation.md`). The canonical campaign can now **carry one Economy-owned, conserved Economic Holding position whose opening amount is a separately authored OOC scenario premise, partition the same position atomically into available and encumbered amounts on a distinct OOC hold instruction — Economy, not the caller, checking the actual current availability — answer a later availability read or competing hold differently, restore the encumbered amount exactly once on a separate OOC cancellation of the still-active hold, and recover all of it exactly across save, close and load and across an interruption at every durable step.**

Governing lock: `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` (F-027 v1.0, unchanged; no lock, register, gate or Runtime Contract snapshot was touched). Source: WS-004 Economy / Exchange / Demand (Working Operational Draft v1.1; lock pin `2026-09-24T11:45:02.825Z`) §2.1 (sparse Economic Holding / Account relations), §2.2 (non-ownership), §3 (persistence; conserved-holding rule), §4 (No-Double-Spend), §11 (Claim / Obligation lifecycle vocabulary), §20 (Commitment and Conservation), §27.6 / §27.8 / §27.14, §28 (retention), §30 (invariants 17, 18, 27). The implementation brief is the BLUE hard-preflight brief of 2026-10-10 (native pin `2026-10-10T00:18:50.989Z`).

This is a **mechanism proof for an OOC scenario**, not an economy. Everything in `packages/testkit/src/economy-holding-fixtures.ts` is synthetic evidence: the holder, the object, the unit and the amounts (7, 3, 4, 5, 2) are not defaults, issuance, legal title, price, rate, tax, credit or unit adoption.

| **Encumbrance**      | `economy` / `economy_state` | ADVANCING | `economy/encumbrance/{holding}/{hold}` | One economic hold against one holding: its quantity, the holding version it was admitted against, the OOC hold instruction, and `active → cancelled` with the separate OOC cancellation instruction.                                                   |

No new owner and no new facet. The Demand → Request → Realized Access kinds keep `ECONOMY_RULE` byte-for-byte; the two new kinds cite their own rule, `ECONOMY_HOLDING_RULE`, so no persisted Economy fact changes provenance. The position is **conserved by construction**: `available + encumbered + restricted = established`, `established` is never rewritten, and nothing exists that could add to or remove from it.

## The flow

1. **Opening premise** — `EconomyAdapter.establishHolding`. The caller states only the premise (holding identity, holder, object, exact amount and unit, premise identity). Economy derives the opening partition (all available) and commits it once. Admission re-derives it, so an already-encumbered, restricted, unconserved or provenance-less opening is rejected, raw or not.
2. **Hold** — `EconomyAdapter.holdEncumbrance`. The instruction names a distinct hold identity, an amount and **the holding version it saw**; it carries no balance, verdict or winner (the schema has no such field). Economy reads the holding AS-OF-NOW, refuses (`INSUFFICIENT_AVAILABLE`, nothing written) unless the amount is wholly available in the holding's own unit, and otherwise commits **one group** that re-partitions the same position and creates the one active encumbrance, under the existing expected-version protection. 7 with a hold of 3 → **4 available / 3 encumbered**; a further hold of 5 is refused; a distinct hold of up to 4 succeeds.
3. **Availability** — `EconomyAdapter.availability` is derived from the committed holding alone.
4. **Cancellation** — `EconomyAdapter.releaseEncumbrance`. A separate OOC instruction names the hold and the encumbrance version it saw. Only a **still-active** encumbrance of **that** holding is released; exactly its recorded amount returns to available, the encumbrance becomes `cancelled`, and a second cancellation, a stale one or a reused hold identity is refused. Back to **7 available / 0 encumbered**, once.

`deriveOpeningHolding` / `deriveHold` / `deriveRelease` (`engine/src/economy/holding.ts`) are the one statement of the rule; the adapter, IM-11 admission (`economyGroupCheck`) and the load-time verifier all use it, so a raw `store.commit`, a tampered group and a tampered journal or state are refused or detected by the same rule.

## Judgment calls and open residuals (none resolves an open gate)

- **Encumbrance as a separate fact, not a §11 Claim / Obligation.** The sources make a Claim / Obligation record conditional ("only when the economic right … remains future-causal and is not authoritatively owned elsewhere") and require `settlement_or_satisfaction_rule`, `authority_or_contract_ref` and a counterparty where applicable — none of which this OOC premise supplies. So no claim registry and no legal obligation is asserted; the encumbrance borrows only §11's established lifecycle words (`active`, `cancelled`).
- **"Authenticated" means the existing canonical write path.** The opening premise, the hold and the cancellation are published only by the archivist path (`role.archivist` + `permission.canon.write`, IM-11/IM-17); any other authority is `AUTHORITY_DENIED`, any other owner/facet/campaign is refused, and the schemas give a caller no field for an Economy conclusion. That is trusted OOC scenario authoring, not proof of any in-world party's will.
- **The hold instruction is not a persisted owner fact.** Its authority is the OOC scenario, which no registered owner holds; it is recorded inside the encumbrance (`authorization`) and the committed journal group. No scenario-instruction owner or key space was invented.
- **Cancelled encumbrances are retained.** They are what make a replayed or reused cancellation / hold identity refusable and the restoration exactly-once (§28: retain while future-causal). The set is bounded by the number of holds placed, not by time.
- **Simultaneous incompatible holds are serialised by the existing version protection, not arbitrated.** Two holds pinned at the same holding version: exactly one commits; the other fails closed (`VERSION_CONFLICT`) and must re-read, after which it is judged on the real remaining availability. Arrival order is **not** an allocation rule; no priority, allocator or tie-break was added. A genuine owner-supplied allocation mechanism for contested scarcity remains missing (compare the order-invariant same-instant repair the drawdown slice needed, F53-R1): the slice picks no winner by any rule other than each caller's pinned view of the version.
- **Opaque `holder_ref` / `object_ref`.** The sources assign no owner that validates a holder or an economic object in this tranche; Economy records the identities and creates neither.
- **Labels.** Hold and cancellation inherit the holding's `basis` / `secrecy`; they are never defaulted or supplied.
- **Cost.** Hold and release read two facts by identity. Only the diagnostic verifier walks facts, from the listing `verifyEconomyIntegrity` already makes once per open (CAL-06).
