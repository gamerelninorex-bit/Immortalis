# Culture & Society Established Shared Meaning / Norm Result-Close Backbone Foundation

Forty-sixth bounded slice, built on the Campaign Continuity Foundation (`docs/campaign-package.md`), the Canonical World Identity & Owner Foundation (`docs/canonical-world-foundation.md`) and the Turnless Temporal & Resolution Transaction Foundation (`docs/temporal-foundation.md`, including its durable pre-stage transaction authority). **One sealed, causally engaged Social Update Context** over **committed non-Culture owner facts** can now become **one Culture-owned, established shared meaning or norm** for one material referent × scope × context, **committed at one declared Communicative Convention Close** through the lawful Culture & Society owner (`culture` / `culture_social`, IM-09), under the existing IM-02 stage/dependency machinery, readable by identity only once that close has committed, and durable across save → close → load → continue. It proves a real owner-owned social-state outcome in the existing engine, not a JSON schema. **It is not an actor's belief, a player's moral choice, a prevalence, a policy outcome, an adoption, a relation or an aggregate position, and it must not — and does not — be inferred from contact, a policy, a packet or its refusal, a KTI transfer receipt or a Cohort count.**

Governing lock: `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` (F-027 v1.0). This is a **foundation proof**, not a social simulation. All fixture data is synthetic (`packages/testkit/src/culture-fixtures.ts`). ## One canon: how it reuses IM-02 / IM-03 / IM-11 / IM-12 / IM-17

Nothing new is persisted outside the existing campaign package. Both Culture kinds are ordinary `OwnerFact`s written through `store.commit`, so the existing validation, expected-version checks, RC-16 receipts, idempotent replay, unknown-outcome reconciliation, hash-chained journal and package/load integrity are the ones in force. **The package format is unchanged (still format version 1); there is no second journal, scheduler, roster, sidecar save path, polling loop, drift tick or per-referent/per-population refresh.** The temporal coordinator and every predecessor owner module were not changed (conformance tests assert none names the Culture owner). The only edits to predecessor engine code are additive wiring: the registry gains the two kinds and the WS-005 pin, `identityMismatch` handles them, `canonicalAdmission` dispatches the Culture owner, object registration also asks the Culture rule, and `CanonicalCampaign.open`/`verify` also run the Culture verifier.

## The lifecycle: evidence → sealed context → close → consumption

1. **Evidence (committed by another owner).** A non-Culture owner has committed facts shaped as the Culture-defined reading. In this slice the only such owner is the test-owned fixture. Culture does not form, recreate or improve evidence, and a Culture fact is never its own evidence.
2. **Sealed context (authored).** `sealUpdateContext` seals the question and the engaged inputs, pinning (never copying) the bound referent, the bound scope and every input at its current version. **A context never precedes the evidence it seals**, and admission refuses it unless the evidence **already establishes** the fact: for exactly this referent, scope and context, every input a CONTINUING shared recognition, all agreeing on one manifestation. Otherwise nothing is written ("evidence-absent and non-material refusals, no fact"). A referent or scope that cannot be bound to a committed World-State locus or GIP Institution Identity fails closed; the optional Cohort attachment is not introduced and no headcount substitutes for it.
3. **Material trigger = the consequence's object.** A dependent consequence registers its temporal object with the Culture close declared ahead of its own stage. Registration is refused unless the context is **already committed**. There is no standalone engagement, tick or sweep: with no consequence engaged, no fact is ever committed, however many contexts exist.
4. **Communicative Convention Close (IM-02 stage).** The coordinator resolves the declared stage first: it reads exactly the sealed context and the pinned inputs, in declared order, **all AS-OF-NOW after their owners' commits**, and commits the fact under its StageKey-derived group/intent identity and the transaction's durable authority, declaring the **Communicative Convention Close**. The StageKey is `(culture-update/<id>, culture / culture-commit-communicative-convention-close, culture/meaning/<id>)`: no horizon, evidence version, retry, consumer or slice is an identity dimension.
5. **Consumption.** The consequence's stage reads the fact AS-OF-NOW after the close, so it cannot resolve before it. A later consequence for the same update declares no second stage and **adopts** the committed result by identity. Cross-owner reads confer no authority to write Culture; the consumer, the evidence source and every registered owner are refused the Culture key spaces.

## Why this is evidence-based and not a caller assertion

The context has no field for a meaning, norm or manifestation. The manifestation is read from the committed evidence; an evidence set that disagrees, concerns another referent, scope or context, is not a reading, was not yet committed, was pinned at a stale version, belongs to the Culture owner, or rests on a downstream result of the update itself, establishes nothing. Evidence committed after the seal is not part of it. The deletion-test gate is carried as three authored literals (as the KTI foundation carries its Class-A/Class-E gates); the engine does not compute whether the test is met, and the evidence — not the literal — is what establishes anything.

## Judgment calls and open source-boundary findings (none resolves an open gate)

These are for Blue's exact-head literal source-fidelity recheck; each is an engineer-chosen technical encoding that must not acquire game semantics.

- **F46-1 — No lawful producer of social evidence exists, and none is claimed.** The frozen sources describe the Culture judgement as qualitative ("contextual qualitative establishment … only for a material case") and register no concrete owner fact that establishes continuing shared meaning. The `CultureEvidenceReadingSchema` (referent, scope, context, opaque `manifestation`, the `CONTINUING_SHARED_RECOGNITION` marker) is therefore an engineer-chosen interface encoding of CS-UPD's "causally engaged owner fact", and **the only producer is the explicitly test-owned `SYNTHETIC-SOCIAL-EVIDENCE-SOURCE`**. In a build with no extension no evidence owner exists, so nothing can be established. A real Culture cause producer needs its own lawful design and slice.
- **F46-2 — Scope is a bound canonical reference, not a Cohort.** Draft v2 §1 names "scope/Cohort reference", with the Cohort attachment optional and, per the brief, not introduced. The slice binds `referent` and `scope` to a committed World-State locus (a place) or GIP Institution Identity (an institution), both items the source lists as referents; anything else, including a Population Cohort, fails closed. Whether a place/institution is an acceptable "scope" for the positive path, rather than requiring a group reference, is for Blue to confirm.
- **F46-3 — The manifestation is opaque.** The ratified minimum-information contract fixes no class or enumeration of meanings or norms (brief §6); none is asserted. The manifestation is an opaque identifier carried verbatim from the evidence; the engine interprets nothing. No uncertainty or contestation field is carried because none is established here.
- **F46-4 — AS-OF-DECLARED-CLOSE inputs are representable but CONDITIONAL.** CS-UPD allows an input at its owner's named close. No evidence owner declares a close in this slice, so such an input is refused (an undeclared basis is CONDITIONAL and safe-stops the dependent conclusion, F-017 successor delta). Only AS-OF-NOW after the owner's commit is exercised.
- **F46-5 — Disagreeing evidence establishes nothing.** Contestation is not implemented (Draft v2 §4.4: a contest is stored only when it itself persists and changes later causality). A set of readings with more than one manifestation is refused rather than resolved.
- **F46-6 — One slot per referent × scope × context; no update.** A second context for the same slot is refused and the first stands. Rebinding, adaptation, contestation, retirement and the Social Relation Close are not implemented and not claimed; the status is the single literal `ESTABLISHED` and "later retained status/retirement is never fabricated from absence of an event".
- **F46-7 — Consumer rows are test-owned.** ECUL-01, GCUL-01, CUL-AF, LC-03 and ASR-03 have no consumer implementation; the registration rule accepts the two settled read forms (NOW after the close; CLOSE naming it) and a test-owned consumer exercises the NOW form. The LC-03 KTI consumer and the host protocol gateways are not wired to the Culture provider (the protocol layer is out of scope).
- **F46-8 — The access class is engine-side.** The source defines no actor-accessible surface for a committed social fact (CUL-AF requires awareness through the actor's own knowledge state and Epistemic Access, never Cohort membership), so every Culture fact carries `culture.engine-sealed`.
- **Cost.** The advance path reads the engaged context and its pinned inputs by identity and writes only that update's fact. `verifyCultureIntegrity` (like the other verifiers) lists all facts once per open — a diagnostic path (CAL-06), not the advance path, and never a global social scan.
