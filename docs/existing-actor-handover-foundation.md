# Existing-Actor → PC Role Registry Handover Cutover Foundation

Thirty-first bounded slice, built on the Canonical World Identity & Owner Foundation (`docs/canonical-world-foundation.md`: the one Role Registry binding), the existing-actor I-002 pre-binding compilation (`docs/existing-actor-compilation-foundation.md`), the existing-actor World-State coupling anchor (`docs/existing-actor-coupling-foundation.md`) and the Named-Actor capability backbone (`docs/actor-capability-foundation.md`). An already-existing, lawfully non-PC actor that has been compiled into its minimum I-002 Existence Contract and is authoritatively coupled to a locus can now **become the operative `role.player_character` by one Role Registry binding commit**, which is the control/writability cutover.

Governing lock: `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` (F-027 v1.0). No source pin, Runtime Contract snapshot, open gate or acceptance status changed.

## Behavior, end to end

1. Named-Actor facts, the coupling and the compilation exist (any order the earlier slices allow). The binding slot is NULL and the actor's prior owner is writable.
2. `handoverExistingActor` commits **one** fact at `role-registry/binding/role.player_character`. Nothing else is written: not a capability, not a PC capability close, not a Causal Draw fact.
3. From that commit: further Named-Actor writes for the identity — through the owner or a raw `store.commit` — are refused (`KEY_NOT_ADMITTED`). Committed actor facts, the coupling, the contract, the world and the locus are unchanged.
4. A second handover, an ordinary activation, a second coupling or a rival fresh contract cannot reopen or contest the cutover.
5. Everything survives save → close → load with the identical ownership result and, from any durable cut, convergence on the uninterrupted run; a lost reply converges by identity (RC-16); a history built outside admission that splits ownership, fabricates, stales or reorders the handover fails the open.

Fresh-PC activation, the Existence Contract and compilation behavior, the coupling, the PC capability receipt (still refusing a compilation) and every existing ownership boundary are unchanged.

## Capability is provenance only

The binding references the actor's committed state by `{owner, fact_key, version}` and holds no capability. No PC capability fact, PC Capability Close or `ACCEPTED_GENERATION_HISTORY` basis is produced; nothing is copied into the Role Registry; unresolved inherited capability is not resolved; Causal Draw and the world-prior method are not invoked. Established-capability carry-forward into `pc_existence / pc_capability_profile` is the next bounded slice.

## Construction decisions that are technical encodings, not new semantics

- **Second transition type, not a second fact kind or key.** One binding slot, one active binding per `role.player`.
- **Versions pinned at 0 are representable** (like the existing pins) so that a missing prerequisite surfaces as `PREREQUISITE_MISSING`; no committed binding carries 0.
- **Migration is exact and ordered**: coupling first, then the compilation's source pins, so equality is checkable without interpretation. The coupling's own `actor_state` pins stay in the coupling and are re-checked through the coupling rule; the two pin sets need not be equal.
- **Coupling reconciliation lives in canonical integrity**, because it is a statement about the Role Registry binding; the coupling modules keep naming no binding (the predecessor's scope assertion is unchanged).

## Not implemented (fail closed; none resolves an open gate)

Established actor-capability → PC-capability carry-forward; unresolved inherited capability resolution and Causal Draw / world-prior work (`UNRESOLVED` stays unrepresented); PC → non-PC release; replacement, succession, rebinding and forks; deferred activation and the I-002 binding-mode (DEFERRED / ACTIVE-LIMITED) record; relocation or multi-locus expansion; general Role Registry lifecycle; Intrinsic Amendment Events; F-014D `BROADCAST_EXPOSURE`; the existing-actor Confirmation Surface itself (the packet display and acceptance flow); provider, database or deployment work.

## Residuals

- The released contract's `mirror_acceptance` is recorded verbatim, as for a generated PC; whether the displayed set is the complete mandatory handover packet is an I-001/I-002 interface concern not implemented here.
- The binding's governing-rule clause still reads as the first-activation citation (IR §3 input 3 / §5 "explicit player-authored fact", §6 Step 3 native); re-citing it for the handover would need a rule-compatibility mechanism and a registry-surface change for every existing save, so it is left to Blue to decide whether a later lock/citation addendum is wanted. The behavior is governed by `existingActorHandoverViolation`, not by that clause text.
- After the cutover the coupling remains the World-State record of the consumed basis; who owns the PC's current locus thereafter, and relocation, are later slices.
- Handover is judged on identity, ownership and provenance only; it does not judge that the compilation sufficiently preserves the entity.
