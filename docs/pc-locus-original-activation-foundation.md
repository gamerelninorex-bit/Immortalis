# Original Active-PC Qualified Physical-Locus Custody Foundation

> **OOC DEVELOPMENT / SCENARIO INSTRUMENTATION.** Mechanism for the BLUE v5.1 bounded implementation brief (verified native pin `2026-10-11T05:30:16.307Z`, <https://app.notion.com/p/3f620af901e0811abc1ee604de833a41>) on `main` `edad9da9d1ceda032131dbae2fb52df080da55e9` (baseline CI run `38112546294`). It lets the **actual human at the console** ask a compatible **AI referee host** one further, narrower question about words that are already committed, and — only if the engine derives an eligible reading from the host's validated answer and the human separately confirms — lets World-State's custodian commit **one create-only, stationary, current physical locus** for an **original, first-activated Player Character**. It is **not** a general insertion/physicality rule, **not** a First Playable certification, **not** a release-gate activation and **not** a claim that any model interprets well. PF-6/F-028 stays BLOCKED / NOT ACTIVATED; IA/V rows are unpromoted; `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` is unchanged.

## Why it exists

`author-character` (PR #61) releases an ORIGINAL PC (I-002) and binds it by a separately confirmed, referee-qualified `NATIVE_ALREADY_THERE` / `EXPLICIT_PLAYER_AUTHORED_FACT` first `ACTIVATION` (Role Registry). That binding's `coupling.world` / `coupling.locus` is a **binding-time referent**, not a movable current physical-locus ledger, and PR #61 expressly establishes **no physical custody**. Genuine local perception, ordinary local resource interaction and later movement need exactly one canonical **current stationary physical-locus owner** first (D1 / M-S prerequisite to M-P). The existing owner `world_pc_locus_custody` already existed, but its only source branch was `EXISTING_ACTOR_HANDOVER`. This slice adds the second, exclusive branch for an original `ACTIVATION`, after a **semantic** qualification the existing `IR_NATIVE_COMPATIBILITY` verdict cannot give: that verdict proves native-vs-bridge and premise compatibility, **not that the character is one material, spatial subject at exactly that locus**.

## The sequence

1. **Prerequisites (other commands, other owners):** `establish-locality` (world, locus, finite source; PR #60); `author-character` (kept I-002 release + referee-qualified first `ACTIVATION` binding; PR #61). `establish-character-locus` refuses unless the character is this operator's released **and** bound character, verified (`CHARACTER_NONE`, `CHARACTER_RELEASED_UNBOUND`, `CHARACTER_CONFLICT`, `BINDING_CONFLICT`); a portal/arrival statement never binds, so it never reaches the physical question.
2. **The question** (nothing written): the engine builds the task from the released character's own statements, the binding's native statement, and the committed world and locus (premise, reference/causal basis, law departures, insertion constraints, locus designation and constraints) at the versions delivered. The host reads it through the same stdio MCP server as before and answers: the **form** (`SINGLE_MATERIAL_BODY` or one of the I-002 §4.3 alternatives, anchored to a verbatim quotation of one delivered character statement); the **presence** (`NATIVE_AT_THIS_LOCUS`, elsewhere, several loci, non-spatial, bridge/arrival, or ambiguous; quoted); every **dependency** of the presence on an independent actor's cooperation, an external right or another external claim (quoted); and every delivered world/locus fact as supports / not relevant / contradicts / unclear (quoted where it supports or contradicts). The engine validates coverage, quotations and coherence and **derives** the verdict. `PHYSICAL_ELIGIBLE` requires one material body, natively at this locus, with no dependency and no contradicted or unclear fact.
3. **Findings, then the third confirmation.** The findings are shown. Only a derived `PHYSICAL_ELIGIBLE` is offered a fresh phrase `establish-character-locus <challenge>`, bound to {campaign, character, world, locus, versions, statement, native decision, referee question}. The earlier `place-character` confirmation was given on the explicit statement that the binding "gives the character no physical position", so a physical fact is **never** written under it. Declining, end of input, a stale or guessed phrase: nothing is written.
4. **Referee evidence, then custody.** The physical decision is committed once, create-only (`role.gm` + `permission.rule.adjudicate` applied by the trusted operation, never by the answer). World-State's custodian then commits the one custody, reading and depending on the released contract, the binding, the world, the locus and **both** qualifying decisions (never copying them), as an ordinary archivist publication.
5. **Fresh reopen.** The package is closed and opened again through the ordinary owner-verified open and the custody is read back and compared. Only then is `LOCUS_ESTABLISHED` reported. A subsequent `pcPhysicalLocusCustody(pcId)` / `pcCurrentLocus(pcId)` answers "where is the PC now" **from custody only**, never from the binding.

## The rule (one statement, used by admission and by load verification)

`pcPhysicalLocusCustodyViolation` dispatches on the basis. The **ACTIVATION** branch (`activationCustodyViolation`) requires: the slot is the PC's; the operative binding names this PC and is the first `ACTIVATION` (never the handover); no actor coupling and no existing-actor compilation exists for the identity (no existing actor is manufactured); the stationary locus is exactly the binding's coupled world and locus; the pinned versions are the exact current versions of the contract, binding, world and locus **and** equal the versions the binding itself pinned; the cited native decision is `BIND_ELIGIBLE` over the binding's preserved statement at its pins (`nativeQualificationViolation`, the very predicate the binding's own consumer check uses); the cited physical decision is `PHYSICAL_ELIGIBLE` for this character over the same statement and cites exactly the contract, binding, world and locus at the custody's pins (`physicalQualificationViolation`); both decisions are at the key their own task identity derives; and **no other current-locus authority** exists (no fallback entity, ECT-01 transition, Logistics commitment or transfer under the identity). The group must read and depend on each of the six prerequisites at their current versions and carry the exclusive governing citation. Canonical integrity accepts the kind's current rule for any value, so `verifyPcPhysicalLocusCustodyIntegrity` additionally requires **each basis to carry its own citation**; `verifyRefereeIntegrity` requires the same of the physical decision. A raw `store.commit` cannot bypass any of it, and a package built outside admission fails on open.

## Interruption, retry and convergence

Identities are keyed digests under the keep's secret of exactly what was confirmed (`locusIdOf`); the physical question's task identity hashes the exact context, so the same question is the same task on every run; the physical record is create-only and a replay of the same answer is a replay, a different one `DECISION_CONFLICT`. Tests interrupt **before qualification** (silent host: `REFEREE_PENDING`, the same question found again), **after qualification but before the physical commit** (decision retained, no custody), **after an ambiguous commit** (the custody landed and its reply was lost: found by identity, nothing asked, nothing written), **on reopen** (kill after the custody commits; a fresh process verifies), and **before and after every mutating file-system operation**; every rerun converges on the uninterrupted campaign. Stale or different source versions, a different PC or locus, a duplicate or rival physical write all fail with nothing written (a duplicate under a new intent is `VERSION_CONFLICT`).

## Falsifying contrasts (each refuses with no physical fact; re-derived from upstream evidence, never from an "approved/denied" fixture)

An incorporeal, coextensive, multi-bodied, distributed, host-dependent or body-less character; an ambiguous form; a presence elsewhere, in several places, in no place or by arrival; a presence resting on an independent actor's cooperation or an external right; a committed world constraint that excludes a material body; a world fact the host cannot judge; no host bound; a host not bound to this operation; a silent host; an answer with ungrounded quotations; a missing, wrong-operation, swapped or non-eligible decision; a different PC; stale pins; a handover basis on an activated PC (and the reverse); a missing read or dependency; the wrong governing citation; a duplicate or rival writer.

## Construction decisions that are technical encodings

- **Two decisions, not one.** Native/compatibility proves "native, no bridge, world-compatible" for the binding; the physical decision proves "one material subject at this locus". Neither is read as the other's evidence.
- **The statement is the binding's.** The physical question never takes a caller's string, a world, a locus or an identity; they are read from the committed binding.
- **A third confirmation, by the same module-private token mechanism**, with its own verb. The earlier placement confirmation told the player no physical position follows.
- **Post-handover consumers stay handover-only.** Voluntary movement (`pcMovementCommitmentViolation`) and the stationary active-PC target advance accept only a custody whose basis is `EXISTING_ACTOR_HANDOVER` (`isPostHandoverCustody`): the original custody states where the PC stationarily is and implies no movement, request, Logistics subject or time advance.
- **No cross-owner rollback.** A bound character with a retained physical decision and no custody is reported as exactly that on the next run; nothing is rolled back.
- **`dependencies` are refused, not stripped.** A presence that rests on an actor's cooperation or an external right is not a physical fact the player may author for the world; the player restates it.

## Residuals

- The host's `claimed_model` is a self-reported label. The engine can check that an answer is well-formed, complete, coherent and grounded in the delivered words; it cannot check that the interpretation is _right_. A wrong but well-grounded answer is possible and is bounded only by the player seeing the findings, the three separate confirmations and the retained, reviewable record.
- A host bound before this slice (two operations) must be bound again for the third operation: `referee-bind-host` refuses an existing host identity, so use a new `--host-id`.
- A custody of this basis is not consumed by anything yet: movement, perception, local resources and time are later slices under fresh preflights.
