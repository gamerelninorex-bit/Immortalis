# Existing-Actor Unresolved Capability → PC Material-Trigger World-Prior Resolution Close Foundation

Thirty-third bounded slice, built on the PC Capability Profile / PC Capability Close Receiving Foundation (`docs/pc-capability-foundation.md`), the Existing-Actor → PC Role Registry Handover Cutover Foundation (`docs/existing-actor-handover-foundation.md`), the Established Existing-Actor Capability → PC Capability Carry-Forward Close Foundation (`docs/pc-capability-carry-forward-foundation.md`), the Named-Actor Randomized Capability Establishment (`docs/actor-randomized-establishment-foundation.md`) and the Causal Draw Foundation (`docs/causal-draw-foundation.md`). An actor that has already undergone the lawful `EXISTING_ACTOR_HANDOVER` and whose capability was **never fixed** before the cutover now has that capability **resolved on its later material trigger by the one PC owner's own `pc_capability_profile`**: by the smallest established world/actor causes when they suffice (WS-016 §16, nothing drawn), otherwise within a branch-invariant bounded prior fixed by the existing neutral Causal Draw. The **existing PC Capability Close** then publishes it through the existing IM-02 temporal path, read cross-owner only **AS-OF-DECLARED-CLOSE**.

Governing lock: `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` (F-027 v1.0 composed with F-027-CD1 v1.0). No source pin, Runtime Contract snapshot, open gate or acceptance status changed.

## Behavior, end to end

1. At handover nothing about the capability exists: no Named-Actor basis or capability, no PC basis or capability, no draw. **There is no eager enumeration or scan, and `UNRESOLVED` stays unrepresented** (no field, value or ledger can hold it).
2. On a later material trigger, the owner states that the smallest established world/actor causes suffice (and authors its marker) **or** that genuine uncertainty remains (and authors the bounded prior).
3. **Causes suffice:** `establishExistingActorCapabilityBasis` commits **one** sealed basis at `pc/<pc>/capability-basis/<capability>`, method `EXISTING_ACTOR_CAUSALLY_DETERMINED`, reading and depending on the contract, the binding and each cause. **Nothing is drawn: no precommit, no result, no entropy.**
4. **Uncertainty remains:** `establishExistingActorRandomizedCapabilityBasis` first refuses what could never be admitted (the handover, the predecessor Named-Actor state, the boundary, each cause) — **before any draw state exists**, because a draw is irreversible — then has the Causal Draw fix the slot (`resolution` is the resolver context CDE-01 requires), reads the owner's meaning off the selected opaque candidate, and commits its **own** basis reading the exact result fact/version and depending on the canonical result group. The draw is committed first.
5. A dependent consequence declares the existing PC Capability Close ahead of its own stage; the coordinator resolves the unchanged `pcCapabilityCloseProvider`; the consequence reads the capability **AS-OF-DECLARED-CLOSE**, naming `PC Capability Close`. The close and the capability record carry no draw field and no same-resolution feedback exists.
6. **Convergence.** Every step is idempotent by identity (RC-16): after a crash or lost reply at the precommit, the result or the basis, a retry reaches the **same** draw and the **same** basis; a committed seed is never replaced; an UNKNOWN outcome withholds new entropy from the store until it is reopened and reconciled; a changed prior or cause set under the same slot is `DRAW_IDENTITY_CONFLICT`, never a second draw. Once draw evidence exists for the slot, **only the establishment that reads it may fill the slot** — a deterministic, carried or generated basis over it is refused at admission and flagged on load by receipt order.
7. Everything survives save → close → load with the identical result and, from any durable cut, convergence on the uninterrupted run.

Fails closed (each by admission for a raw commit and again on load): no handover, an ordinary `ACTIVATION` binding, another identity; an absent, stale, unread or undepended cause, or a cause owned by kernel state, Causal Draw provenance, the PC's own capability state or the predecessor's capability state; a capability the Named-Actor owner already holds (basis **or** capability — an authoritative one is PR #34 carry-forward territory and is never rerolled or regraded) or already began a Causal Draw for (the interrupted pre-handover establishment: fail closed rather than reroll); a Population ledger identity; a smuggled marker/prior/weight/seed/claim/source or a draw on the deterministic method; a randomized basis whose draw, rule, causes, prior, candidate or marker is not exactly what its committed result determines; the superseded citation on a new write; a second establishment or an overwrite (sealed).

## Construction decisions that are technical encodings, not new semantics

- **Two methods, not a second kind.** The Integration Map assigns PC mastery to the one `pc_capability_profile` facet; the received basis is how the PC owner already receives an established fact before its close. The two world-prior methods are distinct and closed so generation history, inherited mastery and world-prior resolution can never share a provenance path.
- **The marker is the owner's, never the engine's.** Like a causally determined Named-Actor basis, the deterministic marker is the owner's authored judgment over the pinned causes; the randomized marker is exactly the owner's reading of the selected candidate. The engine judges identity, ownership and provenance, never sufficiency, rarity or sparse-evidence (§17 stays an authoring discipline: no source supplies a rule from causes to possibilities or weights, so none is invented).
- **Draw-key components.** `causal_process_id`, `opportunity_id` and the operation name are technical encodings of CDK-01's four dimensions; there is exactly one genuine opportunity per slot because the basis slot is create-only.
- **Causes.** The same `CapabilityCauseSchema` as the Named-Actor path (pinned foreign facts, never copies). The PC's own compiled Existence Contract is a lawful cause (compiled I-002 material); the predecessor's capability state is not, because an authoritative actor capability is carried exactly, never re-resolved.
- **Cause currency.** Admission requires each cause at its exact current version (and the draw layer re-proves inputs at precommit); load verification judges a cause at the version it pinned, because a cause may legitimately advance afterwards.
- **Rule citation.** The pre-slice citation did not cite the Handover Contract, I-002 v2.1, WS-016 §16 or F-027-CD1, which was true provenance for the facts committed under it and is not for a world-prior fact. The current citation extends it; the old one survives through the repository's existing bounded `legacyRule` pattern. The overlays are cited in provenance and docs, **never added to the spec lock**.
- **Cross-module imports.** The PC modules do not import the Named-Actor modules; the predecessor Causal Draw identity is recomputed from the contract's `namedActorCapabilityDrawKey`.

## Residuals

- **A cause that advances between the committed draw and the establishment** (shared with the Named-Actor consumer's recorded gap). CD1 §12 leaves the outcome to the owner's rule and no source defines it, so the establishment fails closed as stale and a changed cause set is `DRAW_IDENTITY_CONFLICT`: the slot stays unestablishable until a source defines the outcome. Smallest case: `tests/integration/pc-capability-world-prior-recovery.test.ts` ("a cause that moves on between the committed draw and the establishment").
- **The CAL-08 port sees no candidates** (shared). A caller holding the resolver context can commit a draw under the PC slot's identity with a prior the owner would never author (under the registry-pinned rule); the owner refuses to read it as its own establishment and its own request then conflicts, so a stray draw can block a slot (fail closed). A fix needs a Causal Draw port decision, outside this slice.
- **No PC materiality field.** WS-016 §16 triggers "only when … personal mastery is materially required", and the Named-Actor record stores the actor-persistence-gate class. §4's PC gate ("a previously deferred capability becomes materially relevant") has no enumerated record, so none is invented: the trigger is the invocation by the dependent consequence's owner, and the engine judges neither materiality nor sufficiency.
- The deterministic branch lets the owner author any marker over any lawful cause set (as for a causally determined Named-Actor basis); §17's rarity guard is the owner's authoring discipline here as well.
- The PC Capability Close of **every** capability committed after this slice carries the extended citation, including closes of historical-class bases; the historical citation remains lawful for facts committed before it.
- Local success is not provider or recovery acceptance (IA-07 BLOCKED). The repository pins Node `>=22.22.2`; the construction container had 22.22.0, so dependencies were installed with the engine check disabled for the validation run only.
