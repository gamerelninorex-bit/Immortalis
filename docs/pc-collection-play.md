# Playing the first consequential action: launch and play instructions

> **OOC DEVELOPMENT / SCENARIO INSTRUMENTATION.** The CPAA-C1 v0.3 loop (a stationary Player Character gathers a finite loose material into an empty local Reserve) as the shipped `immortalis-operator` commands run it. **It is mechanism, not an accepted playable game.** The rules marked _newly authorized_ were written under the Sovereign's bounded semantic delegation (PR #72 comment 6215171891; mission addendum pin `2026-10-16T12:07:41.219Z`); they are not frozen-source rules, not spec-lock pins and await independent BLUE review. No First Playable PASS, gate promotion or merge is claimed. The design lineage is [`pc-collection-foundation.md`](pc-collection-foundation.md).

**No paid API, no credential, no network.** The engine calls no model. Every question to a referee goes through the owner-only authenticated exchange ([`ai-referee-host-foundation.md`](ai-referee-host-foundation.md)): a connected subscription host (any MCP client registered to `immortalis-referee`) or a **human game master** at `immortalis-referee-console`. Every player confirmation is typed at the operator's own console.

Run the operator as `node --experimental-strip-types packages/operator/src/bin.ts <command> --campaign <dir> --campaign-id <id> [--operator-dir <dir>] …`.

## What is external (nothing below can be supplied by the engine, a script or a model)

1. **The player** at the operator console: every choice and every confirmation phrase (`relocate`-style typed challenge). There is no default answer and no auto-confirmation anywhere. The choice of _which_ character, setting and amount is the player's; this document does not make it.
2. **The referee** for each interpretation: character classification and placement, the **cycle** attestation, the **reach / scene** reading, and the observation. A real GM reads the delivered words and answers through the MCP tools or the console.
3. **The setting author's files** (below). The author states only what is the author's to state; the owners derive the rest.
4. **A phone or remote route is NOT installed.** The confirmations are terminal prompts of the operator's own process; nothing here is reachable from a remote client. Verify the route you actually have before relying on it.

## Before you look: what `observe-surroundings` can rest on (authoring guidance, no content is suggested)

`observe-surroundings` earns a receipt **only** from statements that already exist: the engine supplies no premise and applies no default, and a look that rests on too little is retained as a non-receipt at that basis and the same question is **not asked again**. How the host reads the words you wrote is the host's reading (it may take a property that is strongly entailed or safely conventional for the declared use, as I-002 section 10 permits), but no reading creates a circumstance that no owner committed. The statements are yours to write or to leave unwritten:

- **The character's own words** (`author-character`): they are what the question reads for a **sense**. Whether an ordinary baseline such as "One ordinary human body" is read as ordinary human vision is the host's reading under I-002 section 10; it does not, by itself, give a facing, open eyes, light, a path or a receipt. Where a material detail is really unresolved, the narrow clarification is the route.
- **The placement** you type and confirm (`author-character`, native presence): it is the only statement the question reads for _where_ the character is, _which way_ it faces and whether its eyes are open. It cannot be amended afterwards.
- **The setting's prose** (`establish-locality`, `locus.constraints`): the only statements the question reads for the **light**, the **clarity of the air**, whether the **path** is open or blocked, and **how what is in view looks**. The author states only what is the author's to state, and may state any of it as blocked or dark.
- **After a gathering,** the only consequence delivered is the mechanical sentence that it was completed. It does not describe how anything looks.

All of this must be committed **before** the look; none of it can be added to a saved campaign (the world, locus, contract and placement are sealed, and the shipped runtime has no pose, original-world amendment or general sensory-amendment producer), and a changed question at the same instant is a different decision record, which is **not** by itself a reason to ask again. This is a guide to what can be authored, not a schema: a world is not required to be written as six sentences, and which statements a given world needs follows its own physical and channel causality. The referee is shown exactly those statements (the character's accepted words, the placement statement, the world and locus prose and, after a gathering, that one sentence), verbatim; it is shown nothing else about the scene. What the evaluator requires, and what a shorthand-authored character is delivered, is in [`active-pc-direct-observation-foundation.md`](active-pc-direct-observation-foundation.md).

## What is verified, and how far

- **By automated tests** (`tests/integration/pc-collection-bootstrap-loop.test.ts` and the operator / exchange suites): the whole sequence runs by the shipped commands from a campaign that begins with **no canonical time**; nothing is set up through the test kit. Every referee reading and every console line in those tests is **scripted by the test (plumbing only)**: that is _not_ a player's choice, a GM's reading or First Playable evidence.
- **Falsifying negatives:** no time → nothing prepared or declared; no attestation → the act is not offered; no closure → the reading is never asked for; a further resident, ledger version, actor or PC makes the closure and scope STALE (an in-flight act is invalidated, never silently completed); a ledger that merely omits the PC is UNKNOWN without a current scope; a forged coverage or a right over a source of another locus is refused; a foreign claim or another process due within the interval defeats enrollment and is not suppressed; every arbitrary-target route refuses whatever horizon is asked while work is pending.
- **ORIGINAL worlds only:** `initialize-time`, `declare-closed-setting` and `prepare-collection` refuse (nothing asked, nothing written) unless every committed world is `ORIGINAL`; file ownership is only the ingress filter, the authority is the bounded delegation.
- **Not established:** that any model or GM reads a real sentence correctly; a real player's transcript; a remote/phone input route; mid-work standing loss (no terminal transition exists: it holds the campaign clock and is excluded from play); the C8 hidden-timing channel and D-2; source _ownership_ beyond the author's stated grounds; §28 labor accounting.

## The supported next step toward real play

1. At a local terminal the operator owns (not the cloud host, which has no phone or remote ingress), create a campaign with one ORIGINAL world, locus, source and bound PC, then run the numbered sequence above. Every confirmation is typed by the operator.
2. Connect the GM: register `immortalis-referee` in a subscription MCP client, or run `immortalis-referee-console` as a human GM, after `referee-bind-collection-host`.
3. Post a genuine bounded task (what the GM would be asked, with the proof and source citations and the exchange shape, never a secret) if BLUE is to supply a real reading. BLUE's answer is read by the operator and typed into the console: a GitHub comment is not authenticated ingress and nothing posted there reaches canon by itself.
4. Only the resulting transcript (a real player's typed choice, a real GM's reading, the receipt, a reopen and a second choice) can be offered as authentic-play evidence. Scripted runs remain mechanism evidence.

## If an act is held at its boundary with its eligibility lost (R-4, newly authorized)

`immortalis-operator settle-collection-lapse --campaign <dir> --campaign-id <id>` takes no input and asks nothing: it presents the mechanical, nonproductive lapse of an act that the time kernel has held at its own earned boundary when its eligibility is no longer supported (Source stays, Reserve stays, the claim and the occupied interval are released). It never resumes time; only a later, separately confirmed `pc-target-resume` consumes the original target. `pc-target-resume` presents the same settlement quietly first. Nothing applicable derives → nothing is written. See [`pc-collection-foundation.md`](pc-collection-foundation.md) for scope, limits (including the narrowed C-1 residual) and status.
