# World-State Entity Continuity / Transition Close Backbone Foundation

Seventh bounded slice, built on the Campaign Continuity Foundation (`docs/campaign-package.md`), the Canonical World Identity & Owner Foundation (`docs/canonical-world-foundation.md`), the Turnless Temporal & Resolution Transaction Foundation (`docs/temporal-foundation.md`), the Population (`docs/population-foundation.md`), Production (`docs/production-foundation.md`) and Economy (`docs/economy-foundation.md`) foundations. An **already-accepted fallback physical entity** — the durable Production completed and the World-State custodian accepted — can now undergo **one material same-entity locus transition**: an already-established external causing result requires it, **World-State, as fallback custodian, commits the entity's new authoritative current locus exactly once** and seals it in **ECT-01 under the named Entity Transition Close**, and a later consumer can read that committed transition without mutating or recreating it. The causing owner never becomes an entity or location writer, and after save, close and load the transition is still pending, still one and never duplicated.

Governing lock: `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` (F-027 v1.0). Sources: WG World Generation & World-State Kernel (lock pin `2026-09-25T13:44:22.668Z`), Ownership Guards — _F-016 Ratified Fallback Objective Physical-Entity Custody_; F-016 v0.4 successor delta (pin `2026-09-25T13:45:28.162Z`) §2 R-1 **ECT-01 — Entity Continuity / Transition Result** and the cause-owner / entity-commit split, §3 R-2 (the accepted custodian is the single writer of the durable's physical state), §5 R-4 **Entity Transition Close**; F-017 v0.3 (conformance pin `2026-09-25T23:42:27.802Z`), the implicated ECT-01 / entity-continuity interface only. **CAL-01 (APPROXIMATE-PARTITION) remains UNCALIBRATED and is not used: every proof is exact.** Every other F-027 open gate is unchanged.

This is a **foundation proof**, not a Logistics slice and not a generic entity or asset system. All fixture data is synthetic (`packages/testkit/src/entity-fixtures.ts`): the **causing owner** and the **downstream consumer** are clearly synthetic testkit parties registered through the existing extension seam (which cannot write a canonical, kernel, Population, Production, Economy or World-State key space); the cause is not a canonical gameplay subsystem. The custodian is real and lives in `@immortalis/engine`.

## One canon: how it reuses IM-02 / IM-03 / IM-11 / IM-12 / IM-17

Nothing new is persisted outside the existing campaign package. A transition is an ordinary `OwnerFact` written through `store.commit` by the **existing** World-State fallback custodian (the same owner and facet as the accepted entity — **no new owner is registered**), so the existing validation, expected-version checks, RC-16 receipts, idempotent replay, unknown-outcome reconciliation, hash-chained journal and package/load integrity are the ones in force. **The package format is unchanged (still format version 1); there is no second entity journal, scheduler, location ledger, sidecar save path, polling loop or per-object refresh.** The temporal coordinator was not changed (a conformance test asserts it never names World-State or the entity transition). The only edits to predecessor engine code are additive wiring: the registry gains the one kind, `identityMismatch` handles it, the custodian's group check delegates the transition kind to its own check, object registration also asks the entity-transition rule, and `CanonicalCampaign.open`/`verify` also run the new verifier.

## One current locus, history intact

The accepted entity is a **sealed, create-only** fact (version 1), and Production's completion and Economy's realized access **pin it and re-derive from it**. So the transition does **not** amend it:

- the entity stays byte-identical — same identity, conserved quantity, physical condition, version and provenance. Its `locality` is, and stays, the locality it was **accepted** at: historical origin;
- the transition is a **separate** sealed World-State fact, one slot per entity, that cites the origin it leaves (`locus.from`, which admission requires to equal the entity's accepted locality) and the destination (`locus.to`);
- the entity's **current locus is resolved by one rule**: the transition's destination if the transition exists, else the accepted locality. Because the transition's origin is pinned to that locality and only one transition per entity can exist, the origin **ceases to be current** without ever being rewritten, and no rival simultaneous current locus can exist;
- Production's acceptance (`availability`, `accepted_from`) and Economy's realization remain historically valid after the later transition (they are byte-identical and still verify; tested with the Economy-realized chain).

**Implementation Escalation: none.** The create-only representation does not contradict the ECT-01 / current-locus requirement: the schema nowhere declares `locality` to be the _current_ locus (it is the acceptance locality), and the requirement is met by a second sealed World-State record resolved by a single rule rather than by amending sealed history. If Blue prefers the current locus to live in the entity fact itself, that would need an amendment mechanism for a sealed kind and would invalidate the pins Production and Economy take on version 1 — a semantic decision for Blue, not taken here.

## The transition is a stage, not a method

One causing result is **one temporal object, one earned boundary and one declared stage** (`world-state-commit-entity-transition`), declared `declares_close: "Entity Transition Close"` — committing that stage **is** the named close (F-017 §3 CLOSE; F-016 v0.4 §5 R-4). The stage reads — AS-OF-NOW — the causing result, the accepted entity, the world and the destination locus, and writes exactly the transition. It is lawful **only as its IM-02 stage**: the group and intent identity derived from its StageKey, caused by a transaction the kernel has durably **opened** before it and which the stage depends on (`stageAuthorityViolation`, the one shared rule for every IM-02 owner stage). A raw publication of the identical group under any other identity — or under the right identity and a forged transaction cause — is refused, and it commits only for an engaged, unretired boundary.

A **downstream consumer** reads the committed Entity Transition Close AS-OF-NOW (the brief's requirement), ordered after the stage when both are due at one instant and by identity when its own boundary is later; the stage also declares the named close, so reading it AS-OF-DECLARED-CLOSE is lawful too. Either way the read is a dependency, never a write: the transition stays version 1 under its one stage group.

## Judgment calls and open residuals (none resolves an open gate)

- **One transition per entity.** The brief asks for _one_ same-entity current-locus transition. The slot is one per entity (create-only), so a second transition is a version conflict, never a sibling truth. A later transition (Logistics) needs a successor mechanism (an owner amendment or a chained record) that this slice deliberately does not invent.
- **The causing result is an unregistered source's own fact, read at its establishment.** It lives in the cause owner's own key space (never a canonical, kernel, Population, Production, Economy or World-State key space), is written by the source it names, and is read at **version 1**. The cause owner keeps its own lifecycle: advancing its fact later neither alters a sealed transition nor fails the load; advancing it _before_ the boundary leaves the transition pending and uncredited (fail-closed), because a result is read as established. A real, registered cause owner (Logistics, Military…) would need its own registered kind admitted as a source, as Economy's request was for Production.
- **The cause names where, not that the entity is there.** Its outcome is "same-entity locus change required, to this destination". It never asserts the entity's current location; that is World-State's own commit.
- **Registration refuses a cause that cannot lawfully transition.** A subject that is not an accepted entity, a destination that is the entity's own locus, in another world or not an established locus of the entity's world, an instant not after the establishment, a source mismatch or a result already advanced past its establishment earns no object and no boundary (it would only hold time unresolved).
- **Two causes for one entity.** Registering a cause for an entity that already transitioned is refused. If two causes are registered _before_ either resolves, one transition commits and the other stays unresolved and fails closed (no sibling current locus); there is no supersession or cancellation mechanism in this slice, so that cause keeps its boundary active.
- **Interrupted path (inherited model).** If the process stops after the transition's stage committed and before the kernel group did, the transition fact is durable while its boundary is still unretired; the resolver already reports the destination. This is the same interrupted-stage model Production's acceptance and Economy's realization already have: load verification accepts it as an interrupted transition awaiting its engaged boundary, and the continuation **adopts** the committed stage and records it once.
- **Stage authority (repaired after Blue review of PR #8).** A stage's identity is derived from public inputs, so identity alone cannot tell the coordinator from a caller that copies it. The transition stage is therefore lawful only under a **durable transaction authority** the kernel opened before it (`docs/temporal-foundation.md`, "Durable pre-stage transaction authority"): the stage depends on it, it must still be open and cover the stage, and admission and load verification apply the one shared rule. A raw stage with the right group and intent ids and a forged `transaction:<id>` cause is refused and publishes nothing; a genuine stage committed before the kernel group is adopted by the same transaction when re-presented. Residual: a trusted archivist that hand-writes a _lawful_ opening and its stage has opened a real transaction, completed by re-presenting it.
- **Basis/secrecy.** The transition inherits the accepted entity's labels, never defaulted.
- **Same world only.** The entity key embeds its world, so a same-entity transition stays within its world; moving between worlds is not representable.
- **Cost.** The advance path reads the engaged cone by identity: the cause, the accepted entity, the world and the destination locus (and the temporal object and state). `verifyEntityTransitionIntegrity` (like the other verifiers) lists all facts once per open — a diagnostic path (CAL-06), not the advance path.
