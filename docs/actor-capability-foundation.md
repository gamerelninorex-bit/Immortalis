# Named-Actor Character Capability & Capability-Close Backbone Foundation

Ninth bounded slice, built on the Campaign Continuity Foundation (`docs/campaign-package.md`), the Canonical World Identity & Owner Foundation (`docs/canonical-world-foundation.md`), the Turnless Temporal & Resolution Transaction Foundation (`docs/temporal-foundation.md`, including its durable pre-stage transaction authority) and, where it must not be touched, the Population foundation (`docs/population-foundation.md`). A **consequential non-PC Named Actor** can now **hold one sparse piece of authoritative durable personal mastery** with the **lawful Named-Actor owner (`named_actor` / `actor_profile`)**: the mastery is **established from committed, pinned causal facts** and sealed **before** any dependent consequence can use it, **published at the Named-Actor owner's declared capability close**, and **read by the consequence AS-OF-DECLARED-CLOSE**. It is a foundation for later Epistemics, KTI and full Named-Actor behavior; it is not their implementation.

Governing lock: `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` (F-027 v1.0). Sources: WS-016 Character Capability Protocol v1 (Concept and Working Operational Draft v1, Sovereign-ratified 2026-09-25, with the Blue Bounded Integration Map; lock pin `2026-09-24T17:38:59.210Z`) §§2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 16, 17, 20, 23, 25, 27, 27.1, 28, 28.1, 32; WS-006 Named Actors — F-014A v3.3 (lock pin `2026-09-25T00:18:36.828Z`), with the F-014A v3.2 Character Capability amendment it inherits and the W3-CP3 PC-Control Handover interface note (F-014A is writable only while the entity is under lawful non-PC actor ownership); F-017 v0.3 §3 (CLOSE), as already implemented by IM-02. **CAL-01 (APPROXIMATE-PARTITION) remains UNCALIBRATED and is not used: every proof is exact. No numeric skill value, XP, level, percentage, modifier, roll, prior, seed, default grade or periodic advancement exists anywhere.** Every other F-027 open gate is unchanged.

This is a **foundation proof**, not a character simulator. All fixture data is synthetic (`packages/testkit/src/actor-fixtures.ts`): a test **history source** commits the causal facts a basis may pin (a stand-in for the foreign owners — KTI, Epistemic, biography — that are not implemented), and a test **consumer** is the dependent consequence that materially requires an actor's mastery. Both are registered through the existing extension seam (which cannot write a canonical, kernel, Population, Production, Economy, World-State, Logistics or Named-Actor key space); neither owns the capability. The Named-Actor owner is real and lives in `@immortalis/engine`.

## One canon: how it reuses IM-02 / IM-03 / IM-11 / IM-12 / IM-17

Nothing new is persisted outside the existing campaign package. Both Named-Actor kinds are ordinary `OwnerFact`s written through `store.commit`, so the existing validation, expected-version checks, RC-16 receipts, idempotent replay, unknown-outcome reconciliation, hash-chained journal and package/load integrity are the ones in force. **The package format is unchanged (still format version 1); there is no second journal, scheduler, roster, sidecar save path, polling loop or per-actor refresh.** The temporal coordinator was not changed (a conformance test asserts it never names the Named-Actor owner). The only edits to predecessor engine code are additive wiring: the registry gains the two kinds, `identityMismatch` handles them, `canonicalAdmission` dispatches the Named-Actor owner, object registration also asks the Named-Actor rule, and `CanonicalCampaign.open`/`verify` also run the Named-Actor verifier.

## The lifecycle: basis → close → consumption

1. **Basis (authored, before any consequence).** `establishCapabilityBasis` publishes the sealed basis. Admission requires each cause to be a committed foreign fact, owned by the source it names, **pinned at its current version** (read and depended on, never copied), not the actor owner's own state and not kernel state; and the actor identity to be a lawful Named-Actor subject (below). The basis is not the capability and is not readable cross-owner.
2. **Material trigger = the consequence's object.** A dependent consequence registers its temporal object with the Named-Actor close stage declared ahead of its own stage. Registration is refused unless the basis is **already committed** — a consequence cannot create the capability it needs. There is no standalone engagement, tick or sweep: with no consequence engaged, no capability is ever resolved.
3. **Close (IM-02 stage).** The coordinator resolves the declared stage first: it reads exactly the basis (NOW) and commits the capability under its StageKey-derived group/intent identity and the transaction's durable authority. The StageKey is `(actor-capability/<actor>/<capability>, named_actor / named-actor-publish-capability-close, <capability key>)`: no horizon, source version, retry, consumer or slice is an identity dimension.
4. **Consumption.** The consequence's stage reads the capability **AS-OF-DECLARED-CLOSE**, naming the actor owner's declared close, so it cannot resolve before the close and observes the committed close itself, not whatever came after it. A later consequence of the same actor/capability declares the same stage in its own object and **adopts** the committed close by identity — never duplicated, recomputed or re-rolled. Two consequences due at the same instant share one close: one declares the stage, the other reads it.

## PC authorship and aggregate identity

By identity, with no scan (`actorBoundaryViolation`), at the basis and again at the close (so an entity that comes under player control between the two gets no capability and its close fails closed, while independent valid work in the same instant still resolves), and re-checked on load by receipt order:

- an identity with an I-002 Existence Contract is a **Player Character**, released or bound: its capability belongs to the PC owner, which this slice does not implement;
- the identity the Role Registry binding names as the active PC is under lawful player control (WS-006 W3-CP3);
- an identity that is a **Population ledger** is an aggregate, never given an individual mastery record (WS-016 §20/§28).

The schemas have no cohort, population, institution, headcount, promotion, body, entity or lifecycle field, so no individual record can be assigned to an aggregate, no individuation can duplicate an aggregate contribution, and no body/entity identity is assumed (the capability belongs to the actor as an agency, WS-016 §28). Establishing an individual's capability leaves a Population ledger byte-identical (tested).

## Judgment calls and open residuals (none resolves an open gate)

- **Implementation Escalation — genuine uncertainty (WS-016 §16 steps 3–4). _Resolved for the consumer side by the twenty-seventh slice (`docs/actor-randomized-establishment-foundation.md`): `BOUNDED_PRIOR_RANDOMIZED` is now a representable method, fixed only by a committed Causal Draw; the text below records the state at the ninth slice._** When the pre-existing causes do _not_ sufficiently determine a capability, the source requires a branch-invariant bounded prior and "the simulation's existing lawful uncertainty/randomization mechanism"; Character Capability "owns no separate RNG". This engine has **no** lawful uncertainty or randomization mechanism, and inventing one (or a default grade) would be a semantic invention. The path is therefore **unrepresentable and fails closed**: the only establishment method is `CAUSALLY_DETERMINED`, and an attempt to supply `BOUNDED_PRIOR_RANDOMIZED` is refused with the explicit code `ESTABLISHMENT_UNCERTAINTY_UNSUPPORTED` and writes nothing. Smallest reproducible case: `tests/integration/actor-enforcement.test.ts` ("the genuine-uncertainty branch fails closed"). Plausible alternatives (not chosen here): an owner-supplied randomization service with a precommitted prior and a recorded draw identity (RC-09); or an explicit authored-determination class. Blue/George decide; nothing else in the slice depends on it.
- **Sufficiency of the causes is authored, not adjudicated.** The engine enforces that a capability's provenance is lawful — committed, pinned, foreign, earlier, causal-method-only, materiality-gated — and that it is sealed before use. It does **not** compute whether the cited causes _justify_ a grade (including the §17 rarity guard for B/A/S), because no source supplies a rule from causes to grade and the engine must not invent one. Evidence is therefore partial; the rarity guard is a host/authoring discipline, not an engine check.
- **No Named-Actor existence record yet.** F-014A's actor record, personality, goals, relationships and the rest are not implemented. A Named Actor is identified here only by an opaque actor identity plus the §4 materiality literal on its basis. No source in the lock defines an actor-existence fact to reference, and none was invented.
- **PC admission is unchanged.** Releasing a PC Existence Contract under an identity that already has a Named-Actor capability is not blocked by this slice (the PC owner and the binding are untouched). The actor owner then authors nothing further for that identity, and load verification judges the boundary at the time of each Named-Actor write; carrying a prior actor record forward as cold provenance is the not-implemented existing-actor-to-PC migration.
- **Development, mastery loss, Revelation, lifecycle, Scope and Constraints are not implemented.** A committed capability is sealed (an overwrite is `AMENDMENT_NOT_SUPPORTED`, even through the owner). Consequently same-resolution feedback (WS-016 §27.1) cannot occur: nothing in a resolution can change a capability, and a consumer sees it only at its close. When development/loss is added it must use the same declared close, so that it becomes visible only at the actor owner's _next_ close.
- **A close is consumable through IM-02 only where its stage is engaged.** The existing coordinator reads AS-OF-DECLARED-CLOSE only from an engaged producer stage (or a Population PCB-01 barrier); so a consequence declares the close stage and adopts the already-committed close by identity. A consequence that reads the close without declaring it is unresolved (`PREDECESSOR_ABSENT`), never credited. The coordinator was not changed.
- **No aggregate bound supply (§28.2) and no single-contribution interface (§28.1).** No aggregate owner declares an interface and no individuation path exists, so neither is representable; promotion neither alters Population nor creates a second contribution because there is nothing to promote.
- **Close publication is not bound to a specific engaged object at admission.** The stage is lawful under an open transaction's durable authority and its value is fully determined by its committed basis (so early publication could change no result); engagement by a declaring object is verified on load (credited by a committed transaction, or awaited by an unretired object) rather than by a scan at commit.
- **Cost.** The advance path reads the engaged basis, the object and the consumer by identity. `verifyNamedActorIntegrity` (like the other verifiers) lists all facts once per open — a diagnostic path (CAL-06), not the advance path.

## Deliberately not implemented (fail closed, not guessed)

Personality, goals, loyalty, relationships, belief, inference or stance; Actor Mandates and autonomous decisions; holder-referent divergence, aliases and impersonation; F-014D AccessEvent / Accessible Content Packet; KTI development, diffusion or learning; Autonomous Collective State; institutions and factions; PC Character Capability, existing-actor-to-PC migration and PC-to-actor resumption; embodiment, form, continuity and lifecycle (clone, possession, reincarnation); development, mastery loss, Revelation and teaching; any default grade or any Named-Actor-owned randomization (the randomized branch exists only as a consumer of the shared Causal Draw — see the twenty-seventh slice); Production, Logistics or Military consumption of actor mastery; host/protocol/UI; provider adoption, deployment and migration activation; PF-6 / F-028.
