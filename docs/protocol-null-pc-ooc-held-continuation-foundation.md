# Trusted Separately Player-Authorized NULL-PC OOC Held-Continuation Temporal Advance Gateway Foundation

Bounded slice built on the existing Temporal kernel (`docs/temporal-foundation.md`), the real shipped owner stage providers and the protocol package's earlier gateways (`docs/protocol-null-pc-ooc-advance-foundation.md`, `docs/protocol-pc-transfer-advance-foundation.md`). **When the initial NULL-PC OOC target-only gateway committed a truthful `DEPENDENCY_UNRESOLVED` transaction and the kernel holds an unresolved remainder, trusted server-side code that holds the player's NEW, separate OOC choice to continue that held work can bind the held lineage and the new choice's durable transaction identity and provenance into a trusted context; an untrusted host can then invoke one allowlisted operation that names nothing, and the existing `TemporalCoordinator.advance` consumes exactly that persisted remainder over the real owner stages.** It is neither a general wait or continuation framework, a natural-language or date interpreter, a retry scheduler, a world-process bypass nor a credential/session implementation, and it replaces nothing in the kernel. The initial NULL-PC gateway is unchanged and still refuses a held continuation.

## The trust boundary and what "separately player-authorized" means here

1. **The player's NEW OOC choice is a precondition of the binder call, not a field.** The trusted service calls the binder only after the player has made a genuine choice, distinct from the one that started the held work, to continue it. It passes (a) the **held lineage the player chose**: `held.from_transaction`, the held transaction's identity, and `held.target`, the canonical target that player originally authorized for it; and (b) the NEW choice's durable identity and authored provenance — exactly the existing `ResolutionRequestSchema` fields the trusted side alone may state: `transaction_id`, `cause`, `trigger { kind, ref }`, `context { role, permission }` and `publication { basis, secrecy }` — validated by the existing strict schema (`ResolutionRequestSchema.pick`). The gateway never states or defaults the role or permission. A binding is inert if the held lineage is not exactly `{ from_transaction, target }` of existing schema values, if the transaction is not stated under exactly `role.player` + `permission.player_choice.commit` (so a `role.player_character` context, a `continues` or `requested` field and any extra field are inert), or if the new transaction identity is the held transaction itself: the earlier choice never stands for this one.
2. **No time and no continuation identity are invented.** The target is the trusted side's attestation of what the player originally authorized; it is only ever _compared_ to the held record, never used to construct a request. The request's `requested` is the sealed held transaction's own recorded request, verbatim. No default calendar, unit, duration, date parser or inferred horizon exists, and the host cannot supply, select or override any of it.
3. **Identity is the trusted side's, stable across retries and reopen.** The new transaction identity and provenance come from the context and are retained by the caller; the gateway derives nothing from a random value, clock, message counter or revision and never re-identifies a retry. A second progress attempt requires another genuine choice and a distinct identity.
4. **Not claimed.** The gateway does not verify a human, a session, an identity or that a choice interaction took place; login, credentials, sessions, accounts, tenants and any confirmation UX are not modelled, not implemented and not claimed. Calling the binder is the trusted side's attestation, nothing more. A deployment that cannot truthfully make that attestation must not call it. The acceptance is mechanism-only at this attestation seam.
5. **Trusted context is not canon.** Every invocation re-reads the campaign's operative `role.player_character` binding, the Temporal state and the sealed lineage, and then runs the kernel's own continuation admission. The context cannot turn a stale, foreign or fabricated hold into a lawful one.
6. **One refusal, one unknown.** Every failure — over-claiming request, forged or incomplete context, an active PC, no held remainder, a stale/foreign/unknown/mismatched hold, a different request type, a changed target, a replay with changed identity or intent, any kernel refusal — is `NULL_PC_HELD_ADVANCE_UNAVAILABLE` ("no advance is available") with no cause, owner, key, identity, target, stop reason or detail, and nothing is written. A failure after anything may have committed (an `UNKNOWN_OUTCOME`, a poisoned or closed store, or any change in the store head since the call began) is the distinct `NULL_PC_HELD_ADVANCE_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` (RC-16), never "safely no advancement".
7. **Minimum receipt.** Success is exactly `{ status: "NULL_PC_HELD_ADVANCE_COMMITTED" }`: no revision, hash, key, owner, replay flag, time, stop reason, remainder, event, recipient or attainment. It is identical for a first call and a retry, and identical whether the kernel reached the target or committed another held remainder. Held is not completion, and the host cannot tell.
8. **Isolation.** A context is bound to one campaign object, frozen and opaque; the gateway constructs its coordinator from that campaign's own store. Two campaigns with identical identifiers cannot cross. This is bounded campaign-isolation evidence for this operation, **not** an authentication or session isolation claim.

## The NULL-PC rule (inert whenever a PC is active)

The first thing the operation does after the strict request check is read the campaign's operative binding (`CanonicalCampaign.binding()`); if a `role.player_character` binding exists, the call is the one refusal and **nothing else is read or written**. This holds for a context bound before a PC was inserted and for the replay of an identity that committed while the campaign was NULL. While the interval is NULL, no PC Actionable-Knowledge-Time test exists (F-014B W3-CP3) and none is run; the operation grants no PC Glance, Narrative-Kernel report, observer privilege, event discovery, placement, status knowledge or later PC memory, and it has no special OOC omniscience.

## The literal Temporal request

The operation builds exactly one `ResolutionRequestSchema` value and nothing else:

```
transaction_id, cause, trigger, context, publication   ← from the trusted context (verbatim, never defaulted)
continues: <held.from_transaction>                     ← exactly the persisted continuation the Temporal state holds (and the player chose)
requested: <the held transaction's recorded request>   ← verbatim: { target: <originally authorized CanonicalTime>, condition: null }
```

Before it submits, the gateway proves the hold is exactly the work the player chose (all by identity; no scan, no boundary-index read):

- on a fresh request the Temporal state still holds a continuation whose `from_transaction` is the bound held transaction — a stale (already consumed), foreign or unknown hold is refused and left untouched;
- the held transaction, and each transaction it in turn continued back to its initial one, is committed under its own identity, stopped `DEPENDENCY_UNRESOLVED` with unfinished dependents, requested exactly the bound target and **no condition**. A condition-only or mixed request (for example the active-PC stop-at-boundary) and a different target are not this lineage; the context of every such record is the player-choice pair by the kernel's own schema.

It drives the request through the **existing** `TemporalCoordinator.advance`, so the kernel alone consumes the persisted remainder (`continuationRequestViolation` stays the final authority), selects what is due from the active boundary index, resolves each declared dependency closure through its owner path and records the one transaction, the new canonical time and the stop/continuation in the kernel's groups. The registry is the real, shipped owner stage providers — the same fifteen the initial NULL-PC gateway registers, with the existing Population barrier evaluator — and nothing else (a conformance test pins the two lists to each other). A stage whose owner has no registered provider, or whose prerequisite still does not hold, stops the new transaction truthfully with a **new** held remainder (`DEPENDENCY_UNRESOLVED`); nothing is faked, fast-forwarded, retried or looped. A third, separately confirmed choice can resume only that latest hold.

## What "committed" means (stop reason is not a side channel)

`NULL_PC_HELD_ADVANCE_COMMITTED` means the **temporal transaction** committed. It does not say that the target was attained, that any world process resolved, that a remainder is still held, or that any observer, narrator or PC learned anything. Reporting a distinct status for a re-held remainder would expose the kernel's stop reason and hidden world state; reporting it as unavailable would claim "no advancement" after the transaction committed. Any narration or information requires separate lawful I-001 / F-014D / NPK access; this slice implements none of it.

## Recovery

The same request with the same new transaction identity and the same held lineage — with the same context or a freshly bound equivalent, in the same process or after save → close → load — is the recovery path (RC-16):

- A transaction that already committed under that identity is replayed by the kernel against the same literal request: the status is the same, nothing is written, no second clock advance occurs, and committed stages are adopted by identity and never recomputed. The identity replays **only itself**, and only as a continuation of this context's held lineage: an initial record (`continues` null), a continuation of another lineage, or the same identity with a different cause, trigger, context or publication is refused, and the committed record is never amended. Mere previous authorization never progresses: re-presenting the original initial choice through the initial gateway replays it and writes nothing.
- A partial run (a crash or lost reply after the opening authority, any owner stage or the sealed record) leaves the boundary pending and the hold intact until the transaction commits, so the re-presented request adopts every committed stage and commits the rest, converging on the uninterrupted run; after the sealed record commits, a further distinct choice finds the hold consumed and is refused.
- A lost reply or an ambiguous commit is `NULL_PC_HELD_ADVANCE_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`. A failure that committed nothing is the plain refusal.

## Residuals

- Whether the player's new OOC choice actually occurred, and whether the held target the trusted side attests is the one that player authorized, is outside this slice; the binder's caller owns that precondition and must retain the held lineage, the new transaction identity and its provenance across retries and reopen. Authentication, session binding and a confirmation surface remain future, separately authorised work.
- **Choice left to engineering:** this is a separate operation and module rather than an extension of the initial gateway, so the initial branch is byte-identical and cannot be relaxed. The fifteen-provider registry is duplicated from the initial gateway rather than shared (sharing would edit that gateway); a conformance test fails if the two lists drift.
- **Choice left to engineering:** the lineage walk requires every transaction in the held chain to be target-only, for the bound target, and `DEPENDENCY_UNRESOLVED`; it reads only the committed `continues` chain of that one piece of work (bounded by the number of prior resume choices) and never scans.
- The mechanism is demonstrated end to end with the Logistics → World-State → Logistics chain (an owner stage refused through the store, then the fault removed); the GIP sunset provider is registered but not separately exercised by this slice's tests.
- A PC that becomes active between a commit and a retry makes the retry the plain refusal rather than a replayed receipt (the operation is inert while a PC is active). The committed transaction is unaffected.
- Local success is not provider or recovery acceptance (IA-07 BLOCKED). No register evidence was added; whole-row status remains separately governed.
