# Established Existing-Actor Capability → PC Capability Carry-Forward Close Foundation

Thirty-second bounded slice, built on the PC Capability Profile / PC Capability Close Receiving Foundation (`docs/pc-capability-foundation.md`), the Existing-Actor → PC Role Registry Handover Cutover Foundation (`docs/existing-actor-handover-foundation.md`), the existing-actor I-002 compilation and World-State coupling slices and the Named-Actor capability backbone (`docs/actor-capability-foundation.md`). An actor that has already undergone the lawful `EXISTING_ACTOR_HANDOVER` can now **carry one already-authoritative pre-handover Named-Actor capability, exactly, into the one PC owner's `pc_capability_profile`**, after which the **existing PC Capability Close** publishes the PC-owned capability through the existing IM-02 temporal path, read cross-owner only **AS-OF-DECLARED-CLOSE**.

Governing lock: `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` (F-027 v1.0). No source pin, Runtime Contract snapshot, open gate or acceptance status changed.

## Behavior, end to end

1. The actor's capability is made **authoritative before the cutover** by the existing Named-Actor path (basis, then the actor owner's capability close). It need not appear in the compilation's `source_state`: the actor owner stays writable until the binding commit, so a capability may close after compilation and before handover.
2. The compiled, coupled actor is handed over (`handoverExistingActor`, unchanged). From that commit the Named-Actor owner writes nothing for the identity; its committed facts are cold provenance.
3. `carryForwardActorCapability({ pc_id, capability_id })` commits **one** sealed basis at `pc/<pc>/capability-basis/<capability>` under the `pc_capability_profile` facet, with `method: EXISTING_ACTOR_CARRY_FORWARD`. Nothing else is written; no draw, entropy, clock advance or unrelated fact. The basis is not the capability and is not readable cross-owner.
4. A dependent consequence declares the existing PC Capability Close ahead of its own stage; the coordinator resolves the unchanged `pcCapabilityCloseProvider`, which transcribes the marker of the basis. The consequence reads the capability **AS-OF-DECLARED-CLOSE**, naming `PC Capability Close`.
5. Everything survives save → close → load with the identical ownership result (one writer, never two, never none) and, from any durable cut, convergence on the uninterrupted run; a lost reply or a crash converges by identity (RC-16); a history built outside admission that places the source after the handover, alters a marker, cites a mere basis or a stale/unpinned reference fails the open at the PC verifier.

Fails closed (each by admission for a raw commit and again on load): no handover or an ordinary `ACTIVATION` binding; another identity or capability; a `named_actor_capability_basis` or an absent capability; a stale, fabricated or unread version; an altered, regraded or dropped marker; a smuggled marker/claim/contract/draw/prior in the establishment; a source that did not exist before the cutover (the actor owner can no longer write it, and a history that says otherwise fails the open); a second carry or an overwrite (sealed).

## Construction decisions that are technical encodings, not new semantics

- **Second method, not a second kind.** The Integration Map assigns PC mastery to the one `pc_capability_profile` facet; the received basis is how the PC owner already receives an already-established fact before its close. The carry-forward reuses it with a distinct, closed `method`, so fresh generation history and inherited mastery can never share a provenance path.
- **References, not copies, and the marker is compared, never carried by the caller.** The basis keeps the marker the existing record always had; the rule makes it exactly the source's. There is no number, grade table or default in the carry-forward statements (a conformance test asserts it).
- **Precedence is an invariant of the cutover, re-proved on load.** A fact map cannot decide commit order. Admission relies on the one cutover invariant — once the binding names the identity the Named-Actor owner refuses every write for it, so a source that exists was committed before the binding — and load verification proves it from the committed revisions (source < binding < basis).
- **The mechanical basis lives in `derive.ts`.** The adapter names no foreign key builder (a predecessor conformance guard), so the one statement of the builder is the pure derivation, as for every other owner operation.
- **The binding is not re-read for coupling.** Admission checks the binding's transition, identity and the contract version it consumed; the binding's own lawfulness is canonical integrity's statement (`existingActorHandoverViolation`), not restated here.

## Not implemented (fail closed; none resolves an open gate)

Unresolved or merely latent inherited capability resolution (`UNRESOLVED` stays unrepresented); the branch-invariant world-prior method and Causal Draw for an inherited PC capability; eager resolution of capability sets at binding; PC capability development or loss and Intrinsic Amendment Events; PC → non-PC handover/resumption; replacement, deactivation, succession, fork and general Role Registry lifecycle; DEFERRED / ACTIVE-LIMITED binding-mode work; the Existing-Actor Confirmation Surface itself; relocation or multi-locus expansion; consumer wiring to the PC close beyond the existing read form; provider, database or deployment work.

## Residuals

- The capability rule's governing citation is unchanged and does not cite the Handover Contract or I-002 v2.1; the behavior is governed by `pcCarryForwardViolation`. Re-citing it would change the provenance of every existing PC capability fact, so it is left to Blue to decide whether a later lock/citation addendum is wanted (as for the binding in the handover slice).
- The carried marker is judged exactly; whether the actor capability itself was lawfully established is the Named-Actor owner's judgment, already enforced at its own admission.
- The carry-forward is one capability per publication; a handed-over actor with several authoritative capabilities carries each by its own publication. Nothing enumerates or scans.
- Local success is not provider or recovery acceptance (IA-07 BLOCKED).
