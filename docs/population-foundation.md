# Population & Cohort Reconciliation Backbone Foundation

Fourth bounded slice, built on the Campaign Continuity Foundation (`docs/campaign-package.md`), the Canonical World Identity & Owner Foundation (`docs/canonical-world-foundation.md`) and the Turnless Temporal & Resolution Transaction Foundation (`docs/temporal-foundation.md`). The canonical campaign can now **establish an exact bounded Population basis attached to its canonical locus, instantiate a sparse Cohort and an externally qualified derived view, resolve one engaged Population reconciliation made due by PCB-01, and — after save, close and load — continue without duplicate consumption, recreation or provenance drift.**

Governing lock: `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` (F-027 v1.0). Sources: WS-001 Draft 3 §§1, 2, 29B, 32, 35 (lock pin `2026-09-25T23:42:28.770Z`), WS-015 Draft v1 §§III, IV, VI–XVI (lock pin `2026-09-20T15:53:58.732Z`) and F-017 v0.3 PCB-01 (conformance pin `2026-09-25T23:42:27.802Z`). **CAL-01 (APPROXIMATE-PARTITION) remains UNCALIBRATED and is not used: every proof is exact. CAL-02 (`L_unavailable`, default labor coefficients) remains OPEN: no labor chain, coefficient, rate, capacity, cadence or productivity default exists.** Every other F-027 open gate is unchanged.

This is a **foundation proof**, not a population simulator. All fixture data is synthetic (`packages/testkit/src/population-fixtures.ts`); places, units, envelopes and the T0/TP/TC coordinates are test data, not IMMORTALIS canon.

## One canon: how it reuses IM-02 / IM-03 / IM-11 / IM-12 / IM-17

Nothing new is persisted outside the existing campaign package. Population facts are ordinary `OwnerFact`s written through `store.commit`, so the existing validation, expected-version checks, RC-16 receipts, idempotent replay, unknown-outcome reconciliation, hash-chained journal and package/load integrity are the ones in force. **The package format is unchanged (still format version 1); there is no second journal, save, sidecar or recovery identity.** `PopulationAdapter` takes only a `CampaignStore`; reconciliation runs only as an owner operation the single `TemporalCoordinator` resolves (IM-02), its stages and the kernel transaction commit through the same store (IM-11), `committedGroup` cold-history lookups (IM-12) back both adoption and Population integrity, and the owner registry / `canonicalAdmission` (IM-03) is the one place Population admission is reached.

## How the Population operation is re-derived at admission

A reconciliation group pins, as `reads`, the foreign facts it used: the causal owners' consequence registers and the Criterion Owner's fact. Population admission rebuilds the whole outcome from those pinned facts alone and refuses any difference: every eligible (addressed to the ledger, effective at or before the resolution point), previously unconsumed envelope is consumed **exactly once** and none is omitted; the units are exactly the opening units plus typed Entries minus typed Exits; the close's opening/closing counts and versions are the ledger's; the ledger is stamped with the close and the constituent versions pinned; every Cohort result is exactly what Population composes from the new units and a criterion **established at that same resolution point**. An envelope's writer must be the owner of the register that carries it. A consumer, a criterion owner, a causal owner or a synthetic owner cannot write count or membership (key-space exclusivity), and Population itself cannot write a count its typed inputs do not produce.

## PCB-01 (F-017; WS-001 §32) — how it is implemented

A consumer whose read is a discrete, AS-OF-DECLARED-CLOSE read of Population declares a `pending_consequence_barrier`. **Only when that consumer is itself engaged** (its own earned boundary is due) does IM-02 consult the IM-04 evaluator, with the frozen conditions evaluated from committed facts by identity — no scan, no polling, no wall-clock:

1. the envelope is already committed, **eligible** (addressed to this ledger, effective at or before the consumer's resolution point) and **unconsumed**;
2. it causally precedes the consumer (committed now, in its writer's own register, not effective after the instant);
3. it **materially intersects** the consumer's engaged Population dependency: the ledger scope, or a Cohort whose composed result the envelope can change — an Exit of a current member; an Entry that lands where the Cohort is indexed **and is not shown, by the authoritative Criterion Owner fact read at the consumer's resolution point, to fail the criterion**; anything while the Cohort has never been composed. An unquantified Cohort has no discrete result to change. If the external criterion is not established at that point it cannot say, so the answer stays "could change" (and a reconciliation that then cannot lawfully compose fails closed);
4. the latest close omits it, and incorporation could change the discrete outcome — exactly (3) (every consequence in this slice is a membership change and so touches every declared discrete facet; no probability, rate or threshold exists).

**Retained, verifiable evidence (repair).** A `DUE_RECONCILED` record carries `evidence`: the engaged scope, the single resolution point, and the triggering envelopes with a structural basis (`LEDGER_SCOPE`, `COHORT_MEMBER_EXIT`, `COHORT_QUALIFYING_ENTRY`, `COHORT_NOT_YET_COMPOSED`). It is **re-derived, never trusted**: the verifier recomputes the triggers from the _committed Population stage group's own writes_ — its close (the consumed Entries/Exits and, for each recomposed Cohort, the membership it had _before_, which Population's admission checks against the prior Cohort fact) and the Cohort's composed result after. A claim that differs, is empty, or names another scope or point is refused at raw kernel admission (state-based) and fails load-time verification (journal-based via `committedGroup`, never against anything that changed later). No second log, sidecar, owner or scheduler: the evidence lives in the existing sealed transaction fact and the existing journal. Residual: an Entry and an Exit of the _same_ unit consumed in one close net to no Cohort change, so no trigger is derivable; such a reconciliation is not recorded as PCB-01-due (the transaction fails closed rather than record an unsupported claim).

If due, IM-02 imports the consumer-declared dormant Population reconciliation object into **that transaction's transient graph**; the existing machinery orders Population's stage before the consumer (CLOSE is still a dependency), the consumer reads that exact close under its named Close Basis, and the transaction records the object as `DUE_RECONCILED` (`trigger: PCB-01`, `due_for` the consumer stage) — moving only its resolved basis, to the consumer's boundary instant, never touching the boundary index. If not due, the consumer reads the **latest committed** close (the evaluator vouches it is a stamped close of the named basis for the ledger and, for a Cohort, the latest close's); a missing or stale close is `PREDECESSOR_ABSENT`, never "current". If the reconciliation cannot lawfully complete (e.g. a criterion not established at the composition point: `BASIS_MISMATCH`), the consumer is `PREDECESSOR_BLOCKED`, the Population object is `DUE_UNRESOLVED` and untouched, independent work proceeds, and the held request is consumed by the next transaction. A re-presented transaction re-engages a due reconciliation whose stage already committed (identity, not a fresh evaluation) and **adopts** it, so a crash at any durable step converges on one reconciliation and one consumption.

## Judgment calls and open residuals (none resolves an open gate)

- **Eligible / precedes / intersects / could change.** WS-001 §32 names the conditions without a formal predicate. The implemented predicate is structural (above) and conservative: it can over-trigger (e.g. an Entry at a Cohort's indexed place, or any consumption for a ledger-scoped consumer) but never needs a probability or threshold. Whether a richer test is wanted is a design question; none was invented.
- **Envelope carriage.** The sources fix no carrier vocabulary. A causal owner holds the envelopes addressed to one ledger in a register in its **own** key space (RC-08 "authoritative writer"); a consumer's barrier and the Population stage name it. The envelope's "consumed-at-close reference" is Population's (the close and the ledger's consumed set), because Population may not write a foreign owner's fact.
- **Exactly-once.** By envelope instance identity: the ledger's consumed set grows with each consumption (bounded to the consumed envelopes; no retention policy is implied).
- **External criterion carrier.** The Criterion Owner publishes `{ resolution_point, qualifying_unit_ids }` in its own key space; "established for the composition point" is that stamp equalling the reconciliation's resolution point. The criterion's own semantics, authoring and cadence are its owner's and are not implemented.
- **The next eligible reconciliation.** Recurrence is not implemented (F-014B recurrence is out of scope), so the "next eligible engaged-scope reconciliation" is a **dormant temporal object the consumer's barrier names** (no earned boundary, never in the active index). An object advances only inside a committed transaction, so one that has already resolved is spent and is never selected again; a later pending envelope with no further dormant object leaves the consumer `PREDECESSOR_ABSENT`.
- **Population → consumer read mode.** Reading a Population fact AS-OF-NOW, or at any basis other than the ordinary reconciliation close, is refused at object registration and re-verified on load (WS-015 §VIII "only one NOW direction"). Criterion Owner → Population is AS-OF-NOW at the composition point.
- **Kernel additions (additive).** `StageInput.prior` (an owner operation's own prior facts, so it can advance its state without a self-edge); `StageUnresolvedError` and the reasons `BASIS_MISMATCH` / `SOURCE_INVALID`; the optional `pending_consequence_barrier` on a dependency declaration; the engaged variants `DUE_RECONCILED` / `DUE_UNRESOLVED`; `ConsequenceBarrierEvaluator` (the IM-04 seam the coordinator takes as an optional constructor argument — without it a barrier consumer is `PROVIDER_ABSENT`, never silently resolved). Existing facts keep their canonical serialization.
- **Cohort retirement and live dependency registrations are not implemented.** The retirement predicate is declared in the Cohort reference (WS-015 §XV) but no retire/rebind/registration mechanism exists; "a consumer may attach by reference" is a consumer fact in the consumer's own key space, and the Cohort carries no consumer state.
- **Bounded Cohorts.** `BOUNDED` and `UNQUANTIFIED` exist as cardinality semantics (and in arithmetic); a `BOUNDED` Cohort cannot be instantiated because no evidence source exists to derive one, and an `UNQUANTIFIED` Cohort carries no count or members and is barred from additive/conserved arithmetic.
- **Time representation / publication labels** are inherited from the predecessor slices: coordinates are opaque, and `basis`/`secrecy` of Population-written facts are inherited from the authored facts they advance, never defaulted.
- **Cost.** The advance path reads the engaged consumer, its ledger/cohort/close, the criterion fact, the register(s) and the one due object, by identity. `verifyPopulationIntegrity` (like the other verifiers) lists all facts once per open — a diagnostic path (CAL-06), not the advance path.
