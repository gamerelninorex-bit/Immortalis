# Consequential Active-PC Action (CPAA-C1 v0.2) — Bounded Direct Collection From a Finite Source — PROVISIONAL

**STATUS: PROVISIONAL CANDIDATE LOWERING. Not ratified, not adopted design authority, not a spec-lock pin.** This slice implements the BLUE candidate "CPAA-C1 v0.2 — RED MODIFY R-1–R-7 Bounded Repair" (native pin `2026-10-16T00:31:39.142Z`, `https://app.notion.com/p/3fb20af901e081c294cbf534d24de7c0`) under the Sovereign's non-final execution direction (native pin `2026-10-16T01:38:29.022Z`, `https://app.notion.com/p/3fb20af901e0815894edc6581ed9f4f2`). The v0.2 candidate lacks RED re-check, consensus and ratification; the existence of this code gives its rulings (C1, P-1, P-2) no authority. Every new kind's governing rule carries `effective_basis: "provisional-candidate-lowering-pending-ratification"` and the `CPAA_C1_PROVISIONAL` lineage, so a later ratification, correction or withdrawal is a visible, bounded change. PF-6/F-028 remains BLOCKED / NOT ACTIVATED. No frozen source lock, Runtime Contract snapshot, open gate or acceptance status changed. Nothing is merged, released or activated by this slice.

It is **one closed class** and **not an action framework**: one active, stationary Player Character directly collecting a finite amount of one loose same-form material from its own current locus into an already-existing local Production Reserve, with no transformation, travel, external labor, specialist work or unresolved rights prerequisite. The names `pc_existence` / `pc_voluntary_commitment` are non-normative technical lowering names and give no precedent for any other non-movement action: every other action needs its own ruling. There is no action bus, no universal action law, no generic duration or capability table.

## The chain (the minimum proof)

player confirms one exact attempt → PC-side commitment-of-record + PC-owned Production request → `role.gm` physical-reach judgment → Population individual occupancy → Production's derived work record + extraction (cycles, causal duration and the earned boundary DERIVED) → the PC's own, separately confirmed time passage through the existing Temporal → Production Source Delta → World-State delivery → Production Reserve inflow chain → the source drops once, the Reserve rises once → a separately confirmed second attempt meets the real remaining source.

`tests/integration/pc-collection-foundation.test.ts` is the witness: collect 3 from a source of 5 → source 2, Reserve 3; a separately confirmed second attempt for 3 is refused by Production's admission against the real remaining 2 (the registration is released by lawful denial; nothing moves).

## What the caller can and cannot say

A trusted server-side context (`bindTrustedConfirmedPcCollectionContext`) carries the exact confirmed choice (PC, per-act identity, source, Reserve, amount, `role.player` authored authority), the PC's established mechanism, Production's Capability / Access basis and the publication material. A value a host fabricated is not a context. Four host operations (`commit_confirmed_pc_collection`, `get_pc_collection_reach_task`, `submit_pc_collection_reach`, `start_confirmed_pc_collection`) accept a strict body of the operation name (plus, for the one judgment, the untrusted reading). There is no field for a campaign, PC, act, source, amount, destination, mechanism, confirmation, role, permission, boundary, duration, identity, rule, provenance, verdict or result. Every refusal is the same refusal; the only things returned are three minimum statuses and, for the one read, the bounded task. Time passes only through the existing separately confirmed active-PC target advance, whose fresh advance now asks the engine's single rule (`pcCollectionCrossingClear`) whether the PC's own in-flight collection is positively recognised and the crossing positively free of any other due boundary.

## Derivations, never declarations

- **Cycles** = the amount (serial, one unit per cycle). **Causal duration** = cycles × τ. **Earned completion** = start + max(causal duration, applicable §11 minimum). No caller supplies a requested horizon, a rate, a duration or a boundary; the extraction's declared start and boundary must equal the derived ones or admission refuses.
- **Reach verdict** = REACHABLE iff the path is open, or closed and the mechanism is able to pass. A closed path requires a decided, mechanism-anchored reading. Every ground must be a verbatim quotation of wording the task delivered (`MIN_GROUND_LENGTH`).
- **Occupancy interval** = exactly the interval the commitment and the mechanism determine.
- **Source/Reserve outcomes** are never written by the collection path: the existing chain computes them.
- Admission (IM-11) re-derives every fact from committed state; load verification (`verifyPcCollectionIntegrity`, code `PC_COLLECTION_STATE`) applies the SAME rule to the facts as they stood before each write, so a history built outside admission cannot say what the premises did not.

## Mutual exclusion and interruption (C5, C8)

- A PC with a voluntary-movement record cannot register or start a collection; a PC with an OPEN occupancy cannot commit to a movement (one narrow seam in `pcMovementCommitmentViolation`). One occupancy at a time.
- Time passage over the PC's own OPEN collection is refused unless the collection's object is engaged and is the only boundary due by the target. The F-014B §10 actionable-knowledge evaluator is **not implemented**, so the absence of an interruption cannot be established when other work is due: the crossing is refused (a declared `creates_epistemic_interruption: false` is not proof). A target short of the earned boundary resolves nothing of the collection.
- At the boundary the stage re-validates the PC's standing, custody, locus, movement-freedom, mechanism, judgment, path facts and open occupancy (`pcCollectionPremiseViolation`). A changed premise leaves the act uncredited and unresolved: never silently completed, partially credited or cancelled (all-at-boundary, no partial credit).

## Unresolved implementation blockers (specific)

1. **C1/P-1/P-2 are unratified.** Kind names, the owner assignments (`pc_existence` facets, Population registration) and the provisional rule citations may change on ratification, correction or withdrawal. Nothing here is a precedent.
2. **The P-1 carrier is a typed projection that nothing can verify against the accepted claim's wording.** The mechanism cites accepted claims by locator, but τ itself is supplied by the establishing call; no rule can check that the authored claim actually states, or supports, that cycle duration. Until P-1 specifies who may establish the mechanism and how τ is bound to authored content, the real campaign has no lawful source of τ other than a trusted-side attestation.
3. **No real PC content establishes a cycle.** No Existence Contract in the codebase carries an exact rate or cycle; the tests use a synthetic claim and a fixture τ.
4. **F-014B §11 floor** is carried only as an authored status (`NOT_APPLICABLE` with a ground, `APPLIES`, or `UNRESOLVED` which withholds). No rule decides which applies for a given act.
5. **F-014B §10 interruption** has no evaluator: any other due boundary in the crossing refuses the PC's own time passage. The PC cannot be interrupted, cancel, abort or divert an in-flight collection; a stage-time premise failure leaves the act held with the PC's occupancy open, and no release path exists.
6. **Population P-2** registers the individual interval only. Population's aggregate availability, cohorts and barrier evaluator are untouched and unconsulted; no labor is debited.
7. **Source ownership and rights** are not modelled: the class is closed to sources with no unresolved rights prerequisite, which is stated by the fixture's access facts and not verified by any owner.
8. **No presentation or perception of the outcome.** The downstream consequences are the owners' facts (source remainder, Reserve credit, refusal of a later attempt); no Event Signal, observation, memory or narration was added.
9. **A PC that has ever held a movement record is outside the class.** The bilateral exclusion is read from the PC's movement commitment/request slots (one journey per PC), so after a settled movement the PC is also outside the stationary-custody premise; no rule yet says how a collection re-enters after a journey.
10. **Scripted host only** (see above). No real GM host acceptance run exists.
