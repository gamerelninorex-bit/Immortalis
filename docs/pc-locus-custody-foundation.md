# Active-PC Post-Handover Physical Locus Custody Foundation

Thirty-ninth bounded slice, built on the Canonical World Identity & Owner Foundation (`docs/canonical-world-foundation.md`), the World-State fallback custody and entity-continuity foundations (`docs/entity-continuity-foundation.md`), the Existing-Actor World-State coupling anchor (`docs/existing-actor-coupling-foundation.md`) and the Existing-Actor → PC Role Registry handover (`docs/existing-actor-handover-foundation.md`). The handover left one thing expressly open: "who owns the PC's current locus thereafter, and relocation, are later slices". This slice closes only the first half of that sentence. The engine can now hold the **one authoritative stationary current physical locus of the active Player Character** immediately after the completed handover, as a World-State custody record taken only from the handover's consumed coupling.

This is a **stationary custody record only**. It implements no movement, no request, no Logistics subject admission, no dispatch, no in-transfer custody, no destination commit, no ECT-01 transition and no relocation.

Governing lock: `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` (F-027 v1.0). No source pin, Runtime Contract snapshot, open gate or acceptance status changed. PF-6/F-028 remains BLOCKED / NOT ACTIVATED.

## Authority

The construction authority is the Thirty-Ninth implementation brief (Blue Hard Preflight, frozen pin `2026-10-08T01:30:19.780Z`) under the Sovereign-ratified and frozen **Active-PC Post-Handover Physical Locus Ownership & Relocation Contract** (frozen pin `2026-10-08T01:25:36.611Z`, `https://app.notion.com/p/3f320af901e08166ad96ca0fc5e5069d`). That contract is **not a spec-lock pin**: it is cited in the governing rule's lineage, never added to the lock. The spec-locked pins it composes with are unchanged: WG `2026-09-25T13:44:22.668Z`, WS-006 / F-014A `2026-09-25T00:18:36.828Z`, IR `2026-09-25T00:17:26.287Z`, I-002 `2026-09-25T00:15:54.536Z` (the Handover Contract's observed pin `2026-09-25T00:16:16.544Z` is likewise not a lock pin). The clauses this slice rests on:

- **§4 / §7** the current stationary physical locus is owned by World-State, or a lawful more-specific physical custodian: exactly one writable current physical-locus truth. I-002 excludes position (§4.3), the Role Registry holds control binding only, Logistics owns the locus only during an active transfer. No more-specific owner exists, so World-State's fallback custody (WG "Ownership Guards", F-016 Ratified Fallback Objective Physical-Entity Custody) is the owner class. The current fallback entity representation admits Production-accepted entities only, so a bounded post-handover PC representation was required.
- **§5** the operative binding's `coupling.world` / `coupling.locus` is a **binding-time referent only**, never read as the PC's current locus; no Role Registry serialization change is authorized or required.
- **§6** the consumed `world_actor_coupling` stays **immutable handover provenance**, never rewritten as the PC moves and never a second current-location truth.
- **§9** exactly one authority answers "where is it now?": the stationary custodian (this record), or Logistics while an active transfer owns the in-transfer locus; never both.
- **§13** minimum durable addition: one bounded World-State/proper-physical-owner record keyed to the already-canonical PC identity and referencing the binding/handover provenance.

## Behavior, end to end

1. The existing machinery commits the actor's Named-Actor state, its World-State coupling and its I-002 compilation, then the one `EXISTING_ACTOR_HANDOVER` binding. Unchanged.
2. `establishPcPhysicalLocusCustody` commits **one** fact at `world-state/pc/<pc_id>/locus-custody`, at the consumed coupling's locus. Nothing it read is touched: the binding keeps its serialization, version and receipt; the coupling stays version 1.
3. A PC with no binding, a PC activated by ordinary first `ACTIVATION` (no consumed coupling, and the binding's referent is never read as a placement), a different identity, a missing coupling, stale or forged pins, a caller-supplied locus or any extra field fail closed with nothing written.
4. A second establishment is a version conflict; an amendment, relocation or overwrite is refused (sealed); only World-State's fallback custodian may write the key space.
5. A PC already represented by another current-locus authority (a fallback entity, an ECT-01 transition, a Logistics commitment or transfer under its identity) is refused, so there is never a rival authority.
6. Everything survives save → close → load with convergence on the uninterrupted run from every durable cut; a lost reply converges by identity (RC-16); a tampered or out-of-admission history fails the open.

The Role Registry binding, the Existence Contract, the coupling, Logistics, Temporal and ECT-01 are unchanged in meaning. Logistics still refuses a PC as a subject (it admits Production-accepted fallback entities only): that admission is a later slice.

## Construction decisions that are technical encodings, not new semantics

- **Slot per PC identity, sealed, create-only.** The frozen contract requires exactly one authority; relocation needs a successor mechanism that does not exist, so none is invented.
- **Locus from the coupling, with the binding hidden for the handover check.** `existingActorHandoverViolation` is the single statement of what the handover must be; it is judged against the facts as they stood before the cutover (the binding hidden), exactly as canonical integrity already does, so the two cannot drift. The record never repeats a locus read from the binding.
- **Field names** (`stationary_locus`, `basis.pinned_versions`, `established_from`) and the key shape are technical encodings; the sources prescribe no serialization vocabulary.
- **`pinned_versions` accepts `0`** so a missing prerequisite surfaces as `PREREQUISITE_MISSING` at admission; no committed record can carry `0`.
- **The rival-authority guard reads only keys** (`worldFallbackEntityKey`, `worldEntityTransitionKey`, `logisticsCommitmentKey`, `logisticsTransferKey`); it constructs nothing of Logistics or ECT-01.

## Not implemented (fail closed; none resolves an open gate)

Voluntary PC movement; a player-owned movement commitment or Movement Service Request; any player-action framework or natural-language parsing; Logistics subject admission or an adapter into Logistics; dispatch; in-transfer custody; destination commit; an ECT-01 movement transition; relocation mutation and any successor mechanism; Role Registry change; a mutable actor-coupling successor; a generic entity or location ledger; PC inventory or possession; route finding, travel duration, cancellation, diversion, return, repeat journeys, passenger/carrier generalization; provider, deployment and migration work; PF-6/F-028 activation; any IA/V row or gate promotion. Local success is not provider or recovery acceptance (IA-07 BLOCKED).

## Residuals

- The record is verified for identity, ownership and provenance only; whether the locus is a _sensible_ place for the PC is not judged (no source supplies one).
- After this slice the active-PC current-locus answer exists but nothing yet consumes it (no Logistics admission, no relocation). A fresh Blue Hard Preflight decides the next slice.
- A PC activated by ordinary first `ACTIVATION` has no custody path here: its binding-time referent is not a consumed coupling, and the frozen contract authorizes only the post-handover case.
  _Update (BLUE v5.1):_ that path now exists as a **separate, exclusive second basis** of the same fact kind, with its own source coverage and a referee-qualified physical reading; see [`pc-locus-original-activation-foundation.md`](pc-locus-original-activation-foundation.md). This slice's handover branch is unchanged, is not reclassified, and the post-handover movement and advance consumers still accept only it.
- `verifyPcPhysicalLocusCustodyIntegrity`, like the other verifiers, lists all facts once per open (diagnostic path, CAL-06), and returns immediately when no record exists.
