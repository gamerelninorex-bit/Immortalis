# I-002 PC Capability Profile / PC Capability Close Receiving Foundation

Twenty-eighth bounded slice, built on the Canonical World Identity & Owner Foundation (`docs/canonical-world-foundation.md`: the I-002 Existence Contract and the one PC owner `pc_existence`), the Turnless Temporal & Resolution Transaction Foundation (`docs/temporal-foundation.md`) and the Named-Actor capability backbone (`docs/actor-capability-foundation.md`), whose close, transaction, persistence, recovery and verification machinery it reuses without change. The **one lawful PC owner** (`pc_existence`, I-002) can now **receive and persist one already-established piece of authoritative durable personal mastery of a Player Character**, **publish it at the literal PC Capability Close**, and expose it to later cross-owner consumers **only AS-OF-DECLARED-CLOSE**. It is initial establishment only.

Governing lock: `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` (F-027 v1.0). Sources, at their locked pins: WS-016 Character Capability Protocol v1 (Sovereign-ratified 2026-09-25) at the frozen integration-map pin `2026-09-24T17:38:59.210Z` — the Blue Bounded Integration Map §2 "I-002 — BOUNDED AMENDMENT" (a compact **PC Capability Profile companion state under I-002 ownership**, the ratified I-002 v2.1 integration) with the Working Operational Draft §§2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 11, 13, 15, 23, 25, 27, 27.1, 28, 32; I-002 Player Character Generation at the frozen pin `2026-09-25T00:15:54.536Z` (§2 Ownership Contract, §4 Minimum Existence Contract, §21, §26); F-016 Phase 2B §H **PCC-01** (the authoritative PC mastery owner is I-002 / the PC-side character-local capability state), **PCC-02** (the Close Basis named **"PC Capability Close"**, a temporal/provenance boundary only) and **PCC-03** (cross-owner reads AS-OF-DECLARED-CLOSE, Close Basis PC Capability Close); F-017 §3 (CLOSE) as implemented by IM-02. **CAL-01 stays UNCALIBRATED and is unused: every proof is exact. No numeric skill value, XP, level, percentage, modifier, roll, prior, seed, default grade or periodic advancement exists anywhere.** No source pin, Runtime Contract snapshot or open gate changed. ## One canon, one owner, no second machinery

Both kinds are ordinary `OwnerFact`s written through `store.commit`, so the existing validation, expected-version checks, RC-16 receipts, idempotent replay, unknown-outcome reconciliation, hash-chained journal and package/load integrity are the ones in force. **The package format is unchanged (still format version 1); there is no second journal, scheduler, roster, sidecar save path, polling loop or per-PC refresh.** The temporal coordinator was not changed (a conformance test asserts it never names the capability). The only edits to predecessor engine code are additive wiring: the registry gains the two kinds, `identityMismatch` handles them, `canonicalAdmission` dispatches `pc_existence` groups whose facet is `pc_capability_profile` (Existence Contract groups are untouched), object registration also asks the PC rule, and `CanonicalCampaign.open`/`verify` also run the PC verifier.

## The lifecycle: received basis → PC Capability Close → consumption

1. **Receive (already-established, before any consequence).** `receiveCapabilityBasis` persists the basis. Admission requires the PC's **own** committed Existence Contract — a released contract by construction — read at its current version and depended on, every cited claim to be an **accepted** claim of it (a hypothesis or unresolved item has no locator, and the engine never infers a grade from fame or reputation: there must be an accepted claim to point at), and the identity/capability not to be already held by the Named-Actor owner nor to be a Population ledger. The basis is not the capability and is not readable cross-owner.
2. **Material trigger = the consequence's object.** A dependent consequence registers its temporal object with the PC Capability Close stage declared ahead of its own stage. Registration is refused unless the basis is **already committed** — a consequence cannot create the capability it needs. With no consequence engaged, no capability is ever resolved: no tick, sweep or scan.
3. **Close (IM-02 stage).** The coordinator resolves the declared stage first: it reads exactly the basis (NOW) and commits the capability under its StageKey-derived group/intent identity and the transaction's durable authority. The StageKey is `(pc-capability/<pc>/<capability>, pc_existence / pc-publish-capability-close, pc/<pc>/capability/<capability>)`: no horizon, source version, retry, consumer or slice is an identity dimension.
4. **Consumption.** The consequence reads the capability **AS-OF-DECLARED-CLOSE**, naming the `PC Capability Close`, so it cannot resolve before the close and observes the committed close itself. A later consequence declares the same stage in its own object and **adopts** the committed close by identity — never duplicated or recomputed. Two consequences due at the same instant share one close.

**No same-resolution feedback.** A committed capability is sealed (an overwrite is `AMENDMENT_NOT_SUPPORTED`), and no consumer stage may produce a PC capability key, so nothing in a resolution can change a capability. When development/loss is later added it must use this same close, so that it becomes visible only at the PC's _next_ close (WS-016 §27.1).

## Construction decisions that are technical encodings, not new semantics

- **Facet, not owner.** The Integration Map assigns the PC's durable personal mastery to I-002; I-002's registered owner is `pc_existence`. The companion state is registered as a second facet `pc_capability_profile` of that owner. Facet name, key spaces, `pc-publish-capability-close` and the kind ids are technical identifiers.
- **Close name.** The Close Basis name is the F-016 PCC-02 text verbatim, `PC Capability Close` (not a slug, and not the F-014A actor-profile close, which the source forbids borrowing by analogy). The literal is the only value the schema accepts.
- **Basis + close in two kinds.** The Named-Actor machinery needs a committed source for the pure stage to read and for registration to refuse a conjured capability; the received basis plays that role for the PC (an already-established fact received and persisted _before_ its close, WS-016 §27.1). It adds no new rule beyond the Named-Actor pattern.
- **Accepted-claim locators.** The slice cannot import the generation compiler (not implemented), and the Existence Contract has no capability field (its schema is unchanged). "Already lawfully established" is therefore represented as a pointer to the committed released contract and to the accepted claim(s) establishing it: `{ section, index }` into the claim-bearing parts of the contract. This restricts nothing the source permits and invents no grade rule; it makes WS-016 §13's "unaccepted, hypothetical or unmirrored content cannot ground" structurally true (hypotheses and unresolved items have no locator).
- **PC gate.** WS-016 §4's first PC alternative ("explicitly authored or strongly established by accepted generation history") is satisfied structurally by the only representable establishment, so no separate materiality field exists. The C+/EX, deferred and development alternatives are unrepresentable.
- **One owner per capability.** WS-016 §32 fails "duplicate ownership of PC or Named-Actor state". Admission, the close and load verification refuse a PC basis/capability when the Named-Actor owner already holds the same identity's same capability (by identity, three keys, no scan), and the Named-Actor owner already refuses any identity with an Existence Contract.

## Judgment calls and residuals (fail closed; none resolves an open gate)

- **Sufficiency of the cited claims is authored, not adjudicated.** The engine enforces provenance and acceptance; it does not compute whether a claim _justifies_ a grade, because no source supplies such a rule. The rarity discipline is a generation/authoring discipline, as for the Named-Actor owner.
- **Not implemented (fail closed, schema-unrepresentable or admission-refused):** carried-forward actor capability facts (the existing-actor→PC handover is the thirty-first slice and the established-capability carry-forward the thirty-second, `docs/pc-capability-carry-forward-foundation.md`); PC→non-PC transfer and resumption; deferred capability derivation from verbatim generation provenance and the verbatim cold provenance object; Revelation; development and loss through the Intrinsic Amendment Event lifecycle; delegated capability branches; Causal Draw or any randomized PC capability; Scope and Constraints; lifecycle (clone, possession, reincarnation); KTI learning; cognition/personality; Epistemic broadcast and lowering; Insertion/I-001 projection of the capability; IM-01; PF-6/F-028; provider, deployment and migration.
- **Consumer wiring.** `pcCapabilityCloseRead` is the one lawful cross-owner read form (F-016 PCC-03). The thirty-fourth slice wires Epistemic (F-014D) to it for the canonically bound active PC recipient only (`docs/epistemic-access-foundation.md`); KTI is not wired and still refuses a PC recipient.
- **Identity/capability overlap by capability only.** The one-owner check is per identity and capability (no scan). An identity that held _other_ Named-Actor capabilities before becoming a PC is the existing-actor-to-PC handover, which remains unimplemented; PC admission itself is unchanged.
- **A close is consumable through IM-02 only where its stage is engaged**, so a consequence declares the close stage and adopts the committed close by identity; one that reads the close without declaring it is unresolved (`PREDECESSOR_ABSENT`). The coordinator was not changed. Publication is not bound to a specific engaged object at admission; engagement is verified on load.
- **Cost.** The advance path reads the engaged basis, the object and the consumer by identity. `verifyPcCapabilityIntegrity` (like the other verifiers) lists all facts once per open — a diagnostic path (CAL-06), not the advance path.
