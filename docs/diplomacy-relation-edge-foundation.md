# Diplomacy Relation Edge Authoritative Core Backbone Foundation

Nineteenth bounded slice, built on the same commit, owner-registry, admission, persistence and recovery machinery as the Diplomacy External Recognition foundation (`docs/diplomacy-external-recognition-foundation.md`), the Externalized Claim / Dispute foundation (`docs/diplomacy-externalized-claim-dispute-foundation.md`) and the earlier owner foundations. It adds the Diplomacy owner's third persistent fact: one sparse Relation Edge for a materially continuing external relation. **Initial authored establishment only.**

Governing source (exact pins live in `spec/f027/spec-lock.json` and the owner registry): **WS-008 Diplomacy / Inter-Faction Relations v1.1** (frozen F-027 pin `2026-09-24T07:01:45.312Z`) §§2.1–2.2, §4, §5.1 (the Relation Edge minimum surface), §24 and §§29.3/29.7, with **F-016 Phase 2C** (pin `2026-09-21T23:21:44.154Z`) and **F-017** (pin `2026-09-25T23:42:27.802Z`).

A Relation Edge is a sparse continuity object: it durably names one continuing external relation, its authored party endpoints and scope, and its relation-level lifecycle and rupture / suspension facts. It is **not** a duplicate ledger of Recognition, Claim / Dispute, Channel, Instrument or Framework truth and **not** a universal relationship, trust or reputation score. It is created only by an explicit authored establishment for a relation the caller's lawful context already deems materially continuing; there is no relevance score, flag, all-pairs generator or background relationship scan.

**The derived-index firewall.** WS-008 §5.1 lists five reference arrays on the edge (`channel_refs[]`, `recognition_refs[]`, `active_claim_dispute_refs[]`, `instrument_refs[]`, `framework_refs[]`). WS-008 §29.7 (R-7) is controlling for storage: those arrays are derived, non-authoritative indexes rebuilt from source records — the stale-index failure R-7 repaired. They are therefore **not** writable persistent fields. The strict contract rejects any attempt to carry one (empty or populated, through the adapter or a raw store commit, and again on load) instead of storing it. Existing Recognition and Claim / Dispute records contain no `relation_id` and WS-008 provides no authoritative party / scope similarity algorithm, so no Recognition or Claim / Dispute is linked to an edge by matching party sets, scopes, names, referents or any other convenience heuristic.

**Not implemented (out of scope, unchanged):** any derived Relation Edge index or association algorithm; automatic linkage of existing Recognition or Claim / Dispute records to an edge; post-establishment rupture, suspension, restoration, retirement or other lifecycle mutation; Diplomatic Channel / Representation; Negotiation Record or procedure; Diplomatic Instrument, Clause, Binding Entry or Framework; callable-right / guarantee, compliance or performance workflow; Recognition withdrawal / succession / re-resolution; Claim / Dispute mutation; GIP procedure, domestic incorporation, Reach, office transition or new authority semantics; Actor / ACS decision generation; Epistemic delivery, receipt, comprehension or awareness generation; downstream Economy, Production, Logistics, Military, KTI, Culture or World mutation; any timer, tick, scheduler, watcher or polling loop; and any RNG, seed, probability, chooser, prior or workaround for GitHub Issue #11 (untouched).

## Identity, keys, lifecycle and idempotence

- **Identity is `relation_id` alone.** The key is `diplomacy/relation/<relation_id>`. The parties, the scope and the statuses are **not** identity dimensions. The edge is stored once: no copy per party, recognition, dispute, channel or instrument, and no all-pairs or all-relations scan.
- **One slot per relation.** A retry, a duplicate construction or a raw-store write reaches the same occupied slot, so no sibling record exists. Identical content under a different `relation_id` is a distinct record; nothing is merged or deduplicated. The recognition, dispute and relation key spaces are disjoint.
- **ADVANCING, initial establishment only.** WS-008 permits formal rupture, suspension, restoration and eventual retirement, so the kind is `ADVANCING`; this slice implements the first creation only. Diplomacy admission refuses every other change (`AMENDMENT_NOT_SUPPORTED`), and a second creation of an occupied key is refused by the store (`VERSION_CONFLICT`). Retirement, restoration and all later edge transitions are outside this slice despite the classification.
- **Retry converges, conflict fails closed.** Re-presenting the same intent is reconciled by identity (RC-16) to the original receipt. A different intent at an occupied `relation_id` — equal or different content — and the same intent with changed content are refused; the committed edge is never rewritten.
- **No dependency is invented.** The edge is Diplomacy-owned authored state, not a persisted conclusion derived from another owner's mutable state, so its group reads and depends on nothing and the record carries no `dependency_refs[]`. A future derived-index implementation that consumes mutable foreign records must obey §29.3 dependency registration and invalidation; this slice pre-builds none of that machinery.
- **No scan, clock or RNG.** Establishing an edge reads nothing and writes only its own key; reading one is a direct keyed read; only the diagnostic load-time verifier lists facts, once per open.

## Interpretations recorded for review

1. **The derived arrays are rejected, not stored.** Brief §4 and WS-008 §29.7 classify the five §5.1 reference arrays as derived indexes. The contract is `strictObject`, so an authored record carrying any of them (even empty) fails closed with `INPUT_INVALID` through the adapter and `VALUE_INVALID` through a raw commit. No empty-array placeholder is accepted or defaulted.
2. **`party_refs[]` carries no source-undefined rule.** WS-008 defines no minimum length, uniqueness, sorting or deduplication. The array is carried exactly as authored — empty, repeated and unsorted arrays are accepted and no element is reordered or removed. Each element is the repository's opaque `Id`; no party class is fixed and nothing is dereferenced to admit the record.
3. **`relation_scope`, `rupture_or_suspension_status` and `lifecycle_status` are singular authored values with no source-undefined subtype.** WS-008 fixes `relation_scope` as required and singular, `rupture_or_suspension_status` as optional and singular and `lifecycle_status` as required and singular, with no closed literal set. All three use the repository's weakest canonical singular representation already used for the dispute `status` and recognition scope: any canonical JSON scalar (string, finite number, boolean, null) or canonical JSON object, carried exactly as authored. An array is multiplicity and is refused; no enum, text-only type, score, taxonomy or alias normalisation is imposed, and no lifecycle or rupture value is defaulted.
4. **`provenance_ref` is optional and opaque.** It is an optional singular `Id`, distinct from the engine's own rule / spec provenance metadata, and is never dereferenced.
5. **`relation_id` uses the repository's key-safe identifier rule** (`EntityId`, the same rule as the recognition and dispute identities) because it is embedded in the canonical key.
6. **Create-time statuses are authored, not transitions.** An edge may be established already carrying a `rupture_or_suspension_status` or any `lifecycle_status` value as authored input; the contract attaches no meaning and no cross-field rule to either, and no transition between values exists.
7. **The existing owner, a new facet and a new governing-rule citation; the others are untouched.** The Diplomacy owner is the existing IM-06 `diplomacy` owner. The citation records the WS-008 artifact and exact pin with clauses §§2.1–2.2, §4, §5.1, §24 and §§29.3/29.7 and the F-016 Phase 2C and F-017 lineage; no Recognition or Claim / Dispute citation is reused.

These are recorded interpretations of the brief, not new rules; none adds a source-undefined executable restriction, and each is a candidate for the later Diplomacy work it belongs to.
